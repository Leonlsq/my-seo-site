## Tesla's Turo FSD Free Trial: How Eligible Hosts Can Claim It (and What It Actually Costs)

Tesla is now using Turo to push Full Self-Driving (Supervised) adoption, and the hook is simple: **eligible Turo hosts get a one-month free trial of FSD software**. If you list a Tesla on Turo, you're probably wondering whether you qualify, how to activate the trial, and whether handing FSD to renters is a smart move or a liability trap. This guide breaks down the eligibility rules, the activation steps, the real cost after the trial ends, and the risks no one mentions in the press release.

The short version: the trial is a genuine freebie **if** your car is FSD-capable and your Turo listing meets the program requirements. If you're on a legacy HW2.5 car or a subscription-only setup, you may hit a wall. Here's how to check before you get excited.

## Step-by-Step: How to Qualify and Activate the Free FSD Trial

### Step 1: Confirm your Tesla is FSD-capable (hardware check)
FSD (Supervised) requires **Hardware 3 (HW3) or Hardware 4 (HW4)**. Check this in the car:
1. Tap **Controls → Software**.
2. Look for **"Full Self-Driving Computer"** under the additional vehicle information.
3. If it says **HW2.5**, you're not eligible without a paid retrofit (roughly $1,000–$1,500 depending on region and service center quote).

### Step 2: Verify your Turo host account is in good standing
The program targets **active, eligible hosts**. In practice that means:
- At least one **active, bookable Tesla listing** on your Turo profile.
- No active account suspensions or unresolved claims.
- Listing location in a **supported market** (the rollout is US-first).

### Step 3: Watch for the invite in your Turo host dashboard
Tesla and Turo are distributing this through host communications, not a public signup page. Check:
- Your **Turo host email** (including spam/promotions).
- The **Turo host dashboard notifications**.
- Any **Tesla app message** tied to the vehicle's VIN.

### Step 4: Activate FSD in the Tesla app
Once granted, the trial attaches to the **vehicle**, not your Turo account:
1. Open the **Tesla app** → select the vehicle.
2. Go to **Upgrades → Software**.
3. Confirm **Full Self-Driving (Supervised)** shows as active for the trial period.
4. In-car, verify under **Controls → Autopilot → Full Self-Driving (Supervised)** that the toggle is enabled.

### Step 5: Set renter expectations in your listing
Add a line to your Turo listing description explaining that FSD is a **supervised assistance feature**, not robotaxi autonomy, and that renters must keep hands on the wheel and eyes on the road. This protects you in disputes.

## The Real Cost After the Free Month (and the Renter Risk)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Trial length | 1 month, free |
| FSD subscription after trial | ~$99/month (US) |
| FSD outright purchase | ~$8,000 (varies by region) |
| Hardware requirement | HW3 or HW4 |
| Who pays after trial | You (the host), unless you pass cost to renters |
| Renter liability | Renter is the driver; FSD is Level 2, not autonomous |

**The catch:** FSD (Supervised) is a **Level 2 driver-assistance system**. It does not make the car self-driving, and the renter remains legally responsible at all times. If a renter misuses it or gets a strike, that's your vehicle's software state and potentially your insurance headache.

### Is it worth keeping after the trial?
- **Yes, if** you rent to tech-curious tourists who specifically seek out Teslas and you can justify a higher daily rate.
- **No, if** you're running a high-volume budget listing where renters won't read instructions and $99/month eats your margin.

## Common Issues and What You Should Know

- **Trial didn't appear?** Confirm your VIN is HW3/HW4 and that your listing was active *before* the program window. Trials are VIN-locked and non-transferable.
- **Renter disabled FSD?** Some renters turn off Autopilot settings. Add a "please leave settings as found" note and check the vehicle state on return.
- **Subscription vs. purchase confusion:** The trial is the **subscription** tier. You are not getting an $8,000 software license for free.
- **Insurance gap:** Standard Turo protection may not explicitly cover ADAS-related incidents. Ask Turo support in writing before promoting FSD as a feature.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do all Turo hosts get the free Tesla FSD trial?
No. It's limited to **eligible hosts** with FSD-capable (HW3/HW4) Teslas in supported markets. It's invite/distribution-based, not a public signup.

### How much is Tesla FSD after the free Turo trial ends?
The FSD (Supervised) subscription runs about **$99/month** in the US. You can cancel anytime in the Tesla app under Upgrades.

### Can I transfer the FSD trial to another Tesla I own?
No. The trial is tied to the **specific VIN** that qualified. It does not transfer between vehicles or to a buyer if you sell.

### Is FSD safe to let Turo renters use?
FSD (Supervised) is a **Level 2 assistance system** requiring active driver supervision. It's safe when used correctly, but you must set clear expectations in your listing to reduce misuse and disputes.

## Final Verdict

Take the free month — there's no downside to testing FSD on your rental fleet. But **treat it as a marketing experiment, not a permanent cost**. Track whether FSD-enabled listings actually command a higher daily rate during the trial. If they don't earn back the ~$99/month, cancel before the billing cycle starts. And whatever you do, document the "supervised, hands-on" requirement in your listing so a renter's misunderstanding doesn't become your liability.
