## Step-by-Step: How to Claim the 75% Discount Before the Deadline

If you've decided to buy, the process is time-sensitive and there are a few traps that can cost you the discount. Follow this exact order:

1. **Verify the deadline in your local timezone.** TechCrunch deadlines are typically published in Pacific Time (PT). If you're in IST, CET, or AEST, the cutoff may land on the *next calendar day* for you — or earlier than you expect.
2. **Create your TechCrunch account first.** Registering an account *before* checkout prevents session timeouts that wipe promotional pricing from your cart.
3. **Apply the promo code at checkout, not before.** Discount codes tied to "last day" campaigns often only validate on the payment screen. Screenshot the price before and after applying.
4. **Choose the correct pass tier** (see comparison below) — upgrading later usually voids the promotional rate.
5. **Complete payment in one session.** Abandoned carts during flash sales frequently lose the locked-in price.
6. **Save your confirmation email and invoice PDF.** You'll need these for expense reimbursement or tax deduction if you're self-employed.

## Common Issues, Hidden Costs, and What You Should Know

The "75% off" framing is real, but the *base price* matters. Here's what the marketing page won't spell out:

- **Base price inflation:** Discounts are calculated off the "on-door" or "full" rate, which is always the highest tier. Your effective savings versus the *early-bird* price may be closer to 30–40%.
- **Travel and lodging dwarf the ticket.** In a typical host city, 3 nights of hotel plus flights can run **$1,200–$2,500** — often 3–5x the discounted pass itself. Budget for this before clicking buy.
- **Refund policies are strict.** Most Disrupt passes are non-refundable but transferable. If your plans are shaky, confirm the transfer window.
- **Team discounts stack differently.** Buying 3+ passes as a group sometimes beats the individual promo — ask sales directly.
- **Virtual/streaming options** occasionally exist at a fraction of the cost if you only want keynote content.

### Alternatives If You Miss the Deadline

- **Waitlist and resale:** Verified transfer marketplaces sometimes list passes at face value post-deadline.
- **Satellite and side events:** Most of the real networking happens at unofficial happy hours and demo nights — many are free.
- **Next-year early bird:** Locking in the *earliest* tier usually beats any "last day" flash sale.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 75% discount legit?
Yes — it's a standard last-day promotional tier. The catch is that the discount applies to the highest base rate, so your real savings versus early-bird pricing is smaller than 75%. Always compare the final checkout total, not the advertised percentage.

### What is the last day to buy a TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 pass?
The promotional window closes at the end of the announced deadline day (typically 11:59 PM PT). Because of timezone differences, buyers in Europe, India, and Australia should treat the cutoff as up to a day earlier in their local time.

### Can I get a refund if I can't attend TechCrunch Disrupt 2026?
Most passes are non-refundable. However, they are usually transferable to another attendee before the event. Check your confirmation email for the exact transfer deadline and process.

### Which TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 pass is best for a startup founder?
The Founder/Investor pass. It includes curated matchmaking and investor access that General Admission lacks — the single feature most likely to justify the higher price for a fundraising startup.
