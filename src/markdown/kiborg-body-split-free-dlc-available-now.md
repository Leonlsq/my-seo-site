## Fixes When the Free DLC Won't Download or Appears as "Unavailable"

If the store shows Body Split as unavailable, greyed out, or it downloads but doesn't appear in-game, work through these in order:

- **Fix 1 — Region mismatch:** Free DLC rollouts are sometimes staggered by store region. If your account region differs from your IP, the listing can 404. Switch store region in account settings or use the web store for your account's home region.
- **Fix 2 — Cache/entitlement desync:** On Steam, run **Steam → Settings → Downloads → Clear Download Cache** (you'll re-login). On consoles, restore licenses (PS: Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licenses; Xbox: Settings → Account → Refresh).
- **Fix 3 — Base game not updated:** Body Split requires the latest base-game patch. An outdated client will hide the DLC flag. Force an update check, then relaunch.
- **Fix 4 — Verify integrity:** Steam: right-click KIBORG → Properties → Installed Files → Verify. This re-flags missing DLC manifests, which is the single most common cause of "purchased but not showing."

If all four fail, the DLC is likely region-locked for your store at the moment — check the game's official channels for the rollout schedule before filing a ticket.
