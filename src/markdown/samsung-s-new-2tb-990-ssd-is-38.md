## How to Install the Samsung 990 2TB SSD (PC and PS5)

### On a PC or laptop
1. **Power down and unplug** the machine, then ground yourself to avoid static damage.
2. **Locate your M.2 slot** — use the primary CPU-connected slot for the boot drive, or a secondary slot for extra storage.
3. **Remove the heatsink/screw**, insert the drive at a slight angle, then press down and secure it.
4. **Reinstall the heatsink** (use one if your board doesn't include one — Gen4 drives run warm under sustained load).
5. **Boot into BIOS** and confirm the drive is detected.
6. **Initialize and format** it in Windows Disk Management (or Disk Utility on macOS) before use.
7. **Optional:** install Samsung Magician to check firmware, health, and enable over-provisioning.

### On a PS5
1. **Update the PS5 firmware** and power it off completely.
2. **Remove the side panel** and the expansion slot cover.
3. **Install the SSD** in the M.2 slot and secure it with the screw.
4. **Add a heatsink** — Sony recommends one, and the 990 benefits from it under load.
5. **Boot up**, format the drive when prompted, and set install location in Settings > Storage.
