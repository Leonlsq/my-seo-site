## Legacy Games Is Shutting Down: Here's How to Keep What You Paid For

If you bought casual games from Legacy Games (the storefront behind thousands of hidden-object, time-management, and match-3 titles), the shutdown news hits differently than most store closures: **your purchases will not be re-downloadable once the servers go dark.** That means the copy sitting on your hard drive right now may be the only copy you'll ever have. This guide walks you through exactly how to back up your library, locate your license keys, and migrate to storefronts that won't leave you stranded. Do this *before* the download servers are switched off — after that, there is no recovery path.

## Step-by-Step: Back Up Your Legacy Games Library

Legacy Games titles typically install as standalone executables (no Steam-style DRM client), which is actually good news — a copied folder usually runs fine offline. Here's the process:

1. **Open the Legacy Games desktop app and log in.** Go to your Library and sort by "Purchased." Screenshot the full list — you'll want it as a checklist.
2. **Download every title you own, one at a time.** Don't assume you can grab them later. If a game shows "Install," install it now.
3. **Locate the install directory.** Default paths are usually:
   - `C:\Program Files (x86)\Legacy Games\`
   - `C:\Users\[YourName]\AppData\Local\Legacy Games\`
   - Or wherever you pointed the installer during setup.
4. **Copy the entire game folder** (not just the `.exe`) to an external drive or NAS. Casual games are small — most are 100 MB–1.5 GB, so a 128 GB USB stick holds dozens.
5. **Test one copied game offline.** Disconnect Wi-Fi, launch the `.exe` from the external drive, and confirm it runs. Some titles phone home on first launch only; if it works once offline, it'll keep working.
6. **Export your purchase history.** In the app, check for an "Order History" or "Account" section and save the page as PDF (Ctrl+P → Save as PDF). This is your proof-of-purchase if you ever need to dispute a charge or request a key from a publisher.

### Where to Find Your Legacy Games License Keys

Some titles activate through a third-party key rather than the Legacy launcher. Check these locations:

| Location | What You'll Find |
|---|---|
| App → Account → Order History | Order numbers, sometimes keys |
| Email inbox (search "Legacy Games") | Original purchase receipts with keys |
| `%AppData%\Legacy Games\` config files | Sometimes cached activation tokens |
| Publisher's own site (e.g., Big Fish, GameHouse) | Key redemption portals |

If you find a key, **redeem it immediately** on the publisher's platform. Unredeemed keys tied to a dead store can become worthless.

## Common Issues and Smarter Alternatives

**"The game won't launch after copying."** This usually means a registry entry or a hardcoded file path. Try running the `.exe` as Administrator, or reinstall to the *same* folder path on the new drive. Tools like `RegScanner` can help you spot missing registry keys, though most casual titles don't need them.

**"I can't download because the server is already down."** Check the [Internet Archive's software collection](https://archive.org) and the publisher's official site — many casual games are re-released elsewhere. Contact the original developer directly; small studios often honor purchase proof with a free key.

**Better long-term storefronts to migrate to:**

- **GOG.com** — DRM-free offline installers, the gold standard for ownership.
- **Steam** — huge library, but requires the client and periodic online checks.
- **itch.io** — DRM-free downloads, great for indie casual games.
- **Big Fish Games** — direct successor for hidden-object/time-management fans.

The lesson here is simple: **buy DRM-free when you can.** A storefront that hands you an offline installer can't take your games away.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Will Legacy Games refund my purchases after the shutdown?
Refunds are typically only issued for recent purchases (often within 14–30 days) and depend on the store's final policy. Older purchases are usually not refundable — which is exactly why backing up your files now matters more than chasing a refund.

### Can I still download my Legacy Games after the store closes?
No. Once the download servers are decommissioned, purchases won't be re-downloadable. Any game not already saved to your own drive or external backup will be permanently inaccessible.

### Are Legacy Games titles DRM-free?
Most are, which is why copied folders often run offline. A minority use one-time online activation — test each game offline to confirm before you rely on it.

## Final Verdict

Treat this shutdown as a deadline, not a news story. **Tonight, download every game you own, copy the folders to an external drive, and test them offline.** Then move your future casual-game spending to GOG or itch.io, where DRM-free installers mean no storefront can ever revoke your library. Ten minutes of copying now saves you from losing hundreds of dollars in games forever.
