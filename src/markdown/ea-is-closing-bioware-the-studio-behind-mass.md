## EA Closed BioWare: How to Preserve Your Mass Effect & Dragon Age Saves Before It's Too Late

EA has confirmed it is closing BioWare and its Edmonton studio after more than 30 years, following the decision not to move forward with early development of the next Mass Effect. For players, the immediate question isn't the news cycle — it's **what happens to my saves, my DLC, and my ability to reinstall these games in five years?** This guide gives you a concrete, step-by-step backup and preservation plan you can execute this weekend, plus what to expect from storefront delisting, online features, and the Legendary Edition.

## Step-by-Step: Back Up Every BioWare Save You Own

Do this in order. It takes about 30–60 minutes total and protects you against account issues, delisting, and launcher changes.

### 1. Locate Your Save Folders (PC)

BioWare games store saves in predictable paths. Copy the entire folder to an external drive **and** a cloud folder:

| Game | Default Save Path (Windows) |
|---|---|
| Mass Effect Legendary Edition | `Documents\BioWare\Mass Effect Legendary Edition\Save` |
| Mass Effect 1/2/3 (original) | `Documents\BioWare\Mass Effect\Save` (and `Mass Effect 2`, `Mass Effect 3`) |
| Dragon Age: Origins | `Documents\BioWare\Dragon Age\Characters` |
| Dragon Age II | `Documents\BioWare\Dragon Age 2\Characters` |
| Dragon Age: Inquisition | `Documents\BioWare\Dragon Age Inquisition\Save` |
| Mass Effect: Andromeda | `Documents\BioWare\Mass Effect Andromeda\Save` |

**Steps:**
1. Press `Win + R`, type `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\BioWare`, and hit Enter.
2. Select every subfolder and copy them to `D:\BioWare-Backup\` (or any external drive).
3. Zip the folder with a password-free archive and upload it to two separate cloud services (e.g., Google Drive + OneDrive). Redundancy matters — one cloud copy is not a backup.
4. Note the exact game version in a `readme.txt` inside the backup so future you knows which patch the saves came from.

### 2. Console Players: Extract Cloud Saves Now

- **PlayStation:** Settings → Saved Data and Game/App Settings → Saved Data (PS4/PS5) → Cloud Storage → Download to Console, then copy to a USB drive via Settings → System → Back Up and Restore.
- **Xbox:** Settings → System → Storage devices → select your drive → **Copy** (not Move) each BioWare save. Xbox Cloud saves will likely persist, but a local copy costs nothing.

### 3. Verify the Backup Actually Works

Reinstalling a game to test is the only real verification. At minimum, confirm file sizes are non-zero and that `.sav`/`.das` files open in a hex editor without errors. A corrupt backup you discover in 2030 is worthless.

## What Happens to Storefronts, DLC, and Online Features?

BioWare's closure does **not** automatically remove games from Steam, the EA app, or consoles — EA owns the publishing rights, and Mass Effect/Dragon Age remain profitable catalog titles. But expect these realistic changes:

- **Delisting risk is low, DLC risk is moderate.** Deluxe edition bundles and minor DLC packs are the first things to vanish from stores. Buy any DLC you still want **now**, not later.
- **Online services are the fragile part.** Mass Effect 3 multiplayer, Andromeda's multiplayer, and Dragon Age: Inquisition's multiplayer rely on EA servers. Server shutdowns typically follow studio closures within 12–24 months. Single-player campaigns are unaffected.
- **Galaxy at War readiness** (ME3) can be maxed offline via the single-player Galaxy at War map, so multiplayer loss won't block your ending.
- **Dragon Age Keep** is the biggest unknown. The Keep is a web service that imports world states into Inquisition. If it goes offline, you lose cross-game choices unless you've already imported them. **Log in and export/lock in your world states now.**

### Priority Checklist

1. Buy missing DLC for ME2, ME3, and Dragon Age: Inquisition.
2. Log into Dragon Age Keep and finalize your world state.
3. Back up saves (above).
4. Screenshot your EA account entitlements page as proof of purchase.
5. Install and fully patch every game you own — patched installs survive better than unpatched discs.

## Common Issues and Alternatives

**"My saves won't load after reinstalling."** Usually a version mismatch — Legendary Edition saves don't transfer to original ME3 and vice versa. Keep the matching install.

**"I only own physical discs."** Console discs are your best long-term asset, but day-one patches matter. Download all patches while servers are live and store them on an external drive if your platform allows.

**Alternatives if servers die:** PC players can use community mods (e.g., ME3 multiplayer restoration projects and save editors like the Trilogy Save Editor) to recover functionality. These are unofficial — back up before use.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Will Mass Effect and Dragon Age games be removed from Steam?
Unlikely in the near term. EA still publishes and profits from the catalog. The realistic risk is DLC delisting, not base-game removal.

### Can I still play Mass Effect 3 multiplayer after BioWare closes?
Only while EA keeps the servers online. Expect a shutdown window of 12–24 months. The single-player campaign and all endings remain fully playable offline.

### Does closing BioWare affect Mass Effect Legendary Edition?
No. Legendary Edition is a complete, self-contained single-player package. Back up your saves and it will remain playable indefinitely.

### What happens to Dragon Age Keep?
EA hasn't confirmed its fate. Treat it as at-risk: log in now, finalize your world state, and screenshot your choices in case the service is retired.

Don't wait for an official sunset announcement — by then, DLC may already be gone and the Keep offline. **Spend one hour this week:** buy missing DLC, lock in your Dragon Age Keep world state, and make two verified copies of every save folder.
