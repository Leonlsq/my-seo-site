## Dark Souls 2 & 3 Servers Offline: How to Keep Playing Right Now

FromSoftware has announced that the online servers for *Dark Souls 2* and *Dark Souls 3* are temporarily going dark. If you just booted up the game and got hit with a "Failed to connect to the Dark Souls 3 server" or "Unable to log in to Dark Souls 2 server" error, you are not alone. The immediate takeaway: **your game is not broken, and your save file is safe.** The servers are down for scheduled maintenance or a security patch, and you can still play the entire single-player campaign offline.

This guide covers exactly how to bypass the login screen, fix the most common connection errors, and—most importantly—how to optimize your offline experience with builds and strategies that don't rely on summoning or invasions.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Play Dark Souls 2 & 3 Offline

When the servers are down, the game will try to connect to Bandai Namco's matchmaking service and fail. Here is how to get past the title screen and into the game without waiting for a server response.

### 1. Force "Offline Mode" on PC (Steam)
If you are stuck on the "Logging in..." screen, you can force the game into offline mode:
1. Open Steam and go to your **Library**.
2. Right-click on **Dark Souls III** (or *Dark Souls II: Scholar of the First Sin*).
3. Select **Properties** > **General**.
4. Under "Launch Options," type: `-offline`
5. Close the window and launch the game. It will skip the server check and load directly into the main menu.

### 2. Disconnect Your Console from the Internet
For PS4, PS5, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X|S users, the fastest workaround is to physically disconnect the network:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Network**.
2. Toggle **Connect to the Internet** to **Off** (PlayStation) or disconnect from the Wi-Fi network (Xbox).
3. Launch the game. It will detect no connection and immediately default to offline mode.

### 3. The "Cancel" Trick (All Platforms)
If you don't want to mess with settings, simply press **Cancel** (B on Xbox, Circle on PlayStation) the moment the "Logging in" prompt appears. The game will time out and drop you into the main menu with a "Start Offline" option. This works on both *DS2* and *DS3*.

### Best Offline Builds for DS3 (No Summons Required)
Since you can't rely on phantoms, you need a build that can solo bosses. Here are two proven setups:

- **The "Sellsword Twinblades" DPS Build:** Start as Mercenary. Infuse Sharp. Pump Dexterity to 40. Use the **Pontiff's Right Eye** ring. This melts bosses like the Dancer and Abyss Watchers before they can punish you.
- **The "Great Club" Poise Build:** Start as Warrior. Get the Great Club in the Farron Keep swamp. Infuse Heavy. Stack Vitality and Endurance. You can stagger almost every boss in the base game, making solo fights trivial.

### Best Offline Builds for DS2 (Scholar of the First Sin)
- **The "Hexer" Build:** Rush to Felkin the Outcast in Huntsman's Copse. Buy Dark Orb and Dark Weapon. Pump Intelligence and Faith equally. This build destroys most bosses from a safe distance.
- **The "Grand Lance" Counter Build:** Found in Forest of Fallen Giants. Use a shield in your off-hand and poke. The counter damage is absurd against enemies that attack you first.

## Common Issues and What You Should Know

**"My game says 'Unable to connect to server' and freezes."**
This is a known bug when the server is unreachable. On PC, verify your game files via Steam (Right-click > Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity). On console, clear your system cache by holding the power button until it fully shuts down, then restart.

**"Will I get banned for playing offline?"**
No. FromSoftware has explicitly stated that playing offline is safe. The recent server shutdowns are often tied to security exploits (like the RCE bug found in 2022). Playing offline actually protects you from malicious invaders.

**"Can I still get achievements/trophies?"**
Yes. All single-player achievements are obtainable offline. However, achievements tied to covenants (like "Master of Pyromancies" in DS3, which requires 30 Sunlight Medals) will require significant PvE farming. For Sunlight Medals, farm the **Lothric Knights** outside the Dancer boss room—they drop them at a decent rate with the Covetous Gold Serpent Ring equipped.

**"What about Elden Ring?"**
As of this writing, *Elden Ring* servers are unaffected. If you need a multiplayer fix, *Elden Ring* is your best bet. However, *Dark Souls 2* and *3* are still fully playable solo.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How long will the Dark Souls 2 and 3 servers be down?
FromSoftware has not given an exact timeline. Historically, these maintenance windows last anywhere from **24 hours to two weeks**. The 2022 server shutdown for *DS3* lasted over seven months due to a critical security vulnerability. Monitor the official *Dark Souls* Twitter/X account for the "servers are back online" announcement.

### Can I still play Dark Souls 3 online with friends using a private server?
Yes, but only on PC. The community-run **"Blue Sentinel"** mod and private server projects (like the "Dark Souls 3 Archthrones" server) allow online play. However, these require modding your game and carry a risk of soft-bans if you connect to official servers later. For most players, waiting is safer.

### Does going offline reset my progress or covenant rank?
No. Your save file is stored locally. Covenant ranks and progress are saved. When the servers return, you will log back in with all your items and ranks intact. The only thing that resets is your current session's connection status.
