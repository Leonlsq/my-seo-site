## Before You Buy: 5 Things to Verify on the Listing

Prebuilts live or die on the parts *not* in the headline. Check these on the Newegg page:

1. **PSU wattage and rating** — You want **850W+ 80+ Gold** minimum for an i9 + RTX 5070 Ti. A no-name 750W unit is a red flag.
2. **RAM speed and channel config** — Confirm **2×16GB (dual-channel) at 6000 MT/s or higher**, not a single 32GB stick.
3. **SSD model** — A Gen4 drive (e.g., WD Black SN850X-class) is fine; a DRAM-less budget drive is a downgrade.
4. **Motherboard chipset** — Z790 enables full CPU tuning; B760 may limit overclocking headroom.
5. **Warranty terms** — ABS typically offers a **1-year parts/labor** warranty. Confirm return window (usually 30 days via Newegg).

> **Pro tip:** Screenshot the listing at purchase. Prebuilt configs silently swap parts between batches.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the RTX 5070 Ti good for 4K gaming?
Yes, with caveats. It handles 4K at high settings in most titles when paired with **DLSS 4 and frame generation**. For native 4K ultra without upscaling, expect to tune settings or consider a 5080-class card.

### Is the i9-14900KF overkill for gaming?
For pure gaming, largely yes — a Ryzen 7 9800X3D often games faster. But the 14900KF's 24 cores make this a genuinely strong **hybrid gaming/workstation** buy at this price.

### Is $2,549.99 a good price for this prebuilt?
It's **competitive but not a steal**. The $450 discount brings it in line with DIY costs. Verify the PSU, RAM speed, and SSD model — those determine whether it's a great deal or an average one.

### Does the ABS Stratos II Aqua come with Windows?
