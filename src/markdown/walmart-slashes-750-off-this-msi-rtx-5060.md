## How to Set Up the MSI Crosshair for Maximum Performance

Out of the box, most gaming laptops run conservative power profiles and bloated software. Follow these steps to unlock the hardware you paid for.

1. **Update GPU drivers first.** Download the latest NVIDIA Game Ready driver directly from NVIDIA's site — skip the OEM driver on MSI's support page, which is often months old.
2. **Enable MSI Center's "Extreme Performance" mode.** This raises the CPU/GPU power limits and fan curves. Expect louder fans but measurably higher sustained clocks.
3. **Set the display to 240Hz.** Windows often defaults to 60Hz. Go to Settings → System → Display → Advanced display → choose 240Hz.
4. **Verify dual-channel RAM** in Task Manager → Performance → Memory. If it says "1 of 2 slots used," consider adding a matching stick.
5. **Disable startup bloat.** Uninstall or disable MSI's trial software, Norton/McAfee trials, and any preloaded game launchers you don't use.
6. **Cap frame rates in esports titles.** For CS2, Valorant, and Fortnite, cap at 240fps to match the panel and reduce heat/noise.
7. **Undervolt the CPU (optional, advanced).** Tools like ThrottleStop can drop temps 5–10°C, improving sustained performance in long sessions.

- **Battery life is short:** Expect 4–6 hours of light use. Gaming unplugged is not realistic — always play on AC power for full GPU wattage.
- **Return window matters:** Walmart's electronics return policy is typically 30 days (verify for your store/online order). Stress-test the laptop in the first week with a benchmark like 3DMark or a long gaming session to catch defects or coil whine.
