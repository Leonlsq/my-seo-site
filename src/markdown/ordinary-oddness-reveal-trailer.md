## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

- **"The trailer looks too good — is it fake?"** Reveal trailers are often "target renders." Wait for a **gameplay deep-dive** or demo before pre-ordering. Never pre-order off a cinematic alone.
- **Wishlist ≠ pre-order.** Wishlisting is free and reversible. Pre-ordering locks your money. For an unproven indie title, wishlist only.
- **Regional pricing traps.** If you're outside the US/EU, check SteamDB's regional price history the moment it's listed — some publishers misprice at launch and correct within 48 hours.
- **Alternatives to watch while you wait:** If the trailer's vibe appeals to you, cross-shop *similar atmospheric indie titles* currently on sale — this scratches the itch without paying full launch price.
- **Demo strategy:** If a demo drops, play it *before* the refund window closes. Steam's 2-hour/14-day refund policy applies to the full game, not demos, so test performance in the demo first.

### Quick Performance Prep Checklist

- Update GPU drivers (NVIDIA GeForce Experience / AMD Adrenalin) — launch-day driver patches fix stutter.
- Enable **Resizable BAR** in BIOS if supported (free 5–10% FPS in many titles).
- Ensure the game installs to an **SSD**, not a mechanical drive — Unreal 5 titles stutter badly on HDDs.
- Set Windows power plan to **High Performance** before first launch.
