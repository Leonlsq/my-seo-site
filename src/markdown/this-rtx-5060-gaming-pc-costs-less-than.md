## What Performance Should You Expect From an RTX 5060 + Ryzen 5?

The RTX 5060 is a 1080p-first card that punches above its weight with DLSS 4 and Frame Generation. Realistic expectations at 1080p high settings:

- **Esports titles** (Valorant, CS2, Fortnite Performance mode): 200+ FPS easily.
- **AAA single-player** (Cyberpunk 2077, Hogwarts Legacy): 60–90 FPS with DLSS Quality + Frame Gen.
- **1440p gaming**: Playable at medium settings with DLSS; expect 50–70 FPS in modern titles.
- **Ray tracing**: Enable only with DLSS on; native RT at 1080p will drop you to 30–45 FPS in heavy scenes.

### First 5 Things to Do After Unboxing

1. **Update GPU drivers** via NVIDIA App — prebuilts often ship with stale drivers.
2. **Enable XMP/EXPO** in BIOS so your DDR5 runs at rated speed (not JEDEC 4800).
3. **Check the PSU wattage** on the label — you'll want 650W+ headroom if you plan a GPU upgrade.
4. **Uninstall bloatware** (trial antivirus, iBUYPOWER utilities you don't use).
5. **Verify the SSD** with CrystalDiskInfo and confirm it's a DRAM-cached NVMe, not a QLC budget drive.

## Common Issues, Hidden Costs, and Alternatives

**The 8GB VRAM question.** The RTX 5060's 8GB buffer is fine at 1080p today but will age faster at 1440p with high textures. If you plan to keep this PC 4+ years, consider the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB variant or a similarly priced AMD card with more VRAM.

**Windows vs SteamOS.** The Steam Machine's SteamOS is excellent for couch gaming and has near-zero overhead, but it can't run Game Pass, many anti-cheat multiplayer titles, or non-Steam launchers natively. The Slate's Windows 11 runs everything — at the cost of OS overhead and setup time.

**Hidden costs to budget for:**
- **Monitor/peripherals:** $150–$300 if you don't own them (the Steam Machine assumes a TV).
- **Windows license:** already included on the Slate.
- **Warranty:** iBUYPOWER typically offers 1-year parts/labor; check Best Buy's return window (usually 15–30 days).

**Alternatives at ~$899:**
- **Build-it-yourself:** A self-built RTX 5060 + Ryzen 5 7600 system runs ~$850–$950 — similar price, better component quality, but you supply the labor and warranty legwork.
- **Steam Machine:** Best for TV-first players who want zero maintenance.
- **Refurb RTX 4070 prebuilt:** Watch for sales; the 4070's 12GB VRAM is a long-term advantage.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the iBUYPOWER Slate with RTX 5060 good for 1440p gaming?
Yes, at medium settings with DLSS enabled. Expect 50–70 FPS in modern AAA titles. For high-refresh 1440p, step up to an RTX 5070-class GPU.

### Can I upgrade the RTX 5060 in the iBUYPOWER Slate later?
Usually yes — prebuilts in this class use standard ATX/micro-ATX motherboards and PCIe slots. Verify the PSU wattage and case clearance first; a 650W+ unit is recommended for a future GPU swap.

### Is the Steam Machine better than a budget gaming PC?
Only for couch/TV gaming where simplicity matters most. A Windows desktop like the Slate is more versatile, runs more games and apps, and is fully upgradeable.

### Does the $899 deal include a monitor or keyboard?
No. The Best Buy deal is the desktop only — budget $150–$300 for a 1080p/1440p monitor and peripherals if you're starting fresh.
