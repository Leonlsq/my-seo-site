## Common Issues, Upgrade Paths, and What to Check Before Buying

Before you click buy, verify these points — they're where prebuilts most often disappoint:

1. **PSU wattage and brand.** Confirm it's at least 750W 80+ Gold from a known brand (Seasonic, Corsair, MSI). Proprietary or no-name PSUs are the #1 prebuilt failure point.
2. **RAM speed.** Ensure the 32GB DDR5 runs at 5600MHz or higher with XMP enabled — some prebuilts ship slow JEDEC kits.
3. **Storage headroom.** 1TB fills fast with modern AAA installs (100–150GB each). Plan a second NVMe drive (~$60) within the first year.
4. **Return window.** Newegg's return policy on prebuilts is tighter than on components — test the machine thoroughly within the first 14 days (run a stress test like Cinebench + FurMark simultaneously).
5. **Warranty terms.** ABS typically offers a 1-year parts/labor warranty. Confirm whether shipping is covered for RMAs.

**Upgrade path:** The 14700F platform (LGA1700) is end-of-life, so the CPU is effectively maxed out — but that's fine, since 20 cores will last 4–5 years. The RTX 5070 can be swapped for a future 60-series card, and RAM/storage upgrades are trivial.
