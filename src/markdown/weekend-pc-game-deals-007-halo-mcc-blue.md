## Step-by-Step: How to Prioritize Your Deal Cart

Follow this sequence to avoid overpaying or buying games you'll never launch.

1. **Check historical lows before checkout.** Cross-reference the current discount against price-tracking sites (IsThereAnyDeal, SteamDB). A "50% off" means nothing if the game hit 75% off three months ago. If this weekend's price is within 5% of the all-time low, it's a green light.
2. **Estimate your real playtime, not the store page's.** Use HowLongToBeat data. A $10 game with 4 hours of content is a worse deal than a $25 game with 40 hours. Cost-per-hour is the only honest metric.
3. **Buy the bundle, not the pieces.** Halo MCC is almost always cheaper as the full collection than buying individual campaigns. Same logic applies to 007 collections—check whether the discounted bundle beats buying single titles.
4. **Verify multiplayer population before buying for online play.** A dead multiplayer mode turns a "deal" into a single-player-only purchase. Check Steam Charts and recent player counts for the specific game.
5. **Set a hard budget and buy in value order.** Rank your picks by cost-per-hour, buy the top one or two, and leave the rest. Sales rotate—FOMO is a marketing tool, not a deadline.

## What You Should Know Before You Buy

**Halo MCC install size is brutal.** The full collection can exceed **100 GB** depending on which campaigns you install. You can selectively install individual games via Steam's DLC management—do this if your SSD is tight. Also note MCC's co-op requires the same campaign installed on all players' machines.

**Blue Prince is not a "deal" game—it's a time game.** It's a puzzle roguelike where progress comes from note-taking and deduction, not reflexes. If you bounce off puzzle games fast, a discount won't fix that. Buy it only if you genuinely enjoy that genre.

**007 titles vary wildly in quality and port status.** Older Bond games may have compatibility quirks on modern Windows. Check the Steam community hub for the specific title before buying—some need community patches or compatibility mode to run cleanly on Windows 10/11.

**Watch for regional pricing and key-reseller risk.** Grey-market key sites can undercut Steam, but carry revocation risk. If you value your library, stick to official storefronts during these sales—the discount gap is usually smaller than the risk.

**Refund windows are your safety net.** On Steam, you can refund within 2 hours of playtime and 14 days of purchase. Use this to test performance on older 007 ports before committing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Halo: The Master Chief Collection worth it on sale in 2026?
Yes—at $10–$15 it's one of the best cost-per-hour values in PC gaming, with 60–100+ hours of campaign and multiplayer content. Just confirm you have the SSD space (100 GB+) and check that the multiplayer playlists you want still have active populations.

### Is Blue Prince a good deal if I don't usually like puzzle games?
Probably not. Blue Prince rewards patience, note-taking, and deduction. A discount doesn't change the core loop—buy it only if you enjoy cerebral puzzle-roguelikes, otherwise it'll sit in your backlog.

### Are the discounted 007 games safe to run on Windows 11?
Most run fine, but older ports may need compatibility mode, community patches, or launcher workarounds. Check the specific game's Steam community discussions before buying, and use the 2-hour refund window to test performance.
