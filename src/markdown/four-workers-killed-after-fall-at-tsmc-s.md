## What the TSMC Fab 22 Tragedy Means for Construction Workers and Contractors

Four contract workers died after a fall at TSMC's Fab 22 construction site in Kaohsiung, Taiwan — a stark reminder that advanced-node fabs are built on scaffolding, steel, and risk. If you work on semiconductor fab construction, manage a subcontractor crew, or run EHS (Environment, Health & Safety) for a Tier-1 supplier, this guide gives you the **concrete fall-protection checklist, regulatory citations, and audit workflow** you need before the next shift. The direct takeaway: most fatal falls on fab sites are preventable through three controls — 100% tie-off above 2 meters, engineered anchor points rated to 22 kN, and a daily pre-task hazard briefing logged with photo evidence.

## Step-by-Step Fall Protection Checklist for Fab Construction Sites

Semiconductor fab builds differ from ordinary high-rise work: cleanroom steel, raised access floors, and multi-level pipe racks create **unprotected edges at 3–12 meters** that shift weekly. Use this sequence before any work at height.

### 1. Pre-Task Hazard Assessment (Daily, 15 minutes)
- Walk the work zone and mark every edge, opening, and skylight with high-visibility barricade tape.
- Photograph each hazard and upload to your EHS log with a timestamp — Taiwan's Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA Taiwan, 職業安全衛生法) requires documented risk assessment under Article 6.
- Assign a **competent person** to sign off; on fab sites this is usually a certified safety supervisor (甲種職業安全衛生業務主管).

### 2. Anchor Point Verification
| Control | Minimum Standard | Fab-Site Note |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor rating | 22 kN (5,000 lbf) per worker | Steel beams on fab frames often need engineered clamps |
| Lifeline | Horizontal system rated 2 workers | Verify manufacturer load chart |
| Harness | Full-body, dorsal D-ring | Inspect webbing for cuts each shift |
| Lanyard | Shock-absorbing, max 1.8 m free fall | Twin-leg for 100% tie-off transitions |

### 3. Fall Protection Hierarchy (Use in Order)
1. **Eliminate** — prefabricate off-site, use ground-level assembly where possible.
2. **Guardrails** — install perimeter rails at 1.1 m height with mid-rail and toe board.
3. **Safety nets** — required where guardrails are infeasible (e.g., under steel erection).
4. **Personal fall arrest** — last resort; requires rescue plan and 4-minute response capability.

### 4. Rescue Plan (The Step Most Sites Skip)
- Designate two trained rescuers per shift.
- Pre-position a rescue kit (tripod, winch, trauma shears) within 100 m of any work-at-height zone.
- Run a **suspension trauma drill** monthly — orthostatic intolerance can incapacitate a worker in under 6 minutes.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

**Issue 1: Language barriers on multinational crews.** Fab 22 crews include Taiwanese, Vietnamese, and Thai subcontractors. Post hazard signage in all three languages and require a bilingual toolbox talk leader.

**Issue 2: Schedule pressure from tool-installation milestones.** When cleanroom tool move-in deadlines compress, guardrails get removed early. Enforce a **permit-to-remove** system: no perimeter control comes down without EHS sign-off and replacement controls in place.

**Issue 3: Subcontractor insurance gaps.** Verify each sub's workers' compensation coverage and safety certification before site access — Taiwan's Ministry of Labor increased penalties for unregistered workers in 2025.

**Cost reality check:** A full personal fall arrest kit runs NT$8,000–15,000 per worker. Engineered horizontal lifeline systems cost NT$150,000–400,000 per level. Compare that to the human and legal cost of a single fatality — the ROI on prevention is not close.

**Alternative approach — digital monitoring:** Wearable fall-detection sensors (e.g., IoT harness clips) alert supervisors within 10 seconds of a fall event. Early adopters on large fab builds report 30–40% faster incident response.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What caused the TSMC Fab 22 fall that killed four workers?
Reports indicate four contract workers fell from height during construction at TSMC's Fab 22 site in Kaohsiung. Taiwanese authorities opened an investigation into fall protection compliance and subcontractor safety management. Exact cause details remain under official review.

### What is the legal fall protection height requirement in Taiwan?
Under Taiwan's Occupational Safety and Health Facilities Rules (職業安全衛生設施規則), fall protection is mandatory for work at **2 meters or higher**, and guardrails are required at open edges above 2 meters.

### How often should fall protection equipment be inspected?
Harnesses and lanyards must be inspected **before each use** and formally documented at least monthly. Any equipment involved in a fall must be removed from service immediately.
