## Why Most Game Suggestion Requests Fail (And How to Fix Yours)

Every week, thousands of players post "recommend me a game" on Reddit's Weekly Game Suggestions Thread — and most walk away with a list of titles they've already played, can't run, or actively hate. The October 16, 2026 thread is live now, and the moderators' pinned advice is blunt: **be detailed or get ignored.** This guide turns that vague instruction into a repeatable formula: a fill-in-the-blank request template, a hardware-spec cheat sheet, and the exact filters that separate a 40-comment thread from a 2-comment dud.

The core problem is signal-to-noise. A post that says "looking for a roguelike" competes with 200 identical posts. A post that says "roguelike, under $20, runs on a GTX 1650 laptop, I loved Hades but bounced off Dead Cells' difficulty" gets laser-targeted answers within minutes. Specificity is the entire game.

## The 6-Point Game Suggestion Request Template

Copy this structure directly into your comment. Each field eliminates a category of bad recommendations.

1. **Genre + sub-genre.** "Roguelike" is too broad. "Turn-based roguelike deckbuilder" or "action roguelite with meta-progression" narrows it instantly.
2. **Non-negotiable features.** Zombies, co-op, controller support, offline play, no permadeath — state hard requirements explicitly. If a feature is optional, label it "nice to have."
3. **Hardware ceiling.** List your GPU, CPU, and RAM. On a Steam Deck, say so — verified status matters more than raw specs.
4. **Budget.** Give a number and a currency. "Under $15 USD" or "Game Pass only" changes every answer.
5. **Reference points.** Name 2–3 games you loved AND 1–2 you bounced off, with a one-line reason. "Loved Hades' pacing, hated Dead Cells' difficulty spikes" tells responders more than a paragraph of prose.
6. **Session length + playstyle.** "30-minute sessions, solo, keyboard only" filters out 100-hour epics and couch co-op picks.

## Common Mistakes That Kill Your Thread

- **Burying the constraint.** If your budget is $0, lead with it. Don't make responders scroll to paragraph three to discover you can't buy anything.
- **Listing only what you hate.** "No anime, no pixel art, no turn-based, no early access" gives responders almost nothing to work with. Always pair a negative with a positive.
- **Ignoring the platform split.** "PC" isn't enough if you mean "Steam Deck only" or "no Epic exclusives." Say the storefront.
- **Forgetting region/language.** If you need full voice acting in your language or a region-locked price, state it.

### The "Already Played" Filter

Before posting, paste your Steam/Backloggd library link or list your last 10 completed games. This single move prevents 80% of wasted suggestions. Responders who see your history will skip the obvious picks and surface hidden gems — which is the entire point of the thread.

## How to Give Great Suggestions (If You're the Responder)

The thread rewards good answerers too. Follow the same discipline in reverse:

- **Match every constraint**, not just the genre. If they said "under $15," don't recommend a $40 game "on sale sometimes."
- **One-line why.** "Slay the Spire — turn-based deckbuilder, runs on anything, infinite replayability" beats a bare title drop.
- **Flag the caveat.** "Great pick, but it's early access and the endgame is thin right now." Honesty earns upvotes and trust.
- **Check the backlog first.** Recommending a game they already listed as completed is the fastest way to get downvoted.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I get good game recommendations on Reddit fast?
Post in the weekly suggestions thread with all six template fields filled in: genre, hard requirements, hardware, budget, reference games, and session length. Detailed posts get targeted answers within minutes; vague posts get buried.

### What specs should I include when asking for game suggestions?
Always include GPU, CPU, RAM, and platform (PC, Steam Deck, console). If you're on a Steam Deck, mention Verified/Playable status and check ProtonDB for anything unverified.

### Is the Weekly Game Suggestions Thread good for backlog help?
Yes — it's specifically designed for it. List your last 10 completed games and your current backlog, then ask responders to pick your next title based on mood and session length.

The October 16, 2026 Weekly Game Suggestions Thread is only as useful as the effort you put in. Spend five minutes filling out the six-point template, paste your hardware specs, and name your reference games — that's the difference between a thread full of noise and a shortlist of games you'll actually finish. Copy the template, post it today, and treat specificity as your single highest-leverage move.
