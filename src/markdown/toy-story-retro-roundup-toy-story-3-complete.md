## Toy Story: Retro Roundup vs Toy Story 3: Complete Edition — What's Actually in Each Bundle?

The launch trailer for **Toy Story: Retro Roundup & Toy Story 3: Complete Edition** dropped and immediately confused a lot of people: is this one game, two games, or a re-release? Here's the direct answer. **Toy Story: Retro Roundup** is a nostalgia-focused collection of classic Toy Story titles (the 16-bit and 32-bit era games), while **Toy Story 3: Complete Edition** is a standalone remaster of the 2010 open-world sandbox game. They are sold as separate products and also bundled together. If you only want the beloved Toy Story 3 sandbox, buy that edition alone. If you want the full history of Toy Story games, get the bundle. This guide breaks down contents, pricing, platform differences, and whether the Retro Roundup emulation quality holds up before you spend money.

## What's Included: Side-by-Side Comparison

The single biggest search question is "what games are in Toy Story: Retro Roundup?" The answer depends on the platform tier, and the trailer doesn't spell it out. Here's the breakdown based on the launch materials:

| Feature | Toy Story: Retro Roundup | Toy Story 3: Complete Edition |
|---|---|---|
| Core content | Classic Toy Story games (16-bit/32-bit era) | Full remaster of the 2010 sandbox game |
| Save states / rewind | Yes (Retro Roundup only) | No (standard autosave) |
| Multiplayer | Local co-op on select titles | Full Toy Box mode, local splitscreen |
| Visual upgrades | CRT filter, upscaling | 4K/60fps on current-gen |
| Price (standard) | $29.99 | $39.99 |
| Bundle price | $59.99 (both) | — |
| Best for | Retro completionists | Sandbox/creative players |

**Key takeaway:** the Retro Roundup is an emulation collection, so expect original frame rates and aspect ratios with optional filters. Toy Story 3: Complete Edition is a native remaster with modern resolution and performance targets.

## Step-by-Step: How to Choose the Right Edition

1. **Check your platform store page first.** The bundle is listed separately from each individual title. On most storefronts, search "Toy Story Retro Roundup" and "Toy Story 3 Complete Edition" individually to confirm the bundle discount is applied — sometimes it isn't shown by default.
2. **Decide based on play style.** Want to build and explore? Toy Story 3's Toy Box mode is the draw. Want to replay the SNES/Genesis-era games? Retro Roundup is the only way to get them.
3. **Verify save-state support.** Retro Roundup supports save states and rewind on most titles; Toy Story 3: Complete Edition does not. If you're a casual retro player, save states are a huge quality-of-life feature.
4. **Check multiplayer needs.** Toy Story 3's Toy Box splitscreen is the stronger co-op experience. Retro Roundup's co-op is limited to specific titles in the collection.
5. **Buy the bundle only if you want both.** At $59.99, the bundle saves roughly $10 versus buying separately — worth it only if you'll actually play the retro titles.

## Common Issues and What You Should Know

- **Emulation quality concerns:** Retro collections sometimes ship with input lag or inaccurate audio. Before buying, check patch notes and community reports for the specific titles you care about — a day-one patch is common.
- **No cross-save between editions:** Toy Story 3: Complete Edition progress does not transfer from the original 2010 release. You start fresh.
- **Physical vs digital:** Confirm whether a physical release exists for your region; some bundles are digital-only, which affects resale value.
- **Performance targets:** The Complete Edition aims for 4K/60fps on current-gen consoles; older hardware may see 1080p/60fps. Check your platform's store listing for exact specs.
- **Refund windows:** If emulation quality disappoints, most storefronts allow refunds within a short window if playtime is minimal — test the retro titles immediately after purchase.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Toy Story: Retro Roundup the same as Toy Story 3?
No. Retro Roundup is a collection of classic-era Toy Story games, while Toy Story 3: Complete Edition is a remaster of the 2010 sandbox game. They are separate products also sold as a bundle.

### How much does the Toy Story Retro Roundup bundle cost?
The bundle is listed at $59.99, versus $29.99 for Retro Roundup alone and $39.99 for Toy Story 3: Complete Edition alone — a roughly $10 saving if you want both.

### Does Toy Story 3: Complete Edition have multiplayer?
Yes. It includes local splitscreen Toy Box mode. Retro Roundup's co-op is limited to select titles in the collection.

### Can I buy Toy Story 3: Complete Edition without the retro collection?
Yes. Both titles are sold individually, so you can skip the bundle if you only want the sandbox remaster.

## Final Verdict

**Buy Toy Story 3: Complete Edition alone if** you want the sandbox experience and don't care about retro games — it's the stronger, more modern product. **Buy the bundle only if** you're a completionist who genuinely wants the classic titles, and only after confirming emulation quality via patch notes. Skip the Retro Roundup as a standalone purchase unless the specific classic games included are must-haves for you; at $29.99, it's priced for nostalgia, not value.
