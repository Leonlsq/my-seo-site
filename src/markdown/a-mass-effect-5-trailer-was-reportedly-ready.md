### How to Back Up Your Mass Effect Saves (Do This Today)

1. Open File Explorer and navigate to `Documents\BioWare\Mass Effect Legendary Edition\Save`.
2. Copy the entire `Save` folder to an external drive or cloud backup.
3. For ME3 multiplayer, screenshot your manifest and N7 rank for your records.
4. On Steam, right-click the game → **Properties → Installed Files → Back up game files**.
5. Install **ME3Tweaks Mod Manager** to keep mods reproducible if you ever reinstall.

### Step-by-Step: Recreate the Mass Effect Experience in Starfield

If you want the closest modern "ship + crew + choices" loop, Starfield is your best bet. Optimize it like this:

1. **Prioritize the main quest** until you unlock the Constellation crew — companions are the heart of the ME feel.
2. **Invest in Ship Design perks** early so you can build a Normandy-style vessel.
3. **Install the "Crew Overhaul" and "Immersive Landing" mods** from Nexus to deepen squad interaction.
4. **Toggle dialogue camera** in Settings → Gameplay for a more cinematic, Mass Effect-style conversation view.
5. **Set difficulty to Hard** — it makes companion abilities and cover matter, mirroring ME2's tactical combat.
