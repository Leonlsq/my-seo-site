1. **Check your address eligibility.** The program is geo-fenced to Minneapolis, Chicago, and Miami metro areas. Suburban addresses may be excluded — confirm your ZIP before investing time.
2. **Find the official intake form.** Go directly to HUMXN's official site or verified press release link. Avoid third-party "sign up here" pages that scrape the news — those are lead-gen traps.

5. **Schedule the visit.** HUMXN coordinates a vetted technician. Confirm whether the repair is **fully free, reimbursed after the fact, or capped** — policies vary by pilot phase.

## The Real Trade-Off: Your Data, Your Privacy, Your Home

This is where most articles stop — don't. The value of this program to HUMXN is the **training dataset**, and that dataset is your home. Before you say yes, evaluate these risks:

- **Video of your home interior.** Cameras capture more than the sink — layouts, valuables, family members in frame. Ask for the camera scope and whether faces/identifying details are blurred.
- **Data resale and model training.** Confirm in the contract whether footage can be licensed to third parties or used in perpetuity. "Anonymized" is not the same as "deleted."
- **Insurance and liability.** If a HUMXN-coordinated tech damages your property, who pays? Get the liability coverage in writing before the visit.
- **Warranty voiding.** Some HVAC manufacturers void warranties if non-certified techs service equipment. Verify the technician's licensing (NATE certification for HVAC, state plumbing license).
- **Scheduling friction.** Data-collection visits take longer and may require you to be home and on camera.

### Cost-Benefit Quick Math

A typical HVAC tune-up runs **$80–$150**; a plumbing service call is **$100–$250** plus parts. If HUMXN covers a $200 repair, you're effectively "selling" a few hours of home footage for that amount. Whether that's worth it depends entirely on your comfort with surveillance and data licensing — for many homeowners, the answer is **no for interior camera work, maybe for a simple diagnostic visit**.

## Common Issues and Safer Alternatives

- **"Is this a scam?"** The program is tied to a real company and press coverage, but always verify via the official domain — never pay a "processing fee" to join.
- **"Can I opt out of recording?"** Usually no — the recording *is* the payment. If a rep says otherwise, get it in writing.
- **Safer alternatives:** manufacturer rebates, utility company HVAC tune-up programs, home warranty plans, and local income-qualified repair assistance (e.g., city weatherization programs) offer savings without data trade-offs.
