## Is Hellraiser: Revival Worth It? A Practical Buying Framework

Before you pre-order, run through this checklist based on what matters for a licensed horror game:

1. **Wait for the gameplay demo.** Barker's praise covers tone and story — it does not confirm frame rate, controls, or bug-free launch. Watch at least 20 minutes of unedited gameplay.
2. **Check the developer's track record.** Licensed horror games live or die on optimization. Look up the studio's previous PC/console releases for patch history.
3. **Verify the puzzle-box mechanic.** The Lament Configuration is central to *Hellraiser* — confirm the game treats it as an interactive mechanic, not a cutscene prop.
4. **Set a price ceiling.** Narrative horror games typically land at $40–$70. If it's a 6-hour experience at full price, wait for a sale.
5. **Check PC system requirements early.** If you're on PC, compare the listed GPU/VRAM requirements against your build before launch week.

### Red Flags to Watch For
- No raw gameplay footage close to launch
- Review embargo lifting on release day only
- Season-pass announcements before the base game is proven
