## Arkane Lyon Acquisition Rumors: What Dishonored Fans Actually Need to Know

If you just saw the Jason Schreier Bluesky post claiming that **several companies — including Ubisoft Entertainment — have made offers to buy Dishonored developer Arkane Lyon from Xbox**, your first question is probably: *what happens to my games, and is Dishonored 3 dead?* Here is the direct takeaway: **nothing changes for your existing library today.** Purchases and saves stay tied to your Microsoft/Bethesda account, and no storefront delisting has been announced. This guide breaks down what the rumor means, how to protect your library, and how to read the signals for a potential Dishonored sequel.

A few grounding facts before we go further:

- **Arkane Lyon** is the studio behind *Dishonored 2*, *Dishonored: Death of the Outsider*, and *Marvel's Blade*. It is separate from **Arkane Austin**, which made *Redfall* and was closed in 2024.
- Arkane Lyon sits under **ZeniMax/Bethesda**, which Microsoft acquired in 2021.
- The report is a **rumor sourced from a social post**, not a confirmed transaction. Treat it as unverified until Microsoft, Xbox, or Ubisoft publishes a statement.

## Step-by-Step: How to Protect Your Dishonored Library During Acquisition Rumors

Acquisition chatter historically triggers two real-world risks for players: **storefront delisting** (rare but possible) and **account/entitlement confusion** (more common). Use this checklist now, before anything is confirmed.

1. **Verify your entitlements.** On Xbox, go to *Settings → Account → Subscriptions & purchases* and confirm *Dishonored: Definitive Edition*, *Dishonored 2*, and *Death of the Outsider* appear as owned. On Steam, check *Account → Licenses and product key activations*.
2. **Install and launch each game once.** A successful offline launch caches your license locally and confirms the game runs on your hardware.
3. **Back up saves.** Xbox cloud saves are automatic, but you can also copy local saves to a USB drive via *Settings → System → Storage*. On PC, back up `Documents\My Games\Dishonored2` and the Steam `userdata` folder.
4. **Screenshot your purchase history.** A dated screenshot of your library is free insurance if a support dispute ever arises after a studio transfer.
5. **Avoid gray-market key resellers.** If a sale/transfer happens, third-party keys bought from unauthorized resellers are the first entitlements to fail verification.
6. **Wishlist rather than pre-order.** If a new Arkane Lyon title is announced mid-transition, wait for platform confirmation before paying.

### What Actually Transfers in a Studio Sale?

| Asset | Typically Transfers? | Player Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Studio staff & leadership | Yes | Future game quality/direction |
| IP ownership (Dishonored) | **Usually stays with publisher** | Existing games unaffected |
| Publishing rights | Negotiated case-by-case | Possible delisting risk |
| Your game licenses | **No — stays with your account** | None |
| Save files & cloud data | No | None |

The key insight most news coverage misses: **buying a studio is not the same as buying its IP.** Microsoft owns the Dishonored trademark; a buyer of Arkane Lyon would likely acquire talent and a pipeline, not the back catalog. That is why delisting fears are usually overblown.

## What This Means for Dishonored 3 and Marvel's Blade

This is where the rumor gets genuinely interesting for fans.

- **Marvel's Blade** is Arkane Lyon's confirmed current project, announced at The Game Awards 2023. A studio sale mid-development would require Disney/Marvel to approve any transfer of the licensing deal — a major legal hurdle that makes a clean sale unlikely in the short term.
- **Dishonored 3** has never been officially announced. It appeared in the 2023 FTC v. Microsoft document leak as an internal ZeniMax roadmap item, but that document is now outdated and was never a commitment.
- **Ubisoft as a suitor is plausible on paper** — Ubisoft is French, Arkane Lyon is based in Lyon, France, and Ubisoft already runs a major French studio network. But Ubisoft's own financial pressures make a large cash acquisition a stretch.

**Bottom line for fans:** a sale would most likely *delay* any Dishonored sequel rather than kill it, because IP rights and publishing control would remain with Microsoft unless separately negotiated.

## Common Misconceptions and What to Watch

- **"Arkane is shutting down."** No evidence supports this. The report describes *offers to buy*, not closure.
- **"My games will be removed."** No delisting has been announced. Even in past studio sales, back catalogs have largely stayed purchasable.
- **"This is confirmed."** It is not. Watch for official statements from **Xbox Wire**, **Bethesda's newsroom**, and **Ubisoft's investor relations page** — those are the only sources that matter.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Will Dishonored games be removed from Steam or Xbox if Arkane Lyon is sold?
Almost certainly not in the near term. Microsoft owns the Dishonored IP and publishing rights, so a sale of the studio would not automatically affect storefront availability. No delisting has been announced.

### Is Dishonored 3 confirmed?
No. Dishonored 3 has never been officially announced. It only appeared in a 2023 internal Microsoft document leak, which is not a public commitment.

### Does Ubisoft buying Arkane Lyon affect Marvel's Blade?
It could. *Marvel's Blade* is a licensed Disney/Marvel title, so any studio transfer would require Marvel's approval and contract renegotiation — a significant obstacle to a quick sale.

### Should I buy Dishonored games now in case of delisting?
If you want to own them, buying directly from Steam or the Xbox Store is low-risk and often discounted. Avoid unauthorized key resellers, whose licenses are the most likely to fail verification after any ownership change.

## Final Verdict

**Do not panic-buy, and do not assume Dishonored 3 is canceled.** The Arkane Lyon acquisition report is an unverified rumor, and the structure of the deal means your existing games and saves are safe regardless of outcome. Your best move as a player is simple: **verify your entitlements, back up your saves, and follow official channels** rather than social posts. If you have been waiting to play *Dishonored 2* or *Death of the Outsider*, buy them from an official storefront during the next sale — that is the only action that actually protects your access. Everything else is speculation until Xbox or Ubisoft says otherwise.
