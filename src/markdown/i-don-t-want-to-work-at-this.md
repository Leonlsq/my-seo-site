## EA Layoffs 2026: How to Check If You're Affected and What to Do Next

If you're an EA employee waking up to headlines about mass layoffs, mismanagement, and corporate greed—and the phrase "I don't want to work at this place anymore" is echoing across your team chat—you need facts, not panic. This guide walks you through **how to verify whether your role is impacted**, what your severance and legal rights typically look like, and the exact steps to protect your income and career starting today. The direct takeaway: **do not sign anything, do not post publicly, and do not assume your manager knows more than HR's official notice**—verify first, then act.

## Step-by-Step: Verify Your Status and Protect Yourself

Layoffs at studios like EA are usually executed in waves tied to project cancellations, studio consolidations, and quarterly cost targets. Here's the practical playbook:

1. **Check official channels first.** Look for a company-wide email, an HR portal notification, or a scheduled all-hands. EA historically communicates reductions through direct manager 1:1s and formal HR documentation—not Slack rumors. Screenshot any official communication with timestamps.
2. **Confirm your WARN Act exposure (US employees).** Under the federal Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act, employers with 100+ employees must give **60 days' written notice** for mass layoffs (typically 50+ employees at a single site). Check your state's mini-WARN law—California, New York, and New Jersey have stricter thresholds. If notice was short, you may have a claim.
3. **Request your severance package in writing before signing.** Typical game-industry packages run **2 weeks to 1 month of base pay per year of service**, plus COBRA subsidies and pro-rated bonuses. Never accept a verbal offer.
4. **Audit your access and data.** Export personal files (portfolio pieces you own, contacts) *before* your accounts are deactivated—but never take confidential company IP, which can void severance and trigger legal action.
5. **File for unemployment immediately.** In most US states you can file the same week your termination is effective; waiting costs you money. Keep pay stubs and your termination letter handy.

| Item | What to Verify | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| WARN notice | 60-day written notice (US) | Potential legal claim if skipped |
| Severance terms | Weeks per year, bonus, COBRA | Negotiable before signing |
| Non-compete | Scope and duration | Affects your next job |
| Equity/vesting | Unvested RSUs, options | Often forfeited—ask for acceleration |

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

**"My manager says nothing is confirmed."** Managers are frequently briefed *after* HR finalizes lists. Treat unofficial reassurance as noise and rely on written HR communication.

**Severance negotiation is real—but narrow.** Individual contributors rarely move the base number, but you can often negotiate **extended health coverage, accelerated equity vesting, or a neutral reference letter**. Ask once, professionally, in writing.

**Watch the non-compete.** Game studios increasingly enforce 6–12 month non-competes. Before accepting a rival offer, have an employment attorney review the clause—some states (California) largely void them.

**If you're staying:** document your workload, request written confirmation of your role's scope, and update your portfolio quietly. Survivor burnout after mass layoffs is a documented retention risk—protect your leverage.

**Alternatives if you're job hunting:** studios actively hiring in 2026 include independent AA studios, live-service teams at competing publishers, and adjacent tech roles (tools engineering, technical art, production). Tailor your portfolio to shipped, measurable outcomes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I know if I'm part of the EA layoffs?
Check for an official HR email or portal notice and a direct manager meeting invite. Cross-reference with your state's WARN filing database—many states publish layoff notices publicly within days.

### What severance does EA typically offer in layoffs?
Historically, packages have included roughly 2 weeks to 1 month of pay per year of service, health coverage extensions, and pro-rated bonuses. Terms vary by region and role—always get the written offer before signing.

### Can I negotiate severance after a mass layoff?
Yes, within limits. Base pay is rarely negotiable for individual contributors, but COBRA duration, equity acceleration, and reference terms sometimes are. Ask once, in writing, through HR.

### Should I post about the layoffs on social media?
Avoid specifics about internal projects, managers, or confidential details—public posts can complicate severance or future hiring. General, professional commentary is safer than venting.

## Final Verdict

If you're caught in the EA layoffs, **treat the first 72 hours as a documentation and verification sprint**: confirm your status in writing, don't sign severance on the spot, file for unemployment immediately, and get a lawyer's eyes on any non-compete. The frustration behind "I don't want to work at this place anymore" is valid—but channel it into protecting your severance, your equity, and your next move. Verify first, negotiate second, and job-hunt with leverage, not panic.
