## No Man's Sky Forsaken Update 7.10: How to Optimize, Install on iOS, and Build the Corvette

The **No Man's Sky: FORSAKEN** update (version 7.10) dropped with spooky new gameplay, strange traders, haunting mementos, mysterious package deliveries, and — most notably — a full port of No Man's Sky to iOS devices. If you're searching for how to actually *use* this update rather than just read the patch notes, you're in the right place. This guide covers the concrete steps to squeeze performance out of Forsaken on PC and console, get the iOS version running without crashing, and construct the new eerie corvette efficiently. Bottom line up front: update your GPU drivers and verify game files before anything else, and on iOS, disable background app refresh and lower the resolution scale to avoid the most common crash loop.

## Step-by-Step Setup: Performance, iOS Install, and Corvette Construction

### 1. Optimize PC/Console Settings for Forsaken's New Effects

Forsaken adds volumetric fog, particle-heavy "haunting" effects, and seamless warp transitions that stress older hardware. Use these baseline settings:

| Setting | Low-End (GTX 1060 / RX 580) | Mid-Range (RTX 3060 / RX 6600) | High-End (RTX 4070+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Texture Quality | High | Ultra | Ultra |
| Shadow Quality | Medium | High | Ultra |
| Volumetric Effects | Off | Medium | High |
| Planet Quality | Medium | High | Ultra |
| Anti-Aliasing | TAA | DLSS/FSR Quality | DLSS/FSR Quality |
| Base Complexity | Medium | High | Ultra |

**Steps:**
1. Launch the game and open **Options > Graphics**.
2. Set **Volumetric Effects** to Off or Medium first — this is the single biggest FPS killer in Forsaken's new spooky biomes.
3. Enable **DLSS** (NVIDIA) or **FSR 2** (AMD/Intel) at "Quality" mode; this typically recovers 20–35% performance.
4. Cap framerate to your monitor's refresh rate to stabilize the new seamless warp transitions, which can cause stutter if uncapped.
5. Restart the game fully so shader caches rebuild for the new assets.

### 2. Install and Stabilize No Man's Sky on iOS

The full experience is now on iOS, but it's demanding. Recommended devices: iPhone 15 Pro / Pro Max, iPad with M1/M2 chips or A17 Pro.

1. Download from the App Store (requires ~12–14 GB free space; check the listing for the current size).
2. On first launch, **skip cloud sync** until you've completed the tutorial — syncing mid-tutorial causes save conflicts.
3. In **Settings > Graphics**, drop **Resolution Scale** to 70–80% and disable **Motion Blur**.
4. Enable **Low Power Mode off** during play — iOS throttles GPU performance aggressively otherwise.
5. If the app crashes on launch, force-quit all background apps, restart the device, and reinstall.

### 3. Build the Eerie Corvette Efficiently

The new corvette is constructed at a **Corvette Assembly Station** (found via the new strange traders). Required materials typically include:

- **Chromatic Metal** (approx. 400–600 units)
- **Activated Indium or Emeril** (for the hull plating)
- **Haunting Memento** (obtained from the new spooky encounters — you need at least one as a catalyst)

**Steps:**
1. Complete the introductory Forsaken mission chain to unlock the Assembly Station blueprint.
2. Farm Chromatic Metal by refining **Copper → Chromatic Metal** (2:1 ratio) at any refiner.
3. Collect a **Haunting Memento** from the new ghostly NPC encounters — they spawn more frequently in abandoned systems.
4. Deliver at least one **mysterious package** to a strange trader to unlock the corvette's unique cosmetic variants.
5. Assemble at the station and test the seamless warp drive in an adjacent system.

## Common Issues, Workarounds, and What You Should Know

**iOS crash loop:** The most reported issue. Workaround: disable iCloud background sync, lower resolution scale, and ensure at least 20 GB free storage (the game needs headroom for shader compilation).

**Seamless warp stutter on PC:** Cap framerate and disable "Motion Blur." The warp transition streams assets mid-flight; an uncapped framerate causes hitching on HDD installs. Move the game to an SSD if possible.

**Missing Haunting Mementos:** These are RNG-gated. Increase spawn odds by visiting **abandoned or uncharted systems** and completing the mysterious package deliveries — traders reward mementos more reliably than random encounters.

**Cross-save caution:** If you play on both PC and iOS, back up your save manually before syncing. The 7.10 build has had isolated reports of corvette build progress not transferring.

**Cost note:** The iOS version is a separate purchase (check current App Store pricing); it is not included free with PC/console ownership. Budget accordingly if you want cross-device play.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is No Man's Sky Forsaken free on iOS if I own it on PC?
No. The iOS version is a separate App Store purchase. Your save can transfer via cross-save, but the license does not.

### How do I get Haunting Mementos fast in the Forsaken update?
Farm abandoned systems and complete mysterious package deliveries to strange traders — these reward mementos more consistently than random encounters.

### Why does No Man's Sky keep crashing on my iPhone?
Most crashes stem from insufficient free storage, background app refresh, or high resolution scale. Free up 20+ GB, disable background refresh, and set resolution scale to 70–80%.

### What are the corvette build requirements in update 7.10?
You need a Corvette Assembly Station (unlocked via the Forsaken mission chain), roughly 400–600 Chromatic Metal, hull metals like Activated Indium, and at least one Haunting Memento as a catalyst.

## Final Verdict

Forsaken is a genuinely strong update, but it's hardware-hungry. On iOS, only jump in if you have a recent Pro-tier device and 20 GB to spare; otherwise, wait for optimization patches. And for the corvette: farm Chromatic Metal early and hoard every Haunting Memento you find — you'll thank yourself later.
