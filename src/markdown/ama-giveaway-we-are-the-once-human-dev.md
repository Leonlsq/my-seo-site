## Once Human Ocean DLC: How to Prepare Your Base and Build for Launch Day

The Once Human Free Ocean DLC drops on **October 21 (PT)**, and if you are still running a landlocked box base in Nalcott, you are about to get wrecked by the new meta. The dev team's AMA confirmed that the ocean update isn't just a cosmetic map expansion—it introduces **underwater territory, aquatic Deviations, and tidal base mechanics** that punish players who haven't prepped their infrastructure. This guide cuts through the hype and gives you the exact base layouts, resource stockpiles, and PC settings you need to dominate the new coastline on day one. Whether you are a returning player or just jumping in after seeing the Steam announcement, here is your actionable launch checklist.

## Step-by-Step Guide: Prepping for the Ocean Update

Do not wait until the servers go live to figure this out. Use the final hours before launch to execute this sequence:

1.  **Relocate Your Territory Core to the Coast.** The Ocean DLC adds buildable zones along the shoreline. Move your base now while land is cheap. Prioritize flat coastal plots near **Chalk Peak** and **Iron River**—these border the new deep-water zones and give you the shortest swim distance to high-tier aquatic resources.
2.  **Stockpile Oxygen and Water Purifiers.** Underwater exploration drains oxygen. Craft at least **20 Oxygen Canisters** and set up a **Water Purifier** chain before the patch hits. The new "Brine" resource requires purification before it can be used in crafting, and early-game purifier queues will be slammed.
3.  **Reroll Your Deviations for Aquatic Traits.** The AMA confirmed new water-based Deviations. Farm **Gel** and **Butterfly** Deviations now and save your **Mitsuko's Marks** for the new capture capsules. Do not waste marks on land Deviations—the ocean meta favors mobility and underwater combat.
4.  **Upgrade Your Memetic Tree for "Seafaring."** Respec your Memetics before launch. The new **Seafaring** branch unlocks the **Aqua-Skiff** vehicle and underwater storage crates. You need 15 Memetic Points banked to grab the tier-1 nodes immediately.
5.  **Cap Your Gear Score at 8,000+.** The new ocean mobs are tuned for endgame players. If you are below **8,000 GS**, spend the next few hours farming **Monolith** bosses for gear fragments. Underwater combat has reduced visibility, so high burst damage matters more than sustained DPS.

### Recommended Launch-Day PC Settings

The Ocean DLC adds heavy water physics and volumetric fog. If you are on a mid-range rig, these settings prevent stutter during the first tidal events:

| Setting | Recommended Value | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Water Quality** | Medium | High tanks FPS during storms; Medium keeps reflections readable. |
| **Volumetric Fog** | Low | Underwater fog is the biggest GPU killer in the new zones. |
| **View Distance** | High | You need to spot aquatic Deviations before they aggro. |
| **DLSS / FSR** | Quality | Essential for 1440p+ players to hold 60 FPS in deep water. |
| **Texture Quality** | High (VRAM permitting) | New coral and shipwreck assets look muddy on Low. |

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

The biggest mistake players make with major DLC launches is **overbuilding on day one**. The Ocean DLC introduces **tidal destruction**—structures placed below the high-water mark take damage during storm events. If you build your main base directly on the beach, you will lose it. Instead, build a **stilted platform base** on the cliff edge and use a separate **underwater outpost** for resource farming. The outpost can be rebuilt cheaply; your main base cannot.

Another issue: **cross-progression sync**. The AMA confirmed cross-play across PC, mobile, and console. If you play on PC but log in on mobile to check your base, your **territory permissions** may reset if you don't enable **two-factor authentication** on your Once Human account. Do this before launch to avoid losing access to your coastal plot.

Finally, consider the **free-to-play economy**. The Ocean DLC is free, but the new **Aqua-Skiff** vehicle requires **Starchrom** to unlock skins and upgrades. Do not spend real money on the first day—wait for the community to datamine the drop rates. Historically, Once Human event currencies are cheapest on day 3 after launch.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the Once Human Ocean DLC free?
Yes. The Ocean DLC is a free content update launching October 21 (PT) for all platforms. There is no paid expansion pass required to access the new map, Deviations, or base-building zones.

### Do I need to start a new character for the Ocean DLC?
No. Your existing character and cross-progression carry over. However, you must complete the **"Strange Tides"** prologue quest in Nalcott to unlock the new coastal territory zones.

### What are the best Deviations for underwater exploration in Once Human?
Based on the AMA and beta data, prioritize the **Aqua-Gel** (oxygen regeneration) and the **Tide-Butterfly** (underwater speed boost). Both are captured in the new deep-water zones, so bring capture capsules on your first dive.

### Will the Ocean DLC break my existing base?
Only if your base is built below the new high-water mark. Check your territory's elevation in build mode—if the foundation is within 3 meters of sea level, relocate it before October 21 to avoid tidal damage.
