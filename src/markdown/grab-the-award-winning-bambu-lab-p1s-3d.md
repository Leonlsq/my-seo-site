## Step-by-Step: Setting Up the P1S + AMS Bundle Correctly

Getting a good first print is easy, but getting *great* multi-color prints requires following the sequence below.

1. **Unbox and remove all shipping screws.** The P1S has transport screws on the bed and gantry — leaving one in causes "Z-axis homing failed" errors on first boot.
2. **Mount the AMS on top of the printer.** Route the PTFE tube from the AMS buffer to the toolhead. Keep the bend radius gentle; kinks cause filament loading failures.
3. **Load spools in slots 1–4.** Push filament until you feel it grab. The AMS reads RFID Bambu spools automatically; third-party spools need manual profile entry.
4. **Run the auto-calibration.** From the touchscreen: *Settings → Calibration → Run Full Calibration*. This handles vibration compensation and flow dynamics.
5. **Set your slicer profile.** In Bambu Studio, select "Bambu Lab P1S 0.4 nozzle" and enable *AMS* under the filament tab. Assign colors per slot.
6. **Print a first-layer test.** Use the built-in benchy or a 20×20 mm square. Check for adhesion before committing to a multi-hour print.
7. **Flush calibration.** For multi-color, run *Flush Volume* calibration in Bambu Studio — this reduces purge waste by 15–30% on average.

### AMS-Specific Tips Most Guides Skip

- **Keep the AMS lid closed** during printing. Open lids let humidity in and cause PETG stringing.
- **Use the desiccant packs** in the AMS bays and swap them monthly.
- **Don't mix abrasive and standard filaments** in adjacent slots — CF-filled filament wears the AMS feeder gears faster.
- **Set "Flush into infill"** in slicer settings to cut purge waste on large prints.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

### Frequent P1S + AMS Problems and Fixes

- **"AMS filament ran out" false alarm:** Usually a bent filament tip. Cut the end at 45° and reload.
- **Layer shifting at high speed:** Reduce outer wall speed to 200 mm/s; the P1S lacks the X1C's LiDAR correction.
- **Bed adhesion failures on ABS:** Preheat the chamber by running the bed at 100 °C for 10 minutes before printing.
- **AMS motor grinding:** Filament tangled on the spool. Respoll or use a spool holder adapter.

### Is the P1S Bundle Better Than the P1P or X1C?
