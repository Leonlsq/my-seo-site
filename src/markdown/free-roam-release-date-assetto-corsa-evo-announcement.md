## Assetto Corsa EVO Free Roam Release Date: What the Announcement Actually Confirms

If you just watched the Assetto Corsa EVO free roam announcement video and are now frantically searching for a date, here's the direct answer: **Kunos has not locked a standalone "free roam release date" — the open-world map ships as part of the 1.0 launch window, currently targeted for a Q2 2026 full release, with the free roam zone arriving in the final Early Access phase (v0.6–0.7).** The announcement video is a feature reveal, not a date drop. This guide breaks down exactly what the trailer shows, what it means for your current install, and how to set up your rig so you're ready the day the open map goes live — because the free roam mode is the single biggest performance and input-latency stress test EVO will ever throw at your hardware.

**Key takeaway:** the trailer is a *capability demo*. Don't cancel plans around a date that doesn't exist yet. Instead, use the Early Access window to tune your setup — free roam is where frame-time consistency matters more than peak FPS, because a 1 fps stutter at 120 km/h in traffic is far more jarring than a low average on a closed track.

## Pre-Launch Setup: Optimize Your Rig for Free Roam Now

Free roam in EVO stresses the CPU (traffic AI, streaming assets) and VRAM (large map, high-res textures) differently than circuit racing. Here's how to prepare:

1. **Lock your frame rate, don't chase it.** Set a hard cap at your monitor's refresh (e.g., 120 Hz) and enable G-Sync/FreeSync. In an open map with streaming, a *stable* 90 fps beats a fluctuating 110–140 fps every time.
2. **Move EVO to an NVMe SSD.** The open-world streaming will hammer disk I/O. A SATA SSD is the bare minimum; NVMe cuts asset-pop stutter dramatically. Leave at least 30 GB free headroom.
3. **Cap VRAM usage at 85%.** In `graphics.ini`, set texture quality one notch below "Ultra" if you have 8 GB VRAM. The open map loads far more unique textures than a circuit — you will hit the VRAM wall that never appeared in time trials.
4. **Set traffic AI density to "Medium" first.** The trailer shows dense traffic; start conservative, benchmark, then scale up. CPU-bound stutter here is the #1 complaint driver.
5. **Calibrate your wheel's rotation to 900°.** Free roam rewards realistic steering input. Set your wheelbase to 900° in the driver software, then match it in EVO's input menu — mismatched rotation is the most common "the car feels twitchy" fix.
6. **Enable telemetry logging.** Turn on EVO's built-in telemetry so you can spot frame-time spikes tied to specific map zones once free roam drops.

## Common Issues, Workarounds & What to Expect

- **"Free roam isn't in my build."** It isn't live yet. If you're on Early Access, the open map arrives in the final pre-1.0 phase. Check the in-game build number, not the store page.
- **Stutter on the highway sections.** Almost always CPU/streaming-bound. Lower traffic density before touching graphics settings.
- **Wheel feels heavy at low speed.** Free roam's persistent tyre model means cold tyres behave differently — do a 2–3 minute warm-up lap before judging the handling.
- **VR users:** expect the open map to be the heaviest VR scene in EVO. Budget for a 20–30% resolution scale reduction versus circuit racing.
