## Common Questions and What You Should Know

**Is this actually tax-deductible?** Generally no — you're receiving goods (game keys) in exchange, so it's a purchase, not a donation, in most jurisdictions. The *net profits* go to UNICEF, not your full payment.

**Why is PlanetPlay able to do this?** Game developers and studios **donated their keys** to the bundle, which is why 100% of net profits can go to charity. PlanetPlay covers operational costs from the "net" portion.

**What if a key doesn't work?** Contact PlanetPlay support directly — as an official reseller, they can issue replacements or escalate to Steam. Document your purchase confirmation.

**Can I buy this as a gift?** Yes — keys are delivered as codes, so you can forward them to anyone with a Steam account.

**Is there a cheaper alternative?** Not for this specific lineup. Comparable bundles (Humble Choice, Fanatical) run $12–$20/month but rarely include titles like Blue Prince or Train Sim World 6 at this combined value.
