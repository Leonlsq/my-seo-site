## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Your $159 Elegoo Printer Correctly

### For FDM (Neptune 4 series)
1. **Assemble on a level, sturdy surface.** A wobbly table ruins print quality more than any slicer setting.
2. **Check belt tension and eccentric nuts.** Loose gantries cause layer shifting — tighten until wheels grip without binding.
3. **Run auto bed leveling** (the Neptune 4 uses a strain-gauge probe). Do this cold, then verify with a paper test at Z=0.
4. **Set your Z-offset carefully.** Too low = scratched bed; too high = no adhesion. Adjust in 0.05mm increments.
5. **Slice your first print** in Elegoo Cura or OrcaSlicer at 0.2mm layer height, 200°C nozzle, 60°C bed for PLA.
6. **Print a calibration cube and a Benchy** before anything ambitious.

### For Resin (Mars 5 series)
1. **Ventilate and wear nitrile gloves + safety glasses.** Uncured resin is a skin sensitizer.
2. **Level the build plate** using the included card method — loosen the four screws, home the plate, tighten in an X pattern.
3. **Fill the vat to the max line only** — overfilling causes spills into the LCD.
4. **Run an exposure test** (e.g., AmeraLabs Town) to dial in layer exposure for your specific resin.
5. **Wash in IPA for 3–5 minutes**, then **cure under UV for 2–4 minutes**.
6. **Filter and store leftover resin** back in the bottle, away from sunlight.

## Hidden Costs and What You Should Know

The $159 sticker is the entry fee, not the total. Budget realistically:

- **Resin route:** Add $60–100 for resin, IPA, gloves, a wash-and-cure station, and a funnel/filter kit. Total realistic startup: **$220–260.**
- **FDM route:** Add $20–40 for a spool or two of PLA. Total realistic startup: **$180–200.**
- **Stock risk:** Elegoo states savings last "as long as stock lasts" — popular SKUs (Mars 5, Neptune 4) sell out first. Set a price alert rather than waiting.
- **Warranty note:** Sale units carry the same standard warranty, but keep your order confirmation — Elegoo support often asks for it.

**Alternatives worth comparing:** Anycubic's Kobra series and Creality's Ender-3 V3 often price-match during Black Friday. If the Elegoo SKU you want is sold out, those are the closest equivalents at similar discounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is a $159 Elegoo 3D printer good for beginners?
Yes — the Mars 5 and Neptune 4 are both beginner-friendly with auto-leveling and large communities. Resin has a steeper safety learning curve (chemicals, curing), while FDM is more forgiving for a first machine.

### What's the difference between Elegoo resin and FDM printers on sale?
Resin printers use UV light and liquid photopolymer for ultra-fine detail but need washing/curing gear. FDM printers melt filament for larger, tougher functional parts with far less mess and lower running costs.

### How long does the Elegoo Black Friday sale last?
Elegoo's November sale runs the entire month, but discounts are stock-dependent. High-demand models like the Mars 5 and Neptune 4 typically sell out before month-end.

### Do I need a wash-and-cure station for a $159 resin printer?
Not strictly — you can hand-wash in IPA and cure in sunlight — but a wash-and-cure station ($60–90) dramatically improves consistency and safety. Budget for it if you plan to print regularly.
