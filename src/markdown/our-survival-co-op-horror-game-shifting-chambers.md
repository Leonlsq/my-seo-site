## Shifting Chambers Demo: How to Set Up PC & VR for Co-Op Survival

The **Shifting Chambers demo** is out now on Steam, and it's a co-op survival horror experience that punishes players who rely on muscle memory. The core hook: when the bell rings, the mansion's rooms physically relocate, so memorizing the map is worthless. You and up to two friends must solve environmental puzzles, escape one floor of the mansion, and avoid **Manor Guardians** that hear your voice and adapt to your tactics. The demo supports both **PC and VR**, which means your first job is getting the right setup so the shifting doesn't break your session. This guide covers the optimal settings, co-op configuration, and the fixes players are already reporting.

## Step-by-Step Guide: PC & VR Setup for the Demo

Before you invite friends, get your own build stable. The demo is short, so a bad first impression from stutter or mic issues will be hard to shake.

### 1. Verify Your Platform and Store Page
- Download the demo directly from the Steam store page: `https://store.steampowered.com/app/3972140/Shifting_Chambers/`
- Confirm the demo is installed on the same drive as your Steam library to avoid asset streaming hitches.
- If you plan to play VR, launch SteamVR **first**, then start the demo from your library.

### 2. Recommended PC Settings (Non-VR)
| Setting | Recommended | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | Native (1080p/1440p) | The demo's shifting rooms rely on lighting cues; upscaling can blur them |
| Shadows | Medium | Guardians use sound, not sight, but shadow pop-in distracts in VR |
| Anti-Aliasing | TAA | Reduces shimmer on shifting geometry |
| V-Sync | Off (use frame cap) | Lower input latency when reorienting after a shift |
| Audio | Headphones, 100% | **Critical**—Guardians are audio-driven |

### 3. VR-Specific Configuration
- Use a wired connection (Link Cable or DisplayPort headset) if possible; the shifting events cause brief geometry reloads that wireless streaming can stutter on.
- Set your headset refresh rate to **90Hz** as a baseline. Drop to 72Hz only if you see reprojection during bell events.
- Enable **seated or room-scale** based on your play space—the demo's puzzles involve reaching and turning, so at least 2m x 2m is ideal.
- Calibrate your microphone in SteamVR settings before joining a lobby. The Guardians react to voice chat, so a hot mic is a gameplay liability, not just an annoyance.

### 4. Co-Op Lobby Setup (Up to 2 Friends)
1. Host launches the demo and selects **Multiplayer**.
2. Invite via Steam friends list—the demo uses Steam's native invite system.
3. Everyone should test voice chat in the lobby before entering the mansion.
4. Agree on a **callout system** before the first bell: e.g., "north hallway," "puzzle room," "guardian close." Since rooms move, absolute directions fail—use landmark names instead.

## Common Issues, Workarounds & What You Should Know

Because the demo is fresh, expect rough edges. Here's what to check first.

- **VR stutter during bell events:** Lower shadow quality and disable motion smoothing. The room-shift reload is the heaviest moment in the demo.
- **Mic not transmitting in co-op:** Check Windows privacy settings (Settings > Privacy > Microphone) and ensure Steam voice is set to the correct input device.
- **Friends can't join the lobby:** Confirm all players are on the same demo build version; mismatched versions block invites.
- **Motion sickness in VR:** Use snap turning, not smooth turning, and take breaks between shifts. The reorientation after a bell is the most disorienting moment.
- **Guardians feel "unfair":** They're audio-driven and adapting. Whisper-call or use push-to-talk if your group keeps getting found. Crouch-walking reduces noise.

**What you should know:** The demo covers **one mansion floor**, so don't expect a full campaign. Treat it as a systems test—shifting rooms, puzzle logic, and guardian AI—not a content demo. Memorization is intentionally useless; your group's communication is the real skill ceiling.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the Shifting Chambers demo playable in VR and on PC at the same time?
Yes. The demo supports both PC and VR, and cross-play between flat-screen and VR players is part of the co-op design. Mixed lobbies are the intended experience.

### How many players can join the Shifting Chambers demo co-op?
Up to **3 players total**—you plus two friends. The lobby is small by design so communication stays tight.

### Do the Manor Guardians actually hear voice chat?
Yes. The guardians are described as intelligent and adapting, and they respond to sound. Keep voice chat controlled or use push-to-talk to avoid giving away your position.

## Final Verdict

Download the **Shifting Chambers demo** now if you have a co-op group ready—it's free, short, and the shifting-room mechanic is genuinely novel. But do the setup work first: headphones on, mic calibrated, VR refresh rate locked, and a callout system agreed before the first bell. If you're playing solo, treat the demo as a mechanics preview rather than a full horror campaign. The mansion guardians are the star—respect the audio, and you'll escape. Ignore it, and the mansion keeps you.
