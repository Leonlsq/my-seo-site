## Step-by-Step: Best Graphics Settings for the Mythical Whalers Demo

Start by launching the demo, opening the **Options > Graphics** menu, and applying these baseline settings. These are tuned for a balance of visual fidelity and frame stability on a mid-tier GPU (think RTX 3060 / RX 6600 class):

| Setting | Recommended Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | Native (1080p/1440p) | Upscaling can smear the stylized art |
| V-Sync | Off (use driver-level) | Reduces input lag; cap FPS instead |
| Frame Rate Cap | 60 or monitor refresh | Prevents GPU overheating spikes |
| Shadows | Medium | Biggest FPS cost, smallest visual gain |
| Anti-Aliasing | FXAA or TAA | TAA smooths edges on stylized models |
| Texture Quality | High (if VRAM ≥ 6GB) | Cheap performance cost, big visual win |
| Ambient Occlusion | Off/Low | Heavy cost in dense water scenes |
| Water/Reflections | Medium | Water-heavy levels tank FPS at High |
| Motion Blur | Off | Personal preference; costs frames |
| FOV | 90–100 | Wider view helps in open-water combat |

### Step-by-step optimization checklist

1. **Update your GPU drivers** — NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel all push game-ready drivers. A stale driver is the #1 cause of demo crashes.
2. **Verify game files** — In Steam, right-click *Mythical Whalers Demo* > Properties > Installed Files > **Verify integrity of game files**.
3. **Disable overlays** — Turn off Steam, Discord, and GeForce Experience overlays one at a time to isolate stutter sources.
4. **Set the demo to High Performance** — Windows Settings > System > Power > **Best Performance**.
5. **Cap your frame rate** — Use the in-game cap or your GPU control panel to match your monitor's refresh rate.
6. **Close background apps** — Browsers and launchers eat RAM; the demo is memory-sensitive during level loads.

## Common Issues and Workarounds in the Demo

Even with tuned settings, demo builds have quirks. Here's what players are reporting and how to fix each:

- **Stuttering on first load:** Shader compilation. Play through the first level once, then restart—stutters usually vanish on the second run.
- **Black screen on launch:** Force windowed mode via the Steam launch option `-windowed`, then switch back to fullscreen in-game.
- **Demo won't launch / instant crash:** Verify files, update drivers, and temporarily disable antivirus real-time scanning for the demo folder.
- **Low FPS in water zones:** Drop Water/Reflections to Medium and disable Ambient Occlusion—these two settings dominate open-water scenes.
- **Controller not detected:** Enable Steam Input under the game's Properties > Controller settings.

### Why is the Mythical Whalers demo stuttering?

### Will my demo progress carry over to the December 2026 release?
