## Common Issues, Workarounds, and What You Should Know

**"The wishlist button is greyed out or won't click."** This is almost always a browser session issue. Clear Steam cookies, disable ad-blockers for `store.steampowered.com`, or switch to the Steam desktop client — the client's wishlist button is the most reliable.

**"I wishlisted it but got no launch email."** Steam only sends release emails to accounts with a **verified email address**. Check **Account Details → Contact Info** and confirm your email is verified, not just entered. Also check your spam folder — Steam launch notifications frequently land there.

**"Is there a demo or playtest?"** Pre-release pages sometimes offer a **"Request Access"** button for playtests. If it's present, click it — access is granted in waves and doesn't cost anything. If it's absent, there's nothing to download yet; any site offering a "HEAVY AS STONE download" before launch is malicious.

**"Can I pre-order?"** Only if the store page shows a pre-purchase option. If it doesn't, there is no legitimate way to buy early — don't pay resellers.

**Spec-checking before you commit:** Since PC requirements are often "TBD" pre-launch, use the **Steam Deck compatibility badge** and the store page's system requirements section as your baseline. If you're planning a build or upgrade, wait for confirmed specs rather than guessing — indie titles vary wildly between a 4 GB and 16 GB RAM requirement.
