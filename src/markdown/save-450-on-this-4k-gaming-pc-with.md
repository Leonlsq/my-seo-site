### Step-by-Step: How to Vet This Prebuilt Before You Buy

1. **Confirm the PSU.** Look for an 80+ Gold unit at **850W minimum**. A 5070 Ti + 9800X3D combo can spike past 500W under load; a no-name 650W unit is a red flag.
2. **Check RAM speed and channel config.** You want **2×16GB DDR5-6000 CL30** in dual-channel — not a single 32GB stick. Single-channel DDR5 can cost you 10–15% in games.
3. **Verify the GPU cooler.** Blower-style or two-fan compact coolers run hot and loud. Prefer a triple-fan AIB card.
4. **Ask about the SSD tier.** A Gen4 drive (e.g., 7,000MB/s class) is fine; a DRAM-less budget drive is a downgrade worth negotiating.
5. **Confirm the return window.** Newegg ABS systems typically carry a limited warranty — check the return policy before it ships.
