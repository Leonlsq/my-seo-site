### How to Actually Use an AI Law Firm (Step-by-Step)

1. **Scope the work first.** Separate your legal needs into "routine" (NDAs, SaaS terms, employment templates) and "high-stakes" (financing, IP disputes, acquisitions). Only route routine work to an AI firm.
2. **Request a fixed-fee quote.** Ask for a flat price per document type, not an hourly estimate. This is where AI firms win — demand it in writing.
3. **Verify human review is included.** Confirm a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction signs off. "AI-only" output is a liability, not a bargain.
4. **Run a pilot on 3–5 low-risk contracts.** Compare the AI firm's redlines against your current counsel's output before committing.
5. **Set a data-handling clause.** Ensure your contracts aren't used to train models and that confidentiality survives termination.
6. **Keep a Big Law relationship on retainer** for escalation. Hybrid models (AI for volume, traditional firm for complexity) are now standard practice.

## What You Should Know Before Switching

- **The Weil Gotshal situation is a warning, not a template.** A famed firm's collapse doesn't mean every Big Law firm is doomed — it means **cost structures are under pressure**. Clients are demanding efficiency, and AI firms are capitalizing on that gap.
- **AI legal tools still hallucinate.** Independent evaluations of legal LLMs repeatedly find fabricated case citations and misread clauses. Always require attorney sign-off.
- **Jurisdiction matters.** An AI trained on Delaware corporate law may miss state-specific employment rules. Confirm coverage for your state or country.
- **Insurance and malpractice.** Ask whether the AI firm carries professional liability coverage equivalent to a traditional firm. Many newer entrants do; some don't.
- **Cost reality check.** A $5,000 fixed-fee contract package sounds cheap until you need rework. Budget 10–20% for human review and revisions.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is an AI law firm like Crosby cheaper than Weil Gotshal?
Yes, dramatically — for routine work. AI-assisted firms typically charge fixed fees in the hundreds-to-low-thousands range per contract, while Weil-tier firms bill $600–$1,200+ per partner hour. For complex litigation or M&A, traditional firms still often deliver better outcomes despite higher cost.

### Can I use an AI law firm for contracts and still be legally protected?
Only if a licensed attorney reviews and signs off on the final output. AI-generated legal documents without human review carry real risk of unenforceable or jurisdiction-wrong clauses. Always confirm human oversight and malpractice coverage before signing.

### What happened to Weil Gotshal, and why is an AI firm bidding on it?
Reports describe Weil, Gotshal & Manges as facing a serious downturn, with AI legal provider Crosby entering a bid — a real-life "Suits"-style storyline. The takeaway for clients: legal cost structures are shifting fast, and hybrid AI-plus-attorney models are becoming mainstream.
