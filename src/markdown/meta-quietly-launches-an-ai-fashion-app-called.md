## Meta Style Sketch Setup: Turn Your Instagram Into a Virtual Closet

Meta quietly launched **Style Sketch**, an AI fashion app that builds a virtual closet from photos you've already posted to Instagram — or from new snaps, short videos, and camera-roll uploads. The promise is simple: your existing grid becomes a searchable wardrobe, and the AI suggests outfits from what you actually own. This guide walks you through the full setup in under 15 minutes, covers the exact upload methods, and fixes the connection and scanning errors most new users hit on day one.

The key takeaway: **connect Instagram first, then manually add your 10–15 most-worn items.** Relying on Instagram alone gives you a partial closet, because the app only reads what's visible in your photos.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Style Sketch and Your Virtual Closet

### Step 1 — Install and sign in
1. Download **Style Sketch** from the App Store or Google Play (it's a standalone Meta app, not a tab inside Instagram).
2. Sign in with the **Facebook or Instagram account** you want linked. Use the account whose photo history you actually want scanned — switching later means re-importing.

### Step 2 — Connect Instagram (the import engine)
1. Tap **Connect Instagram** on the onboarding screen.
2. Approve the permission prompt. Style Sketch reads your **public and private posts** but does not post on your behalf.
3. Choose **Full History** or **Last 12 Months**. Full History takes longer but catches older outfits.
4. Wait for the AI to tag garments. Expect roughly **1–3 minutes per 50 photos** on a mid-range phone.

### Step 3 — Add items manually (the accuracy boost)
Instagram import misses flat-lays, items you've never posted, and anything worn before your account existed. Use all three manual methods:

| Method | Best For | Speed | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Snap a photo** | Single items on a hanger or flat surface | ~5 sec/item | High |
| **Quick video** | Full-body outfits, showing fit and drape | ~15 sec/item | Highest (captures multiple angles) |
| **Camera roll upload** | Store receipts, screenshots, old photos | ~3 sec/item | Medium |

**Pro tip:** Shoot against a plain wall with even lighting. Style Sketch's segmentation struggles with busy backgrounds and patterned rugs — it may split one dress into two "items."

### Step 4 — Review and correct the AI tags
1. Open **Closet → Review Queue**.
2. Confirm category (top, bottom, outerwear, shoes, accessory), color, and season.
3. Fix mislabels now — a jacket tagged as a "dress" will poison every outfit suggestion.

### Step 5 — Generate outfits
Tap **Style Me** and set a context: weather, occasion (work, casual, date), and dress code. The AI pulls only from items in your closet, so the more you've added, the better the output.

## Common Issues and What You Should Know

- **Instagram won't connect:** Revoke Style Sketch in Instagram → *Settings → Apps and Websites*, then reconnect. Accounts with two-factor auth sometimes need a fresh login.
- **Photos imported but no items detected:** The AI skips group photos, mirror selfies with heavy occlusion, and heavily filtered images. Add those pieces manually.
- **Duplicate items after import:** Merge them in the Review Queue — the app doesn't auto-dedupe the same jacket across five posts.
- **Privacy:** Style Sketch processes images to build your closet; review the in-app data controls to limit what's stored. You can disconnect Instagram at any time without deleting manually added items.
- **No shopping integration yet:** Unlike some rivals, Style Sketch doesn't push purchases — it's a styling tool, not a storefront.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does Meta Style Sketch post to my Instagram?
No. The Instagram connection is read-only for building your closet; it does not publish, like, or comment on your behalf.

### Can I use Style Sketch without Instagram?
Yes. You can skip the Instagram step entirely and build a closet using photo snaps, quick videos, and camera-roll uploads.

### Why are some of my clothes missing after the Instagram import?
The AI only detects garments clearly visible in photos. Items never posted, worn in group shots, or obscured in mirror selfies must be added manually.

### Is Style Sketch free?
It's a free standalone app from Meta. Expect account-based sign-in and standard Meta data terms rather than a subscription paywall.

## Final Verdict

Style Sketch is worth installing **if you already post outfits to Instagram** — the import saves real time and the outfit generator is genuinely useful once your closet is accurate. Don't trust the auto-import alone: spend 10 minutes adding your core 15 items manually and correcting tags, and the suggestions improve dramatically. If you never post fashion content, skip the Instagram link and treat it as a manual wardrobe app instead.
