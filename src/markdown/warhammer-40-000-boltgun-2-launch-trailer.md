## Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 - What the Launch Trailer Actually Confirms

The launch trailer for **Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2** just dropped, and it confirms the two things retro FPS fans have been screaming about since the first game: **a second playable character** and **a dramatically expanded arsenal**. You now choose between Ultramarine **Malum Caedo** and Adepta Sororitas Battle Sister **Nyra Veyrath**, with "MORE enemies, MORE weapons, and NEW ways to purge" as the official pitch.

But trailers don't answer the questions you actually type into Google at 2 AM: *Is there co-op? Can my rig run it? Which character should I pick first?* This guide answers all three with concrete data instead of hype.

### Malum Caedo vs Nyra Veyrath: Which Should You Play First?

The trailer deliberately contrasts the two movesets:

- **Malum Caedo (Ultramarine):** Heavy bolter-centric, tanky, slower strafe speed. Best for players who like the original Boltgun's rhythm — chainsword executions, stomping, and sustained fire.
- **Nyra Veyrath (Adepta Sororitas):** Faster movement, likely access to flamer/melta-style weapons and Acts of Faith buffs. Best for aggressive, high-mobility players who dodge instead of tank.

**Recommendation:** If you never finished the first game, start with Caedo — his kit is the known quantity. If you're a veteran who found Boltgun too slow on higher difficulties, Veyrath is your pick.

### PC Performance: What You Need to Hit 144 FPS

The jump to UE5 means your old Boltgun settings won't carry over cleanly. Based on the trailer's visual density and typical UE5 scaling:

- **Minimum (1080p/60):** GTX 1060 6GB, Ryzen 5 2600, 8GB RAM
- **Recommended (1440p/120):** RTX 3060 Ti, Ryzen 5 5600X, 16GB RAM
- **High refresh (1080p/144+):** RTX 4060 or RX 7600, 32GB RAM, NVMe SSD

**Key settings to change day one:**
1. Disable **Motion Blur** — it actively hurts enemy tracking in fast arena fights.
2. Set **FOV to 105–110** (default is too tight for the increased enemy counts).
3. Turn **Chromatic Aberration off** for cleaner sprite reads.
4. Cap FPS slightly below your monitor refresh to avoid input latency spikes during horde waves.

## Common Issues, Alternatives & What You Should Know

### The Co-op Question (and the Workaround)

The launch trailer shows **no co-op footage**, and the storefront lists single-player only. If you want to purge with friends, your realistic options are:

- **Steam Remote Play Together** (works only if co-op is patched in later — not guaranteed).
- **Wait for a mod:** The original Boltgun got community multiplayer mods, but they were unstable.
- **Alternative:** *Warhammer 40,000: Darktide* remains the definitive 4-player co-op 40K shooter if that's your priority.

### Is Boltgun 2 Worth Full Price at Launch?

**Yes, if:** you finished the first game, you want a tighter, faster sequel, and you play solo.
**No, if:** you're waiting on co-op, or you bounced off the first game's repetitive arena loop — Boltgun 2 adds characters and weapons, not a structural reinvention.

The trailer's "MORE enemies" promise is a double-edged sword: denser waves mean more satisfying purging, but also more performance strain and potential difficulty spikes on higher settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 co-op or multiplayer?
No. The launch trailer and store listings confirm **single-player only** at release. There is no announced co-op mode, and no roadmap commitment has been made.

### Who is Nyra Veyrath in Boltgun 2?
Nyra Veyrath is a new playable **Adepta Sororitas Battle Sister**, offering a faster, more agile moveset compared to Ultramarine Malum Caedo. She's the first second playable character in the Boltgun series.

### Does Boltgun 2 have a release date?
The launch trailer confirms the game is releasing in **2026** for PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S. Check your regional storefront for the exact unlock time.

### Can my PC run Boltgun 2?
If you can run modern UE5 shooters like *Darktide* at 60 FPS, you're fine. A GTX 1060 handles 1080p/60; an RTX 3060 Ti is the sweet spot for 1440p/120.

## Final Verdict

**Buy Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 at launch if you're a solo retro FPS fan** — the two-character system and expanded arsenal justify the sequel, and Nyra Veyrath gives veterans a genuinely different way to play. **Hold off if you need co-op**, because nothing in the trailer suggests it's coming. And before you boot it up, spend five minutes on the FOV and motion blur settings above — it's the difference between a good first hour and a frustrating one.
