## Instagram Just Became a YouTube Rival: Here's How to Actually Upload a 90-Minute Video

Instagram has flipped the script. After years of pushing Reels and short-form video, the platform now supports uploads of up to **90 minutes** — a direct shot at YouTube's long-form dominance. If you've heard the news and your first thought was "wait, how do I actually post something that long?", this guide is for you. Below you'll find the exact upload steps, supported file specs, the current platform limits that still trip people up, and a straight comparison against YouTube so you know where to publish your next long video.

The short version: long-form video uploads are rolling out to eligible accounts (Creator and Business accounts first, with personal accounts following), and you post them through the standard Reels/Video upload flow — but the file must meet specific codec, resolution, and duration rules or the upload will silently fail or get rejected.

## How to Upload a 90-Minute Video to Instagram: Step-by-Step

Before you start, confirm your app is current — long-form uploads require a recent version of the Instagram app (check the App Store or Play Store for updates) and, on some accounts, access is gated behind Creator/Business status.

1. **Update the app** to the latest version on iOS or Android. Older builds cap uploads at the previous limits and won't show the extended duration option.
2. **Switch to a Creator or Business account** if you haven't already (Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account). Long-form access is being prioritized for professional accounts.
3. **Prepare your file** to the specs in the table below. This is where most failed uploads originate.
4. **Tap the + (Create) button** in the app and select your video from the camera roll or gallery.
5. **Trim and confirm duration.** If your clip exceeds the limit, the editor will prompt you to trim. For a 90-minute upload, keep the file under the hard cap and avoid re-encoding on export.
6. **Add a cover image and caption.** Long videos benefit from a custom thumbnail — tap "Cover" and select a frame or upload an image.
7. **Toggle "Share to Feed"** if you want the video to appear on your profile grid rather than only in the Reels/Video tab.
8. **Upload over Wi-Fi.** A 90-minute 1080p file can run 2–8 GB; mobile data uploads are slow and prone to timeout errors.
9. **Wait for processing.** Long files take several minutes to process. Don't close the app — backgrounding it mid-upload is the #1 cause of "upload failed" errors.

### Supported Specs for Long-Form Instagram Video

| Spec | Recommended Value |
|---|---|
| Max duration | 90 minutes |
| Resolution | 1080p (1920×1080) |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 (landscape) or 9:16 (vertical) |
| Frame rate | 30 fps (60 fps supported) |
| Video codec | H.264 |
| Audio codec | AAC, 128 kbps+ |
| Container | MP4 or MOV |
| Max file size | Keep under ~4 GB where possible |

## Common Upload Failures and How to Fix Them

Even with the new limit, users are hitting predictable walls. Here's the troubleshooting checklist:

- **"Video too long" error despite a 90-minute cap:** Your account may not have the feature rolled out yet. Feature flags roll out gradually by region and account type — try again in 24–48 hours, or test with a shorter 20–30 minute file to confirm access.
- **Upload stalls at 90%:** Almost always a file-size or connection issue. Compress with HandBrake (H.264, CRF 23, 1080p) to shrink the file, then re-upload on Wi-Fi.
- **Video looks blurry after posting:** Instagram re-encodes everything. Export at 1080p with a high bitrate (8–12 Mbps) and avoid uploading already-compressed files pulled from other platforms.
- **Audio out of sync:** Caused by variable frame rate (VFR) exports from phone screen recordings. Re-encode to constant frame rate (CFR) before uploading.
- **No "long video" option at all:** Log out and back in to refresh the feature flag, and confirm you're on the newest app build.

### Instagram vs YouTube for Long-Form Video

| Feature | Instagram (90-min) | YouTube |
|---|---|---|
| Max length | 90 minutes | 12 hours (verified) |
| Monetization | Limited (bonuses, gifts) | Mature AdSense program |
| Discovery | Feed + Reels + Explore | Search + suggested |
| Best for | Existing follower engagement | Search-driven evergreen content |
| Analytics depth | Basic | Advanced (retention curves, CTR) |

The practical takeaway: Instagram long-form is a **retention and community play**, not a search play. YouTube still wins for discoverability and ad revenue. Smart creators will post the full video to YouTube and cut 60–90 second hooks for Instagram to funnel viewers.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I upload a 90-minute video to Instagram from my phone?
Yes — the upload happens through the in-app Create flow, but large files are best transferred and uploaded over Wi-Fi. For files over 4 GB, compress to 1080p H.264 first to avoid timeouts.

### Does Instagram pay creators for 90-minute videos?
Not through a YouTube-style ad program. Monetization remains limited to creator bonuses, gifts, and brand deals. Long-form is currently a reach and engagement tool rather than a direct revenue stream.

### Why don't I see the 90-minute upload option yet?
The feature is rolling out gradually and is prioritized for Creator and Business accounts. Update your app, confirm your account type, and retry within 24–48 hours.

## Final Verdict

Instagram's 90-minute video support is a genuine strategic shift, but it's not a YouTube replacement — yet. **If you already have an engaged Instagram following**, start testing long-form now: upload one 15–30 minute video, watch your retention and saves, and scale up. **If you're building an audience from scratch or rely on ad revenue**, keep YouTube as your primary long-form home and use Instagram for clips that funnel viewers. Either way, get your export settings right before your first upload — the codec and file-size rules above will save you hours of failed attempts.
