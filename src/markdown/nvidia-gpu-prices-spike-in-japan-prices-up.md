## How to Import a GPU From Amazon US to Japan (Step-by-Step)

If the math favors importing, follow this exact process to avoid surprises:

1. **Check Amazon US "Ships to Japan" status.** Not all third-party sellers ship internationally. Filter by "Amazon Global" or sold-by-Amazon listings.
2. **Calculate landed cost before checkout.** Use Amazon's import fees deposit estimator. It typically covers duties and consumption tax upfront.
3. **Verify voltage and power connectors.** US and Japan both use 100–120V, so PSU compatibility is fine. But confirm the card uses standard 12V-2x6 or 8-pin PCIe — no region locking exists on GPUs.
4. **Budget for the 10% consumption tax.** Amazon Global usually collects this at checkout. If you use a forwarding service (Tenso, Buyee), you'll pay it on delivery instead.
5. **Expect 1–3 weeks shipping.** Air freight for a 2–3kg GPU runs $40–80 via forwarding services.
6. **Register the warranty carefully.** Most Nvidia AIB warranties (ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte) are region-locked. EVGA-style global warranties are largely gone.

## Common Issues, Hidden Costs, and Alternatives

### The Warranty Trap
This is the single biggest reason NOT to import. If your RTX 5080 dies in month 8, you'll be shipping it back to the US at your own cost — often ¥15,000–25,000 round trip — and waiting 4–8 weeks. Japanese domestic purchases give you local RMA through the retailer.

### Forwarding Service Fees
If you use Tenso or Buyee instead of Amazon Global:
- Service fee: ¥500–1,000 per package
- Domestic US shipping to warehouse: $10–20
- International shipping: $50–90 for a GPU
- Customs duty: typically 0% on computer parts, but 10% consumption tax applies

### Better Alternatives to Consider
- **Wait for the next restock cycle.** Japanese retailers often reprice downward when supply stabilizes. Historically, 3–6 months after a spike, prices settle 8–15% lower.
- **Buy used domestically.** Mercari and Yahoo Auctions Japan list RTX 40-series cards at 20–30% below new 50-series equivalents — often better value per frame.
- **Consider the RTX 4070 Super or 4080 Super** if you can find them new. They're often cheaper than spiked 50-series equivalents with similar raster performance.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Why did Nvidia GPU prices spike in Japan specifically?
The spike is driven by yen weakness against the dollar, increased import costs, and a Japanese retailer adjusting margins after supply tightened. It's not an Nvidia-mandated price increase — it's a regional retail response to currency and logistics pressure.

### Is it cheaper to import an RTX 5090 from Amazon US to Japan?
Yes, marginally — roughly ¥50,000 cheaper even after shipping and tax. But you sacrifice local warranty coverage, which on a ¥400,000 card is a significant risk. Only import if you're comfortable with self-insuring.

### Will Japanese GPU prices go back down in 2026?
Historically, post-spike prices settle within 3–6 months if the yen stabilizes and supply recovers. Watch the USD/JPY rate — if it drops below 145, Japanese prices typically follow within weeks.

### Do I pay customs duty on a GPU imported to Japan?
Computer graphics cards generally carry a 0% tariff in Japan, but you still pay 10% consumption tax on the declared value plus shipping. Amazon Global collects this upfront; forwarding services collect on delivery.
