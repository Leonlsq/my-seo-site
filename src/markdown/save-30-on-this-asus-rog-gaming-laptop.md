## How to Get the Most Out of an RTX 5060 Laptop

If you buy it, these five steps unlock the performance you paid for:

1. **Enable MUX Switch / Discrete GPU mode.** In Armoury Crate, switch the GPU mode to "Ultimate" or "Discrete." This bypasses the integrated GPU and typically adds **8–15% more FPS** in CPU-bound titles. Reboot required.
2. **Set a custom fan curve.** The default "Performance" profile throttles the GPU to ~90W. A manual curve holding 100–115W keeps clocks higher but is louder — use headphones.
3. **Cap VRAM-heavy settings.** In-game, set Textures to **High, not Ultra**. This is the #1 fix for 8GB stutter. Drop Ray Tracing to Medium or off in VRAM-hungry titles.
4. **Undervolt the CPU.** A -80mV to -120mV offset in ThrottleStop or Armoury Crate lowers temps 5–10°C, giving the GPU more thermal budget.
5. **Use DLSS 4 + Frame Generation.** On RTX 50-series, DLSS 4 with multi-frame generation can triple perceived FPS in supported titles. Enable it in any game that offers it.

Expected real-world FPS at 2560x1600, high settings:

- *Valorant / CS2*: 300+ FPS (esports, trivially maxed)
- *Fortnite* (DLSS Quality): 120–160 FPS
- *Cyberpunk 2077* (High, no RT): 70–90 FPS
- *Black Myth: Wukong* (High, DLSS): 60–80 FPS

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

**Thermal throttling is the hidden catch.** A "hefty" 16-inch ROG has better cooling than a thin-and-light, but sustained loads still push CPU+GPU into the 85–95°C range. Repaste after 18–24 months if you're a heavy user.

**The 8GB VRAM problem is real in 2026.** Newer titles increasingly list 12GB as recommended at 1440p. The RTX 5060 will age faster than a 12GB card. If you keep laptops 4+ years, this matters.

**Who should buy:** gamers who play esports and 1080p/1440p titles, want a single machine for work + play, and don't need maximum portability.

**Who should skip:** anyone gaming at 4K, VRAM-sensitive AAA players, or frequent travelers — 5.5 lbs plus a 240W brick is a backpack anchor.

Yes, for most titles at high settings with DLSS enabled. Expect 60–90 FPS in demanding AAA games. The 8GB VRAM is the limiting factor at ultra textures, not raw GPU power.

### Is $1,399 a good price for an RTX 5060 gaming laptop?

### Does the RTX 5060 laptop support DLSS 4 and Frame Generation?
Yes. RTX 50-series GPUs support DLSS 4, including multi-frame generation, which significantly boosts perceived smoothness in supported titles.

### How much RAM and storage should I get?
16GB dual-channel DDR5 is the minimum for gaming in 2026; 32GB is ideal if you multitask or stream. 1TB SSD is standard at this price — plan to add a second drive later.
