## Step-by-Step: Planetron Playtest Setup & Co-Op Strategy

### 1. Optimize for Procedural Moons (Settings That Actually Matter)
Procedurally generated terrain means your GPU load spikes when new chunks stream in during fast vehicle movement. Prioritize these settings:

| Setting | Recommended | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Draw distance / LOD | High | Low LOD causes pop-in on spherical horizons and breaks targeting |
| Shadows | Medium | Biggest FPS cost during bombing runs at low altitude |
| Anti-aliasing | TAA | Spherical geometry aliases badly; TAA smooths horizon edges |
| Motion blur | Off | Hurts target acquisition when driving the RAM crawler |
| VSync | Off + frame cap | Reduces input lag for FPS-style piloting |

### 2. Assign Co-Op Roles Before You Spawn
With 2-player co-op, the biggest mistake is both players flying. Split the workload:

- **Player 1 — Commander/Pilot:** Stays in the recon drone for an overview, issues fleet orders, and handles takeoff/landing (currently automatic).
- **Player 2 — Action/Driver:** Takes the RAM (anti-vehicle crawler) for ground pushes or flies bomb runs once AA is neutralized.
- **Swap trigger:** When Player 1 spots a cluster of AA, hand off drone control and let Player 2 do the strike.

### 3. The AA Suppression Sequence (Do This Before Bombing)
The unit AI dodges anti-air with flares, so bombing runs into live AA waste your aircraft. Sequence it correctly:
1. Send recon drone over the target zone to tag AA positions.
2. Order ground units or a first strike to destroy AA emplacements.
3. **Only then** commit bombers and close-air support.
4. Keep the drone overhead to re-tag any surviving AA that repositioned.

### 4. Use the RAM for Momentum, Not Sieges
The anti-vehicle crawler is your action fix, but it's not a tank. Drive it in short bursts, break line of sight on spherical terrain (hills curve away fast), and pair it with air cover. Never solo-push into a defended zone.

## What You Should Know Before the Playtest

- **Takeoff/landing is automatic** — don't fight the controls; learn the auto-approach timing so you're not caught mid-transition under fire.
- **Spherical maps change flanking** — there are no map edges to camp; enemies can come from "behind" the horizon. Keep recon up.
- **Fleet AI is your force multiplier** — micromanaging every unit is slower than Carrier Command 2's pace by design. Issue intent, then pilot one vehicle yourself.
- **Performance on procedural chunks** — if you stutter when crossing terrain seams, drop shadow quality first, not draw distance.
