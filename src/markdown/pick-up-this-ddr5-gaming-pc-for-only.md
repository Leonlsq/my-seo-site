**Bottom line:** this is a genuine 1080p/144Hz machine for competitive shooters and a 1080p/60 machine for AAA. It is *not* a 1440p card.

## Setup Checklist: Get It Running Right in 30 Minutes

1. **Verify the GPU on arrival.** Open Task Manager → Performance → GPU and confirm the model. Prebuilt SKUs sometimes substitute a weaker card.
2. **Enable XMP/EXPO in BIOS.** DDR5 often boots at 4800MT/s by default. Reboot into BIOS (Del/F2), enable EXPO (AMD) or XMP (Intel), and confirm your kit's rated speed.
3. **Check RAM is dual-channel.** Both sticks should be in slots A2/B2. Single-channel DDR5 can cost you 10–15% in games.
4. **Update GPU drivers** via NVIDIA App or GeForce Experience, and run Windows Update.
5. **Set your monitor to its max refresh rate** in Display Settings → Advanced Display.
6. **Run a 10-minute stress test** (Cinebench + a game) and watch temps in HWMonitor. CPU under 85°C and GPU under 80°C is healthy.
7. **Uninstall bloatware** (trial antivirus, RGB utilities you won't use) for a cleaner baseline.

## Common Issues, Upgrade Limits & Better Alternatives

**Watch out for these prebuilt pitfalls:**
- **PSU quality:** Bronze-rated no-name units are common. If you plan to upgrade the GPU later, budget $80–$100 for a quality 750W Gold unit.
- **Single-channel RAM mistakes:** Some budget SKUs ship one 16GB stick. Insist on 2x8GB.
- **AM5 vs LGA1700:** The AMD version (B650) lets you drop in future Ryzen chips on the same socket — a real long-term win. The Intel B760 platform is a dead end after 14th gen.
- **8GB VRAM ceiling:** Fine for 1080p today; expect to lower textures in 2027+ AAA titles.

**Alternatives worth comparing at ~$899:**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the $899 iBuyPower Slate good for 1440p gaming?
It can handle 1440p at Medium settings with DLSS in most titles, but the RTX 4060's 8GB VRAM and modest memory bandwidth make it a 1080p-first machine. For consistent 1440p/60+ at High, step up to an RTX 4070-class GPU.

### Can I upgrade the RAM and storage later?
Yes. DDR5 boards in this tier typically have four DIMM slots (two free), and the case supports additional 2.5" SATA drives plus an extra M.2 slot on most B650/B760 boards. Adding a second 16GB DDR5 kit to reach 32GB is the single best future-proofing upgrade.

### Is DDR5 actually better than DDR4 for gaming?
In pure gaming fps, DDR5-5600 vs DDR4-3600 is roughly a 3–8% difference — modest. The real reason to buy DDR5 here is platform longevity: AM5 and LGA1700 boards are DDR5-native, so you're not buying into a dead memory standard.

### Does the iBuyPower Slate come with a warranty?
Yes — iBuyPower typically includes a 1-year parts-and-labor warranty plus lifetime technical support. Keep the original packaging for RMAs and register the system on iBuyPower's site within 30 days.
