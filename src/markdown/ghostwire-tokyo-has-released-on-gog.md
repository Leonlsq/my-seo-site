## How to Transfer Your Steam Saves to the GOG Version

One of the biggest questions from players who already own the game elsewhere: **can you move your progress?** Yes, with a manual file copy.

1. Locate your Steam save folder: `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\userdata\[YourSteamID]\223750\remote\`
2. Copy all `.sav` files inside.
3. Open GOG Galaxy, right-click Ghostwire: Tokyo → **Manage installation** → **Show folder**.
4. Navigate to the save directory: `[GOG Install]\GOG Games\Ghostwire Tokyo\Saved\SaveGames\`
5. Paste the `.sav` files into that folder.
6. Launch the game and load your save from the main menu.

**Note:** Achievements do **not** transfer between storefronts. You will need to re-earn them on GOG Galaxy.

## Best Settings for a Stable 60 FPS on Mid-Range Hardware

The game's default "High" preset is overly aggressive on shadows and volumetric fog. Here is a tuned configuration for an RTX 3060 / RX 6600 XT targeting 1080p 60 FPS:

- **Resolution Scale:** 100%
- **Ray Tracing:** OFF (or "Low" if you have an RTX 40-series)
- **Shadows:** Medium
- **Volumetric Fog:** Low
- **Motion Blur:** Off (personal preference, but it costs frames)
- **DLSS/FSR:** Quality
- **Texture Quality:** High (VRAM permitting)
- **Screen Space Reflections:** Medium

This combination typically nets a **15–20% FPS gain** over the default High preset with almost no visible quality loss.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

- **GOG Galaxy not detecting the game:** Reinstall the GOG Galaxy client and run it as administrator. The offline installer (standalone `.exe`) bypasses Galaxy entirely and is the fastest way to play without a launcher.
- **Controller not recognized:** Enable Steam Input as a wrapper, or use DS4Windows for DualSense. GOG's native controller support is functional but less polished than Steam's.
- **Missing Denuvo:** Good news — the GOG version ships without Denuvo, which is why it is the preferred choice for preservationists and low-end rigs. Expect slightly faster load times than the Steam build on the same SSD.
- **Alternatives to consider:** If you want mods, buy on Steam. If you want the cheapest price and own nothing yet, wait for a GOG seasonal sale where it frequently drops to **$14.99**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Ghostwire: Tokyo on GOG DRM-free?
Yes. The GOG release is 100% DRM-free, meaning no online check, no launcher requirement, and no Denuvo. You can install it on multiple personal machines and play fully offline.

### Can I transfer my Steam save to the GOG version of Ghostwire: Tokyo?
Yes, via manual file copy. Locate the Steam `remote` save folder under your userdata directory, then paste the `.sav` files into the GOG install's `Saved\SaveGames\` folder. Achievements will not carry over.

### Does Ghostwire: Tokyo on GOG support Steam Deck?
It is rated **Playable**, not Verified. You will need to manually configure the controller layout and may need to lower settings to Medium to hold 40 FPS. It runs fine via Proton.

### Is the GOG version cheaper than Steam?
Base price is identical, but GOG's **30-day refund policy** and frequent DRM-free sales (as low as $14.99) make it the better long-term value for buyers who prioritize ownership.
