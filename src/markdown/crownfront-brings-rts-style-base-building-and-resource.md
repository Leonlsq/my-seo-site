## Crownfront Demo: Is This RTS-Turn-Based Hybrid Worth Playing?

Crownfront is a medieval strategy game that fuses two genres that rarely share a table: real-time-strategy base building and resource management, layered on top of turn-based tactical combat played out on a physical board-inspired battlefield. The free demo is live right now as part of Steam Next Fest, and the developer (u/KolaCape) is actively asking players whether the combat and turn-by-turn decisions actually feel engaging. The short version: if you've ever wished a turn-based tactics game let you *build* your economy the way an RTS does, Crownfront is the closest thing on the market right now. But there are real caveats — read on.

## How Crownfront Actually Plays: The Core Loop Explained

Crownfront splits its gameplay into two distinct phases, and understanding the rhythm between them is the key to enjoying the demo.

1. **Base Building & Resource Phase (RTS-style):** You construct and upgrade buildings, manage resource production chains, and expand your settlement. This is the "real-time strategy" DNA — think production queues and economy scaling rather than instant unit spam.
2. **Turn-Based Combat Phase:** When conflict arrives, play shifts to a grid/board battlefield where you command units turn by turn. Positioning, unit matchups, and action economy matter more than raw click speed.
3. **Decision Carryover:** Your economy choices feed your army. What you build during the resource phase directly determines what you can field in combat — this is the strategic tension the developer wants feedback on.

The "physical board-inspired" battlefield is the standout design choice. It reads visually like a tabletop wargame, which makes turn resolution easy to parse and rewards planning over reflexes.

### What to Test in the Demo (Developer's Own Focus Areas)

The developer explicitly wants feedback on two things. Structure your playtest around them:

- **Combat engagement:** Does each turn present a meaningful choice, or do you auto-pilot? Try deliberately losing a unit to see how punishing mistakes feel.
- **Turn-by-turn decision quality:** Track whether you ever feel like you're just clicking "end turn." If yes, that's the feedback worth posting.

## Common Issues & What You Should Know Before Downloading

- **It's a demo, not early access.** Don't expect save persistence or polished balance. Systems may change based on Next Fest feedback.
- **Performance on low-end hardware:** Turn-based games are typically forgiving, but base-building phases with many units can spike CPU load. Lower shadow and unit-detail settings first if you see stutter.
- **Genre expectations mismatch:** If you want fast RTS action (StarCraft-style), this is *not* that. The RTS layer is about economy, not real-time micro.
- **Wishlist before the demo ends.** Next Fest demos sometimes get delisted after the event — wishlisting secures your notification for launch and any demo updates.

### How Crownfront Compares to Similar Hybrids

- **vs. classic turn-based tactics (XCOM-likes):** Crownfront adds an economy layer those games lack — more strategic depth, but slower pacing.
- **vs. pure RTS (Age of Empires):** No real-time combat micro; the trade-off is deeper, more deliberate battles.
- **vs. board-game adaptations:** The board-inspired battlefield will feel familiar to tabletop wargamers, but with digital convenience.
