This isn't a one-off. SNL has been steadily sharpening its tech satire, and the Musk sketch is the latest in a line of impressions that treat CEOs as characters. Here's how the recent wave stacks up:

The through-line: tech executives have become the new political dynasties for late-night comedy, because they're famous enough to be household names but unscripted enough to generate endless material.
