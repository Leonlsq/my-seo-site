### Your 6-Step Pre-Launch Checklist

1. **Update GPU drivers now.** Don't wait for launch day — AMD and NVIDIA typically push game-ready drivers 24–48 hours before release. Enable auto-notifications.
2. **Verify SSD free space.** Narrative games with dense urban assets routinely balloon past 80 GB. Clear at least 120 GB on your fastest drive.
3. **Set a page file of 16–32 GB.** If you're on 16 GB RAM, a manual page file prevents stutter during scene transitions.
4. **Disable overlays.** Steam, Discord, and GeForce Experience overlays are the #1 cause of launch-window crashes in Unreal-based titles.
5. **Pre-configure your controller.** The trailer shows controller prompts — if you're on keyboard, plan a remap for the parry button (default bindings are usually cramped).
6. **Back up your save folder path.** Know where `%AppData%` saves live *before* you need them.

## Common Launch Issues & What You Should Know

New narrative releases almost always ship with a predictable set of problems. Here's what to watch for and how to get ahead of it:

- **Shader compilation stutter:** Expect a 5–15 minute "compiling shaders" screen on first boot. Do *not* alt-tab during this — it's the most common cause of corrupted shader caches.
- **Frame pacing on 60 Hz displays:** If the game targets 60 fps with V-Sync, cap your framerate to 60 externally (via NVIDIA Control Panel or AMD Adrenalin) to avoid judder.
- **Audio desync in cutscenes:** Historically tied to 5.1/7.1 surround configs. If dialogue drifts, switch to stereo in Windows sound settings as a quick test.
- **Alternatives while you wait:** If you want the same vibe, *Alan Wake 2* and *Judgment* scratch a similar itch and are fully optimized — good benchmarks to compare your rig against.
