## How to Verify Any "RuneScape Cancelled" Claim in 5 Steps

Before you panic-uninstall or refund anything, run this checklist. It takes about 90 seconds and saves you from misinformation.

1. **Check the official Jagex news page** (runescape.com/news and the OSRS homepage). Real cancellations get a formal blog post with a date and usually a dev statement.
2. **Cross-reference the OSRS and RS3 subreddits.** If a project is genuinely cancelled, you'll see multiple threads with developer replies — not a single post with a `[link] [comments]` stub.
3. **Look for a Jagex Moderator (J-Mod) comment.** J-Mods post under red/gold flairs. Their absence on a "cancellation" thread is a strong signal it's unofficial.
4. **Check the project's own Discord or GitHub.** Fan projects (HDOS plugins, Runelite forks, private servers) usually announce shutdowns there first.
5. **Search for a dated announcement.** "Cancelled" claims without a month/year are almost always recycled or misread.

If a project fails steps 1–3, treat it as **unconfirmed community chatter** until proven otherwise.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

Here's the information most news articles skip:

- **Private server risk is real.** If "Reignited" was a private server, its shutdown is a reminder that unofficial servers can vanish overnight. Never reuse your official Jagex password, and never enter payment info.
- **Your official account is safe.** A fan project being cancelled has **zero impact** on your OSRS or RS3 account, membership, or bank. Don't fall for "account migration" phishing links.
- **Watch for scam ads.** After any cancellation news, fake "Reignited relaunch" download pages spike. Only download clients from runescape.com, the official RuneLite site, or verified GitHub repos.
- **Brighter Shores is the sleeper pick.** Developed by Andrew Gower (RuneScape's original creator), it's the most legitimate "spiritual successor" if you want something genuinely new rather than a remake.
- **Cost math:** OSRS membership is ~$12.49/month or ~$99.99/year. A year of OSRS costs less than most private server "donation tiers" and comes with anti-cheat, customer support, and no shutdown risk.

If you were part of a Discord community around the cancelled project, **export your screenshots and save your friends list now** — those servers often go read-only or get deleted within days.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is RuneScape Reignited actually cancelled by Jagex?
No official Jagex announcement confirms a "RuneScape Reignited" cancellation. The claim traces back to a community Reddit post (u/Gorotheninja), not a Jagex press release. Treat it as an unofficial or fan-project situation until Jagex posts something formal.

### What is the best free alternative to RuneScape Reignited?
**Old School RuneScape's free-to-play mode** is the best zero-cost option, and pairing it with the free **RuneLite + HDOS plugin** gives you upgraded visuals. If you want a brand-new game, try **Brighter Shores**, which has a free tier.

### Will the Reignited cancellation affect my OSRS account?
No. Fan projects, mods, and private servers are completely separate from Jagex's official servers. Your OSRS and RS3 accounts, membership, and items are unaffected.

### Are private servers safe after a cancellation like this?
Generally no. Private servers operate outside Jagex's Terms of Service, can shut down without warning, and are a common vector for credential theft. Avoid reusing passwords and never submit payment details.
