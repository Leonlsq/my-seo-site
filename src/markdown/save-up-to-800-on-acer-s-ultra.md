### Step-by-Step: Setting Up Your Helios Neo 16S AI for Peak Performance

1. **Update the BIOS and GPU drivers first.** Run Acer's PredatorSense updater, then install the latest Nvidia Game Ready driver directly from Nvidia's site — not the OEM build.
2. **Enable the discrete GPU mode.** In PredatorSense, switch to "Discrete GPU only" (MUX switch) for maximum frame rates; use "Optimus/Hybrid" when on battery.
3. **Calibrate the OLED.** Set the 240 Hz refresh rate in Windows Display settings, enable HDR, and run Windows' HDR calibration tool to avoid washed-out QHD+ output.
4. **Set a custom fan curve.** The Core Ultra 9 runs hot under sustained loads; a slightly aggressive curve keeps clocks stable without maxing noise.
5. **Cap frame rates below 240 fps** in esports titles to reduce coil whine and heat, letting the OLED's response time do the work.

## What You Should Know Before Buying

- **OLED burn-in risk:** Static UI elements (taskbars, HUDs) on a 240 Hz OLED demand screen-shift and pixel-refresh features — enable them in PredatorSense.
- **Battery life reality:** A 24-core CPU plus a 5070 Ti means expect **4–5 hours** of light use, not all-day endurance.
- **Alternatives at $2,400:** Comparable RTX 5070 Ti laptops from Lenovo (Legion Pro) and ASUS (ROG Strix) often ship with 32GB standard but rarely match this OLED panel's 240 Hz QHD+ spec.
- **Deal timing:** Best Buy's $800 discount is promotional; verify the price at checkout, as base configs can fluctuate weekly.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is 32GB RAM overkill for gaming on an RTX 5070 Ti laptop?
For pure single-title gaming at QHD+, 16GB is usually adequate today. But with streaming, heavy multitasking, or any AI/creative work, 32GB prevents stutter and future-proofs a laptop you'll likely keep 3–4 years.

### Can I upgrade the 16GB model to 32GB myself?
Yes — the Helios Neo 16S AI uses SODIMM slots, but opening the chassis may affect warranty depending on region. Given the $175 delta, buying the 32GB model upfront is simpler and often cheaper than a retail RAM kit.

### Is the 240 Hz QHD+ OLED worth it over a 165 Hz IPS panel?
Absolutely for competitive and mixed use. OLED delivers near-instant response and true blacks; the 240 Hz refresh shines in fast-paced shooters. Just enable burn-in mitigation settings.
