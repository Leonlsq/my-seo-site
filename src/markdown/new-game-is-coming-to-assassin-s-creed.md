## AC Valhalla New Game+ Is Finally Here: What Actually Carries Over?

After years of community requests, New Game+ has arrived in *Assassin's Creed Valhalla*, and the first question every returning player asks is the same: **what carries over, and what gets wiped?** The short answer: your gear, skills, and wealth transfer, but the story, settlement upgrades, and world progress reset. This guide breaks down the exact carry-over rules, the best loadout to start your second run, and the difficulty settings that make NG+ actually worth playing. If you're planning a fresh conquest of England, read this before you hit "New Game+."

## What Carries Over in AC Valhalla New Game+ (Full Breakdown)

Ubisoft's NG+ implementation is generous with progression but strict with world state. Here's the exact breakdown based on the patch notes and community testing.

| Category | Carries Over? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gear & weapons (all rarities) | ✅ Yes | Includes fully upgraded and rune-slotted items |
| Runes & weapon upgrades | ✅ Yes | All runes stay in inventory |
| Skill tree progress | ✅ Yes | All acquired skills and skill points retained |
| Player level & XP | ✅ Yes | Level and XP carry over |
| Wealth (silver, opals, resources) | ✅ Yes | Silver, opals, and crafting materials transfer |
| Settlement level | ❌ No | Ravensthorpe resets to Level 1 |
| Story progress | ❌ No | Full reset — replay from the prologue |
| World map (fast travel, wealth) | ❌ No | All collectibles and viewpoints reset |
| Abilities & books of knowledge | ⚠️ Partial | Abilities retained; world pickups respawn |
| Order of the Ancients progress | ❌ No | Full reset |

**The key takeaway:** You keep your power fantasy (gear, skills, level) but lose your map completion. That means NG+ is about *replaying the story with your endgame build*, not re-grinding.

### Step-by-Step: How to Start New Game+ Correctly

1. **Finish the main story** — NG+ unlocks only after completing the final quest "The Last Chapter" (or the base-game ending, depending on your version).
2. **Back up your save** — Manually copy your save to a USB or cloud slot. NG+ overwrites your current playthrough slot.
3. **Unequip nothing, but re-slot runes** — Runes carry over, but verify your weapon slots are filled before starting; the prologue strips temporary gear.
4. **Spend leftover skill points** — You keep them, but respeccing is free, so dump points into a build before the reset.
5. **Select "New Game+" from the main menu** — Choose your difficulty; you can change it later in settings.
6. **Skip the tutorial** — NG+ lets you bypass the Norway prologue intro cinematics after the first time.

## Best Starting Loadout for New Game+

Because your gear carries over, NG+ lets you trivialize early England — if you pick the right build. Here's the meta loadout for a fast, fun second run.

- **Weapon: Vordr's Bite (dagger) or Gungnir (spear)** — Fast attack speed plus the spear's reach makes early raids trivial. Both carry over fully upgraded.
- **Armor: Mentor's Set or Brigandine Set** — Mentor's boosts assassination damage; Brigandine favors raw defense for higher difficulties.
- **Runes: +Critical Chance and +Attack** — Stack crit runes on your primary weapon for burst damage.
- **Skills to prioritize:** *Stomp*, *Brush with Death*, and *Missile Reversal* — all retained and devastating in early-game fights.
- **Difficulty:** Start on **Drengr** (Hard) if you want a challenge; your carried-over level makes Aesir (Nightmare) viable but punishing.

## Common Issues & What You Should Know

**"My settlement reset — is that a bug?"** No. Ravensthorpe always resets in NG+. You'll need to rebuild buildings and re-recruit crew members. Plan for a few hours of settlement rebuilding.

**"Can I change difficulty mid-run?"** Yes, via Settings > Gameplay > Difficulty. There's no penalty for switching.

**"Do I lose my Jomsviking or ship cosmetics?"** Cosmetics and your Jomsviking carry over; ship upgrades reset with the settlement.

**"Is NG+ worth it over a fresh save?"** If you want to experience the story again with your favorite build, yes. If you want a true challenge, a fresh save on Aesir is harder. NG+ is a *power fantasy replay*, not a hardcore mode.

**Watch out for:** Ability pickups respawn in the world, so re-collecting them is redundant but harmless. Also, Order of the Ancients targets reset — you'll need to re-hunt them for the full completion.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does New Game+ in AC Valhalla carry over gear and level?
Yes. All gear, weapons, runes, skill points, player level, and wealth carry over. Your settlement, story progress, and world map reset completely.

### Can you start New Game+ before finishing the story?
No. You must complete the main story (including "The Last Chapter") to unlock New Game+. Back up your save before starting, since NG+ overwrites your active slot.

### Is there a level cap in AC Valhalla New Game+?
The level cap remains tied to the base game's power level system. NG+ doesn't raise the cap, but carried-over XP means you'll hit it faster on a second run.

### Does NG+ reset the Order of the Ancients and settlement?
Yes. Both reset fully. You'll rebuild Ravensthorpe and re-hunt all Order targets, though your combat power makes both much faster.

## Final Verdict

**Start New Game+ if you want to replay Valhalla's story with your endgame build — it's the definitive power-fantasy run.** Just back up your save first, expect your settlement to reset, and pick a difficulty that matches your appetite. If you crave a genuine challenge, a fresh Aesir save beats NG+ every time. For most returning players, NG+ on Drengr with a crit-dagger build is the sweet spot: fast, fun, and finally worth the wait.
