## Revolut Outage: How to Access Your Money Right Now

If you opened the Revolut app and got a spinning wheel, a "something went wrong" error, or a blank balance screen, you're not alone — a massive worldwide Revolut outage has disrupted payments, top-ups, card transactions, and account logins across multiple regions. The good news: **your money is not gone.** Revolut holds customer funds at licensed banks and segregated accounts, so an outage is an access problem, not a solvency problem. The bad news: if you rely on Revolut as your only account, you need a plan in the next 30 minutes. This guide gives you concrete workarounds, ranked by speed, plus what to do after service returns.

## Step-by-Step: What to Do When Revolut Is Down

Work through these in order. The first three take under five minutes.

### 1. Confirm it's a real outage (not your phone)
- Check **Downdetector** for a spike in Revolut reports.
- Check **Revolut's official X/Twitter status account** and their in-app status banner.
- Try logging in on **mobile data instead of Wi-Fi** (and vice versa). If both fail identically, it's server-side.
- Do **not** repeatedly force-close and reinstall the app — that won't fix a server outage and can lock you out of biometric login.

### 2. Test your physical card (it often still works)
Revolut card transactions are processed through card networks (Visa/Mastercard), not the app. In many past incidents, **contactless and chip-and-PIN payments kept working** while the app was down. Try a small purchase first. Note: online payments that require **3-D Secure approval in the app** will fail — use a different card for those.

### 3. Use the ATM with your physical card
- Insert your Revolut card and use **PIN-based withdrawal**.
- Expect **fair-use fee limits** to still apply (e.g., free ATM withdrawals up to a monthly cap depending on your plan).
- If the ATM declines, it's likely the issuer authorization service is down — move to step 4.

### 4. Switch to a backup payment method
This is the single most important habit. Keep at least one **separate bank account or credit card** (not a Revolut virtual card) for emergencies. If you don't have one:
- Use **Apple Pay/Google Pay with a different bank's card** if already loaded.
- Ask a trusted contact to send money via a **different app** (Wise, PayPal, a local bank transfer).
- For urgent bills, call the provider and request a **grace period** — most utilities and lenders will note an outage.

### 5. Protect your account while it's down
- **Do not** enter your Revolut credentials into any "status checker" site — phishing spikes during outages.
- Only trust links from the **official Revolut app, website, or verified social accounts**.
- If you get a "verify your account to restore access" email during the outage, treat it as **fraud by default**.

### 6. Document losses for reimbursement
If a declined transaction caused a fee (late payment, overdraft elsewhere), screenshot the error and timestamp it. Revolut support can log complaints once systems recover — keep a record.

### 7. Wait for the all-clear, then verify
Once service returns:
- Open the app and check your **transaction history** for duplicates or failed-but-charged payments.
- Re-enable any **frozen cards** you locked during the outage.
- Update your **backup payment method** so you're not caught again.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

### Is my money safe during a Revolut outage?
Yes, in the ordinary case. Customer funds are held in **segregated accounts at licensed banks**, and Revolut is regulated as an e-money institution in the UK/EU (with banking licenses in some markets). An outage affects **access**, not the existence of your balance. The real risk is **liquidity** — being unable to pay for something time-sensitive.

### Why do fintech outages happen?
Most are caused by:
- **Cloud provider failures** (AWS/Azure regional incidents).
- **Third-party payment processor outages** (card authorization, 3-D Secure).
- **Database or authentication service failures** during high load.
- **Botched deployments** or configuration changes.

### Revolut vs. a traditional bank during an outage
| Factor | Revolut (fintech) | Traditional bank |
|---|---|---|
| App-only access risk | High during outage | Lower (branch/phone/ATM) |
| Physical card fallback | Usually works | Usually works |
| Human support during outage | Delayed | Phone lines available |
| Speed of recovery | Fast (cloud-based) | Slower but redundant |

**Takeaway:** Fintechs are fast and cheap but **single-point-of-failure** prone. Traditional banks are clunky but have more redundancy. The smart move is **one of each**.

### Backup accounts worth considering
- **Wise** — multi-currency, strong uptime record.
- **Monzo/Starling** — UK digital banks with FSCS protection.
- **A local high-street bank** — for branch/phone fallback.
- **A credit card** — for emergencies where debit authorization fails.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Revolut down right now?
Check **Downdetector** and Revolut's official status channels. If reports spike worldwide and the app shows errors across regions, it's a service-wide outage, not your device. Don't reinstall the app until status pages confirm recovery.

### Can I still use my Revolut card during an outage?
Often yes. **Chip-and-PIN and contactless payments** frequently keep working because they route through card networks, while in-app features (top-ups, virtual cards, 3-D Secure) fail. Test with a small purchase.

### Will I get my money back if a payment failed during the outage?
Failed transactions are usually **reversed automatically** once systems recover, but authorization holds can take **1–5 business days** to clear. Check your transaction history after service returns and contact support with timestamps if a charge sticks.

### Is my Revolut balance safe if the app is down?
Yes. Funds are held in **segregated accounts** at licensed banks. The outage affects access, not your balance. The main risk is being unable to pay time-sensitive bills — hence the backup-account advice.

### How do I contact Revolut during an outage?
In-app chat is usually unavailable during full outages. Use **official social channels** for status updates and log a complaint once service returns. Avoid third-party "support" numbers found online — they're a common scam vector.

## Final Verdict

A Revolut outage is a **liquidity test, not a solvency event** — your money is safe, but your ability to spend it isn't guaranteed. The single highest-value action you can take today is to **set up a backup payment method with a different provider** and keep a physical card in your wallet. If you're Revolut-only, treat this outage as a warning: split your day-to-day spending across **two accounts**, keep a small cash buffer, and never rely on a single app for time-critical payments. Do that, and the next outage becomes an inconvenience — not a crisis.
