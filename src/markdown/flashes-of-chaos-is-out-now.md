## Best Settings and Performance Fixes for Flashes of Chaos

The game is a 2D action roguelike, so it should run on modest hardware, but launch builds often ship with aggressive default settings. Here is a practical configuration to target a stable 60 FPS or higher.

### Recommended Graphics Settings

| Setting | Recommended Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| V-Sync | Off (use driver-level) | Reduces input latency in a fast shooter |
| Frame Rate Cap | Match monitor refresh (e.g., 144) | Prevents GPU overheating and coil whine |
| Resolution Scale | 100% native | The pixel art style doesn't benefit from upscaling |
| Particle Density | Medium | Heavy spell effects are the main FPS killer |
| Screen Shake | Low or Off | Improves visibility during chaotic fights |
| Bloom / Glow | Off | Minor FPS gain, cleaner visual clarity |
| Fullscreen | Exclusive Fullscreen | Better frame pacing than borderless |

### Step-by-Step: Fix Launch Stutter and Crashes

1. **Verify game files.** On Steam, right-click the game > Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity. This fixes most corrupted-install crashes.
2. **Update GPU drivers.** Launch-day builds frequently rely on newer driver profiles. A clean install via DDU is worth it if you see black screens.
3. **Disable overlays.** Turn off the Steam overlay, Discord overlay, and any recording software. These are a common cause of micro-stutter in fast-paced titles.
4. **Cap your frame rate.** Uncapped FPS in a lightweight 2D game can push your GPU to 100% and cause thermal throttling. Cap it at your refresh rate.
5. **Run as administrator** if the game fails to save progress between runs.
6. **Check for a day-one patch.** Indie launches often push hotfixes within 24–48 hours. Restart Steam to force the update.

If you still get crashes, lower **Particle Density** first. Spell-heavy builds generate hundreds of particles per second, and this is the single most impactful setting for stability.

## Wand-Building for Beginners: A Practical Framework

The wand system is the heart of the game, but it is easy to overcomplicate. Think of your wand as a **trigger, a payload, and a delivery method**. Here is a simple starting framework.

- **Trigger spells** (e.g., a projectile that casts on impact) let you chain effects without spending mana on separate casts.
- **Modifiers** change projectile behavior. Start with one damage modifier and one speed or homing modifier per wand.
- **Mana management** is your real constraint. A wand with high mana regen but low capacity suits rapid-fire builds; a high-capacity wand suits slow, heavy hitters.
- **Cast delay vs. recharge time** determine your fire rate. Lower both for a machine-gun feel; accept higher values for a burst-damage cannon.

A reliable first build: a fast projectile + a damage modifier + a homing modifier on a wand with low cast delay. Test it in the tutorial arena before committing to a run. If a spell combo fizzles, check your mana cost against your regen rate — that is the most common beginner mistake.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

- **"My wand won't fire."** You are almost certainly out of mana or your cast delay is longer than you think. Check the wand stats panel.
- **"The game feels too fast."** Lower screen shake, increase your FOV if available, and practice in short sessions. The pace is intentional; muscle memory comes with repetition.
- **Controller vs. keyboard.** The game supports both, but twin-stick aiming on a controller is generally more precise for this genre. Test both in the tutorial.
- **Is it worth it?** If you loved Noita's tinkering but found its runs too long and slow, this is a strong fit. If you want a deep narrative or long-form progression, it is a lighter experience by design.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Flashes of Chaos similar to Noita?
It borrows Noita's wand-building and spell-combination systems but applies them to a faster, top-down, Hotline Miami-style shooter with shorter runs.

### Why is Flashes of Chaos stuttering on my PC?
The most common causes are uncapped frame rates, high particle density, and background overlays like Steam or Discord. Cap your FPS, lower particle settings, and disable overlays first.

### Does Flashes of Chaos have controller support?
Yes. Twin-stick controller input is generally recommended for precision aiming in this fast-paced format, though keyboard and mouse work fine.

### How long is a typical run in Flashes of Chaos?
Runs are designed to be short and action-focused, which is the core design difference from Noita's longer, more methodical sessions.
