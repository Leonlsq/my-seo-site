Reports say Instacart held early discussions about selling itself to DoorDash or Uber — a signal that the standalone grocery-delivery model is under real pressure. If you're an Instacart customer, Instacart+ subscriber, or shopper, the practical question isn't "will it happen?" (talks are reportedly early and non-binding) — it's **what should I do with my account, subscription, and data right now?** Short answer: don't panic-cancel anything, but do take 15 minutes to lock in your savings, export your order history, and understand how a merger would likely affect fees, tips, and shopper pay. This guide walks you through exactly that.

## Step-by-Step: Protect Your Account in 15 Minutes

Do these now, regardless of whether a deal happens:

1. **Export your order history.** In the app: *Account → Orders → select order → View receipt → Download/Share*. Do this for your last 12 months if you claim grocery expenses or run a small business.
2. **Check your Instacart+ renewal date.** Go to *Account → Instacart+ → Manage Membership*. If you renew within 60 days and order 2+ times per week, an **annual plan ($99/yr)** beats monthly ($9.99/mo) — that's a ~$20 annual saving you keep even if pricing changes later.
3. **Screenshot your current fee breakdown.** Open a recent order and capture the itemized fees (delivery, service, priority, heavy-item, long-distance). This is your baseline to compare against post-merger pricing.
4. **Diversify one backup app.** Install and set up **one** alternative — Walmart Grocery, Target Same-Day (via Shipt), or your local store's app — so you're not stranded if service terms shift.
5. **For shoppers:** Export your earnings. In the Shopper app, go to *Earnings → Weekly Summary* and screenshot 4 weeks of batch pay, tips, and active hours. This protects you if batch-pay structures change.

## Common Concerns and What You Should Know

- **"Will my Instacart+ perks disappear?"** Unlikely immediately. Acquisitions typically honor existing subscriptions until renewal, then migrate users to a new tier. Watch your email for a "terms update" notice — that's your 30-day warning.
- **"Should I cancel now?"** No. Canceling forfeits accrued savings and free-delivery thresholds. Wait for an official announcement with a concrete timeline.
- **"Will DoorDash/Uber raise prices?"** Historically, consolidation reduces promo competition. Expect fewer stacked coupons rather than a sudden price hike.
- **Shopper angle:** If batch algorithms merge with Dasher or Uber systems, expect changes to how batches are assigned and rated. Track your **earnings per active hour** weekly so you can spot a bad change fast.
- **Data privacy:** Both DoorDash and Uber have their own privacy policies. If you're uncomfortable, you can request a data export or deletion under CCPA/GDPR — but do this *after* any deal closes, not before, to avoid losing order history you might need.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Instacart actually being sold to DoorDash or Uber?
As of the latest reports, Instacart has held **early, non-binding discussions** about a potential sale with both DoorDash and Uber. No deal has been confirmed, and any transaction would require board approval plus likely FTC review — so treat this as a possibility, not a done deal.

### Will my Instacart+ subscription still work if Instacart is acquired?
In most acquisitions, existing subscriptions are honored until their next renewal, after which users are migrated to a new plan or tier. Screenshot your current terms and renewal date now so you have proof of your grandfathered pricing if it changes.

### Should Instacart shoppers look for other work right now?
Not yet — but prepare. Export four weeks of earnings data, note your average earnings per active hour, and keep a backup gig app (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Shipt) active. This gives you a fast exit if batch pay or assignment rules change post-merger.

### What happens to my order history and personal data if Instacart sells?
Account data typically migrates to the acquiring company under its privacy policy. Download your receipts now via *Account → Orders* for your records, and review the acquirer's privacy policy once a deal is confirmed before deciding whether to request deletion.
