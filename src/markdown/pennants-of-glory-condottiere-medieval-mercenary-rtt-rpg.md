## Pennants of Glory vs. Battle Brothers vs. Mount & Blade: System Comparison

The developers describe a **real-time tactics (RTT) / RPG hybrid** set in a simulated medieval Italian mercenary era. Here's how the confirmed feature set stacks up against the obvious inspirations:

| Feature | Pennants of Glory | Battle Brothers | Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combat type | Real-time tactics (pause/command) | Turn-based tactical | Real-time action + command |
| World simulation | Procedural, factions fight for dominance | Static-ish map events | Dynamic AI factions |
| Persistent losses | Men and weapons must be replaced | Roster attrition | Troop casualties |
| Endgame threat | Survive or join invasions | Crisis events | Late-game wars |
| Player role | Mercenary captain (Condottiere) | Mercenary company | Lord/mercenary |

**Key takeaways from the spec sheet:**
- **"Each loss is felt"** — this signals permadeath-style roster attrition. Budget for replacement costs, not just recruitment.
- **"Victory might be too expensive"** — implies a post-battle cost economy (wages, repairs, wounded). Expect to fight *fewer, better* battles rather than grind.
- **Procedural world** — no two campaigns identical; factions battle each other independently of you.
- **Endgame invasions** — a late-game escalation you can fight or ally with, similar to Battle Brothers' crisis system.
