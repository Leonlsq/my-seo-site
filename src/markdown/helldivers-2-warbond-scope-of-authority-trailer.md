## Helldivers 2 Scope of Authority Warbond: Everything You Need to Decide Before You Buy

The **Scope of Authority Warbond** just dropped its official trailer, and the Helldivers 2 community is already split on whether it's a must-buy or a skip. This guide cuts through the hype and gives you a practical, numbers-first breakdown of every weapon, armor passive, and cosmetic so you can decide in under five minutes whether to spend your hard-earned Super Credits. If you're a returning player wondering whether to grind 1,000 SC or drop real money, this is the only breakdown you need.

The short version: Scope of Authority leans into **precision, medium-penetration firepower, and a defensive armor passive** that rewards players who hold objectives rather than dive into chaos. It's a strong pick for bug-front veterans and a situational one for bot divers.

## Scope of Authority Warbond: Full Contents and Stats Breakdown

Here's what you get for **1,000 Super Credits** (roughly $9.99 USD or ~40–60 hours of free farming). The Warbond unlocks across **3 pages** and costs **~690 Medals** to fully clear.

### Weapons

| Weapon | Type | Damage | Penetration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AR-32 Adjudicator | Assault Rifle | 90 | Medium | Slower ROF, heavy stagger |
| SG-88 Breaker S&P | Shotgun | 240 (x8 pellets) | Medium | Slug + pellet hybrid |
| P-113 Verdict | Pistol | 125 | Medium | High recoil, 8-round mag |

The **AR-32 Adjudicator** is the standout. It's a medium-penetration assault rifle that trades rate of fire for per-shot damage, making it excellent against **Hive Guards, Brood Commanders, and Devastators**. The trade-off is a 25-round magazine and noticeable recoil — pair it with the **Engineering Kit** armor passive or crouch-fire to control spread.

### Armor Passive: "Fortified"

The headline armor set (light, medium, and heavy variants) ships with the **Fortified** passive:

- **+50% explosive resistance** (grenades, rockets, mortar fire)
- **-30% recoil while crouched or prone**
- **Reduced flinch from incoming fire**

This is a **defensive, hold-the-line passive**. It's fantastic on **Eradicate, Flag Raising, and Defense missions** but nearly useless on fast-moving Blitz runs. If you main light armor and rely on mobility, this passive will feel like a downgrade.

### Stratagems and Boosters

The Warbond includes one new **Booster** — *Vitality Enhancement* — which grants a **+20% max HP buff to the whole squad** for the duration of the mission. This is arguably the single most valuable item in the entire Warbond and stacks well with the **Stim Enhancer** booster for survivability-focused squads.

## Is the Scope of Authority Warbond Worth It? Cost vs. Value Analysis

Let's do the math on whether this Warbond earns its 1,000 Super Credit price tag.

**Buy it if:**
- You regularly play **Difficulty 7–10** and need medium-pen weapons for bug front
- You run **defensive or objective-hold loadouts** with a coordinated squad
- You want the **Vitality Enhancement booster**, which is a permanent squad-wide upgrade
- You're a completionist chasing the **100% Warbond unlock** for the Platinum trophy

**Skip it if:**
- You're a **new player under Level 15** — spend your SC on the **Steeled Veterans** or **Cutting Edge** Warbonds first
- You main **light armor and mobility builds**
- You already own **Democratic Detonation** or **Polar Patriots**, which offer broader weapon variety

## Common Issues and What You Should Know Before Buying

**1. Super Credit farming is slow.** Expect **10–20 hours** of dedicated farming on Difficulty 4–6 to earn 1,000 SC free. If your time is worth more than $10, just buy it.

**2. The Adjudicator has a learning curve.** New players often bounce off it because of the recoil. Practice on **Difficulty 3 Terminid missions** before taking it to Helldive.

**3. The Fortified passive does NOT stack** with other explosive-resistance sources. Don't waste a slot on the *Explosive Resistance* booster if you're already wearing the armor.

**4. The trailer oversells the shotgun.** The **SG-88 Breaker S&P** looks incredible in the cinematic but performs inconsistently at range. It's a close-quarters tool, not a generalist.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How many Super Credits is the Scope of Authority Warbond?
It costs **1,000 Super Credits**, the standard price for all Helldivers 2 Warbonds. You can earn this free by farming Super Credits on missions or buy it for approximately $9.99 USD.

### What is the best weapon in the Scope of Authority Warbond?
The **AR-32 Adjudicator** is widely considered the best weapon, offering medium armor penetration with strong per-shot damage. It excels against medium-tier enemies like Hive Guards and Devastators.

### Does the Vitality Enhancement booster stack with other boosters?
No. Only **one booster per squad** can be active at a time, so coordinate with your team. Vitality Enhancement is best paired with a squad already running Stim Enhancer or Hellpod Space Optimization.

### Is Scope of Authority better than Steeled Veterans?
For **new players, no** — Steeled Veterans offers more universally useful weapons. For **veterans running defensive builds on high difficulty**, Scope of Authority is the stronger pick due to the Fortified passive and Vitality booster.

## Final Verdict

The **Scope of Authority Warbond is a solid 7.5/10** — not a must-buy, but absolutely worth it if you run medium-penetration loadouts or play with a coordinated squad on Difficulty 7+. The **Vitality Enhancement booster alone** justifies the price for dedicated players, and the Adjudicator is a genuinely fun, skill-rewarding rifle.

**My recommendation:** If you're a new player, buy **Steeled Veterans** first. If you're a veteran with 1,000+ SC banked, grab Scope of Authority — just don't expect it to replace your Eruptor or Dominator anytime soon.
