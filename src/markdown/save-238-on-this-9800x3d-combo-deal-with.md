## How to Build a 4K Gaming PC Around This Combo

1. **Lock the combo first.** Buy the CPU + board + RAM bundle before prices shift; combo stock is limited.
2. **Pick a GPU that fits the 4K target.** Budget ~$700–$900 for a current-gen card (e.g., RTX 5070-class or RX 9070-class). At 4K, the GPU is the deciding factor — don't overspend on the CPU and underspend here.
3. **PSU:** 850W 80+ Gold (ATX 3.1) is the safe floor for a 4K card. ~$110.
4. **Storage:** 2TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe (~$120). Use the board's PCIe 5.0 slot only if you find a deal.
5. **Cooler:** The 9800X3D runs cool, but a 240mm AIO or a strong dual-tower air cooler (~$60–$110) keeps it quiet.
6. **Case:** Mesh-front mid-tower with good airflow (~$80–$110).
7. **Enable EXPO in BIOS** on first boot so your DDR5 runs at its rated speed — this is the single most-missed step.

**Rough all-in total:** ~$1,029 (combo) + ~$800 (GPU) + ~$400 (PSU/SSD/cooler/case) ≈ **$2,200–$2,400** for a complete 4K build. The "just over $1,000" headline refers to the combo alone, not the finished PC.

## Common Pitfalls, Alternatives, and What to Know

- **Don't buy if you already have DDR5 or an AM5 board.** You'd be paying for parts you can't use; the $238 savings only exists for new builders.
- **Verify the exact RAM kit.** Confirm the DDR5 speed (6000 MT/s CL30 is the sweet spot for AM5) and that it's on the board's QVL list.
- **Check the specific Gigabyte X870E model.** Features vary — confirm PCIe 5.0 lane allocation and M.2 slot count before committing.
- **Alternative:** A 7800X3D + B650 combo often runs $250–$350 cheaper and delivers ~90–95% of the 9800X3D's 4K gaming performance. If budget is tight, that's the smarter value play.
- **Alternative 2:** If you game at 1440p, the 9800X3D's CPU advantage is more visible — but at 4K, spend the savings on the GPU instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the 9800X3D combo deal worth it in 2026?
Yes for new builders — the ~$238 savings is real and the 9800X3D remains a top gaming CPU. Skip it if you already own AM5 or DDR5 parts.

### Can this combo alone run 4K gaming?
No. The bundle is CPU, motherboard, and RAM only. You still need a GPU, PSU, SSD, cooler, and case — budget roughly $1,100–$1,400 more for a complete 4K system.

### What RAM speed should I enable with this bundle?
Target DDR5-6000 CL30 and enable AMD EXPO in BIOS. That's the performance sweet spot for AM5 and the 9800X3D.

### 9800X3D vs 7800X3D for 4K — which is better value?
At 4K the GPU dominates, so a 7800X3D combo saves $250–$350 with minimal frame loss. The 9800X3D wins on longevity and 1440p headroom.
