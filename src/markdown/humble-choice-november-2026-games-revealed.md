## How to Claim, Pause, or Skip (Step-by-Step)

1. **Log in** at humblebundle.com and open **Account → My Subscription**.
2. **Review the reveal** under "This Month's Games." Confirm your tier shows **Classic** (if grandfathered).
3. **Claim titles individually** — do NOT hit "Redeem All" until you've checked your Steam library for duplicates. Keys are revealed one at a time.
4. **To pause:** click **"Manage Plan" → "Pause a Month"** *before* the auto-billing date (typically the last Tuesday of the month). Pausing preserves your Classic rate; canceling does not.
5. **To skip permanently:** cancel via the same menu, but note you'll rejoin at Premium pricing later.
6. **Check key regions** — some keys are region-locked. Verify your Steam account region matches before revealing a key you can't activate.

## Common Issues & What You Should Know

- **"My key is already redeemed" error:** usually means a duplicate you owned. Contact Humble support with the order ID; they'll swap it if it's a genuine duplicate, but not if you simply forgot.
- **Gift link restrictions:** 2026 policy limits gifting; don't buy the bundle planning to resell keys — you'll eat the loss.
- **Charity split:** you can still allocate your purchase to charity, but the default split has shifted. Adjust it manually under checkout if that matters to you.
- **Store discount stacking:** Premium's discount does **not** stack with sale prices on most titles — verify before assuming a double discount.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### When does Humble Choice November 2026 auto-bill?
Auto-billing typically occurs on the **last Tuesday of the month**. Pause or cancel before that date to avoid being charged for the November slate.

### Is Humble Choice November 2026 worth it for $11.99?
Yes, **if** you'll play both marquee titles. The two headline games alone exceed the Classic price. If you only want one, compare its historical Steam low first — the bundle may not beat a sale.

### What happens to unclaimed keys if I pause?
Unclaimed keys for the current month remain available in your account after pausing; you don't lose them, but you won't receive next month's games until you resume.
