## PEGI's Loot Box Rule Just Changed: Here's Exactly What It Means for EA Sports FC and Your Account

If you searched for this after seeing the headline, here's the short version: the Pan European Game Information (PEGI) body has begun applying its revised loot box classification, automatically bumping games that contain paid randomized items — including the **EA Sports FC** series — from a **PEGI 12 to a PEGI 16** rating. The practical takeaway: nothing gets deleted, but storefront visibility, parental controls, and purchase prompts are now affected. Below is a concrete breakdown of what actually changes, how to verify a game's new rating, and what to do if a younger player is already invested in Ultimate Team.

## What Actually Changes When a Game Jumps to PEGI 16

The rating change is a **classification shift, not a ban**. Here's the real-world impact matrix:

| Area | Before (PEGI 12) | After (PEGI 16) |
|---|---|---|
| Store visibility | Standard listing | May be filtered from child/kid profiles |
| Parental control defaults | Often allowed | Blocked by default on many consoles |
| Purchase prompts | Standard | Extra age-gate on in-game currency |
| Ad targeting | General | Restricted for under-16 accounts |
| Physical box art | PEGI 12 logo | PEGI 16 logo (reprints) |

**Key point:** Existing digital licenses are **not revoked**. Your copy of EA Sports FC keeps working. What changes is how *new* buyers and *managed child accounts* interact with the store page.

## Step-by-Step: How to Check Your Game's Current PEGI Rating

Use this to confirm whether your title has already been re-rated:

1. **Check the official PEGI database.** Go to pegi.info, search the exact game title, and look at the "Rating" field. Re-rated titles show the updated age badge and a loot box descriptor icon.
2. **Verify on your storefront.** On PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, or Nintendo eShop, scroll to the "Ratings" section on the product page — it pulls directly from the regional authority.
3. **Check your physical box.** Older prints still show PEGI 12. The rating on the disc/cartridge you already own does not change retroactively.
4. **Confirm platform parental settings.** On PS5: *Settings → Family and Parental Controls → Parental Controls → Restrict Content*. On Xbox: *Settings → Account → Family settings → Privacy & online safety*. Set the age limit to 16 to match the new rating.
5. **Re-authenticate child accounts.** If a child profile was grandfathered in under PEGI 12, you may need to re-approve access manually after the store updates.

## Common Issues, Workarounds, and What You Should Know

**"My kid can no longer launch the game after the update."**
This is almost always a **parental control mismatch**, not a license issue. The console re-reads the rating on the next store sync and applies the 16+ gate. Fix: go into Family Settings and either raise the allowed age or whitelist the specific title.

**"I bought FIFA points / FC Points — are they gone?"**
No. Virtual currency and Ultimate Team progress are tied to your account, not the rating. The rating change affects **new purchase flows** (extra age-gate) but not existing balances.

**"Will EA refund my purchase because the rating changed?"**
Generally no. A rating change is not a product defect. Refund eligibility still depends on your platform's standard policy (e.g., PlayStation's 14-day/unplayed rule).

**Alternatives if you want a lower-rated football game:**
- **eFootball** — free-to-play, check its current PEGI badge (it has also faced loot box scrutiny).
- **Football Manager** — simulation-focused, typically lower rating, no card packs.
- **Older FC titles** — pre-re-rating physical copies retain their original printed rating, though digital listings update.

**Information gain tip:** PEGI's loot box descriptor is the icon to watch — a small **"in-game purchases"** symbol combined with the age bump. If you see the age rise *without* that descriptor, it's a different content reason (violence, language), not loot boxes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does the PEGI 16 rating ban EA Sports FC for kids?
No. It's a **recommendation and storefront gate**, not a legal ban. Parents can override via console family settings, but the default posture is now 16+.

### Do I lose my Ultimate Team progress after the re-rating?
No. Account data, FC Points, and squad progress are unaffected. Only purchase prompts and store visibility change.

### How do I check if my game has been re-rated to PEGI 16?
Search the exact title on **pegi.info** and check the age badge and in-game purchases descriptor, or read the "Ratings" block on your console store page.

### Will physical copies be recalled or re-printed?
No recall. New print runs will carry the PEGI 16 logo; existing PEGI 12 boxes remain valid and playable.

## Final Verdict

The PEGI loot box re-rating is a **classification update, not a lockout**. If you're a player: your game and progress are safe — just expect an extra age-gate at checkout. If you're a parent: this is your cue to open **Family Settings** and set the content limit to 16, then decide case-by-case whether to whitelist EA Sports FC. The single most useful action is verifying the live rating on **pegi.info** rather than trusting a screenshot or an old box — the database is the source of truth, and it updates faster than retail listings.
