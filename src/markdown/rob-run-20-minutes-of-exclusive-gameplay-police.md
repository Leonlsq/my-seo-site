### Bank Heist: How the Loop Works

From the 3:15 chapter, the heist follows a recognizable structure:

1. **Scout the entrance** — approach routes matter because cop response is role-based, not scripted.
2. **Breach and secure** — the payout window is limited; the longer you stay, the higher the risk multiplier from responding players.
3. **Extract** — the escape is where most runs fail. Plan your exit before you enter.

**Practical takeaway:** treat the heist as a timed risk-reward equation. If the setup takes longer than the payout justifies, grind lower-tier jobs first and reinvest in gear.

### Cop Mode: How to Counter Heisters

The 7:16 segment shows cop gameplay as a genuine role, not a scripted NPC. Key observations:

- **Response timing is the whole game.** Knowing where criminals spawn lets you intercept rather than chase.
- **Positioning beats firepower.** Holding chokepoints near bank exits is more effective than roaming.
- **Cop payouts are steady, not spiky.** If you want low-variance income while you learn the map, start here.

### Item Catalogue: Reading the Stats

- **Check weight/slot limits** — the catalogue shows constraints that determine what you can actually carry into a heist.
