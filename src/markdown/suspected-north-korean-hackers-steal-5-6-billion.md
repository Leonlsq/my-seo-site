## Step-by-Step: Verify, Unstick, and Secure Your Funds

### Step 1 — Confirm the freeze is platform-wide, not account-specific
1. Open the Binance app → **Wallet → Spot → Transaction History**.
2. Filter by **Withdrawals** and check the status tag. A platform freeze shows a generic *"Withdrawals suspended for maintenance/security review"* banner on the withdraw screen. An account-level restriction shows a **KYC/risk review** prompt instead.
3. Cross-check the official **Binance Status** page and the `@binance` X account. If the banner appears for everyone, you are in the same queue as millions of other users — nothing is wrong with your specific account.

### Step 2 — Match your asset to the network freeze status
The $2.4B asset freeze is happening at the **stablecoin issuer level**, not inside Binance. That means USDT/USDC on certain chains may be blocked on-chain while BTC and ETH are unaffected.

| Asset | Freeze risk | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| USDT (TRC-20/ERC-20) | High — issuer can blacklist addresses | Don't send to unknown wallets; wait for issuer confirmation |
| USDC | High — Circle can freeze at contract level | Hold in spot wallet, avoid bridging |
| BTC / ETH | Low — no central issuer | Withdrawals resume once Binance lifts the queue |
| Exchange tokens | Medium — liquidity shock | Avoid market-selling into thin order books |

### Step 3 — Get your withdrawal unstuck
- **Cancel and resubmit** any pending withdrawal stuck in "Processing" for over 30 minutes — a stale order can block your daily limit.
- **Switch networks** if one chain is congested (e.g., move USDT from ERC-20 to TRC-20 — but only if the destination exchange supports it).
- **Raise your 2FA readiness**: enable **Google Authenticator + anti-phishing code** now, because heist events trigger a spike in phishing SMS and fake "Binance security" emails.
- **Check withdrawal limits** under *Security → Withdrawal Whitelist*. New addresses added during a freeze often carry a 24-hour hold.

### Step 4 — Move to self-custody the right way
Once withdrawals reopen, do **not** dump everything into one hot wallet. Use a hardware wallet (Ledger/Trezor), verify the receive address on the device screen, and send a **small test transaction first**. For large balances, split across two cold wallets so a single compromised seed phrase can't wipe you out.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

- **"My withdrawal says completed but I don't see funds."** This is usually a network confirmation delay, not theft. Check the TXID on a block explorer before contacting support.
- **Fake "unfreeze" scams are surging.** No legitimate Binance agent will DM you first, ask for your seed phrase, or request a "release fee." Any of those = 100% scam.
- **Loss coverage reality check:** Binance says it will cover losses from its SAFU insurance fund. That protects *exchange* losses — it does **not** protect you if you self-custody badly or approve a malicious contract.
- **Alternatives if you need liquidity now:** Kraken, Coinbase, and OKX may still process withdrawals, but expect wider spreads and higher fees during a sector-wide freeze. Compare before you move.
- **Revoke old approvals.** Use Revoke.cash or Etherscan's token approval checker to kill permissions you granted months ago — a common attack vector in the aftermath of large heists.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is my money safe on Binance during the withdrawal freeze?
Yes, in almost all cases. A withdrawal freeze locks *outbound transfers*, not your balances. Your funds remain visible in your spot wallet and are covered by Binance's SAFU insurance fund for exchange-side losses. The freeze exists to stop stolen assets from being laundered through the platform.

### How long will Binance withdrawals stay frozen?
Historically, security-driven withdrawal pauses last **a few hours to 72 hours**, depending on how fast the stablecoin issuers finish blacklisting stolen addresses. Watch the official Binance Status page and X account for the "resumed" notice — don't trust screenshots circulating on social media.

### Can North Korean hackers steal crypto from my personal wallet?
Only if you expose your seed phrase or sign a malicious contract. The $5.6B heist targeted exchange and protocol infrastructure, not individual hardware wallets. Keep your seed offline, never enter it on a website, and verify every transaction on your device screen.

### Should I sell my crypto because of this heist?
Panic-selling into a frozen market is the worst move — order books are thin and spreads widen. If you must reduce exposure, wait for withdrawals to reopen and use limit orders rather than market sells.
