## How The Black Pool's 9-Element System Actually Works

The core hook is that you pick from **9 elements** for your abilities, and each element has roughly **6 variations** across your kit. That means your build is defined by *which element goes into which slot*, not just a generic class pick.

Your ability slots break down like this:

| Slot | Function | Example (Ice) |
|------|----------|--------------|
| Light Attack | Fast, low-commitment damage | Ice shard projectile |
| Heavy Attack | High-damage, slower | Charged ice blast |
| Support | Team utility | Buff/heal variant |
| Dash | Mobility/evasion | Ice dash |
| Jump | Verticality/traversal | Ice platform under you **and teammates** |
| AOE Buff | Area effect | Frost field |

The **Ice jump creating a platform for your whole squad** is a perfect example of why co-op matters here — traversal and positioning abilities scale with team play. That's the Returnal-meets-Risk-of-Rain-2 DNA in action.

### Status Effects and Synergy Unlocks

Elements aren't just damage types — they carry **status effect bonuses**. The clearest example: filling the **Earth status** meter makes your **next damaging attack deal double damage**. That's a burst-window mechanic you should actively play around, not ignore.

On top of that, you unlock **synergies between elements**, which is where the build depth lives. As you progress through a run, you get **upgrades for your skills**, and the variation pool is genuinely diverse for what's been shown so far.

## How to Build Your First Character (Practical Tips)

1. **Pick one damage element and one utility element.** Don't spread across three elements early — synergies reward commitment.
2. **Put a traversal element on Jump.** Ice (platforms) is the safest co-op pick because it helps teammates reach vertical space.
3. **Learn the status-fill loop.** With Earth, deliberately "charge" the status with light attacks, then dump your heavy attack into the double-damage window.
4. **Coordinate element picks with your squad.** In 4-player co-op, overlapping status effects waste potential — split elements so synergies trigger across players.
5. **Upgrade your most-used slot first.** Roguelike upgrade economy is tight; funnel early upgrades into the ability you spam most.
