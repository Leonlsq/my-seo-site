## SPEARHEAD Tank Desant Explained: The WW2 RTS Tactic You Need to Learn

If you have seen the buzz around **SPEARHEAD**, the WW2 RTS from a solo Korean developer, you are probably curious about its standout feature: **tank desant**. This is the real Soviet military doctrine of infantry riding on top of tanks into battle, and SPEARHEAD implements it with a twist that changes how you approach armored warfare. Unlike most RTS games where infantry and tanks fight as separate units, SPEARHEAD lets infantry **ride on tanks, fire anti-tank weapons from the hull, and even let the tank tow an anti-tank gun** behind it. This guide breaks down exactly how to use these mechanics, when to deploy them, and how to avoid the classic mistakes that get your desant squads wiped out in seconds.

The short version: tank desant in SPEARHEAD is a high-risk, high-reward mobility tool. Use it to rush AT guns into ambush positions, escort tanks against enemy armor, and keep infantry alive under fire. Abuse it, and a single machine gun will erase your squad.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Using Tank Desant in SPEARHEAD

Here is a practical workflow for putting desant infantry and towed guns to work in your matches.

1. **Load infantry onto the tank.** Select an infantry squad, then right-click (or use the embark command) on a friendly tank. The squad mounts the hull. Confirm the unit card shows the "desant" status icon before moving.
2. **Keep the tank at medium speed.** Infantry on the hull are exposed. Sprinting through open ground draws MG fire. Move in short bounds, using cover and smoke to break line of sight.
3. **Fire anti-tank weapons from the hull.** While mounted, AT-capable infantry can still engage enemy armor. This is the core advantage: your tank and its riders can focus fire on the same target, doubling your anti-armor burst.
4. **Dismount before close assault.** Riding infantry cannot take cover. Dismount behind buildings, hedges, or craters before pushing into urban or forest fights.
5. **Hitch the anti-tank gun.** Order a tank to tow an AT gun by selecting the gun and issuing a tow command to the tank. The gun follows at reduced speed, letting you reposition heavy AT assets without slow manual pushes.
6. **Set up the gun immediately after unhitching.** Unlimber the AT gun behind cover, orient it toward the expected armor lane, and only then move the tank forward.

## Common Mistakes, Visual Settings, and What You Should Know

The developer noted that brightening the shader made the game feel like **Company of Heroes (CoH)**, while darkening it pushed it toward **Gates of Hell (GoH)**. That matters for gameplay readability: desant infantry are small models on a moving tank, so a brighter, higher-contrast look helps you spot riders and incoming threats. If your build allows visual tweaks, favor clarity over mood when learning the mechanic.

Common pitfalls to avoid:

- **Riding into machine gun arcs.** Exposed infantry die fast. Scout first.
- **Towing guns through artillery.** A towed gun is a slow, fat target. Unhitch before crossing open kill zones.
- **Forgetting dismount timing.** Mounted squads cannot use cover bonuses; dismount before the firefight starts.
- **Overloading one tank.** Spread desant squads across multiple tanks so one AT round does not delete your entire infantry force.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can infantry shoot anti-tank weapons while riding a tank in SPEARHEAD?
Yes. That is the defining feature of tank desant in SPEARHEAD: mounted infantry can still perform anti-tank attacks from the hull, letting a tank and its riders focus fire on enemy armor.

### Can a tank tow an anti-tank gun in SPEARHEAD?
Yes. Tanks can hitch and tow anti-tank guns, letting you reposition heavy AT assets quickly instead of slowly pushing them by hand. Unhitch and set up the gun behind cover before engaging.

### Is SPEARHEAD more like Company of Heroes or Gates of Hell?
The developer said a brighter shader made it feel like CoH and a darker one made it feel like GoH. Expect a tactical WW2 RTS that sits between those two styles, with desant mechanics as its signature twist.
