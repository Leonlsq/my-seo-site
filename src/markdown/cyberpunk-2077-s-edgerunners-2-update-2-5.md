## Cyberpunk 2077 2.5: How to Unlock Everything in the Edgerunners Update

Patch 2.5 is live across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S, and it's the biggest post-Phantom Liberty drop yet. The headline act is the **"Clampdown" prequel quest** tied to the *Edgerunners* anime, but the real meat for build-crafters is **dual-wield pistols and revolvers**, the **Glamdoc cyberware skin system**, and the new **Edgerunners radio station**. This guide skips the press-release fluff and gives you the exact steps to trigger every new feature, plus the fixes for the console mod.io rollout that's already tripping people up. If you searched "how to start Clampdown" or "Cyberpunk dual wield not working," you're in the right place.

## Step-by-Step: Triggering Clampdown & Unlocking the King Gear

The Clampdown quest does **not** auto-pop on your journal the moment you load a save. You have to satisfy a prerequisite chain first.

1. **Complete "The Heist"** on your current save (Act 1 finished). New characters must reach Act 2.
2. **Reach Street Cred 20** — Clampdown is gated behind this to prevent low-level players wandering into the new encounter zone.
3. **Fast-travel to the new Edgerunners marker** in **Santo Domingo / Rancho Coronado**. A blue holo-call from a new fixer, **"Reyes,"** triggers within 60 in-game minutes of the marker appearing.
4. **Accept the call** — declining it once is fine, but a second decline soft-locks the quest until you reload a save. Don't decline twice.
5. **Complete the three-stage Clampdown encounter** (stealth infiltration → chase → boss). Finishing it awards the **King gear set**: the *King's Jacket*, *King's Pistol*, and *King's Shades*.

**King gear stat notes:**

| Item | Slot | Key Perk |
|------|------|----------|
| King's Jacket | Outer torso | +15% crit damage while dual-wielding |
| King's Pistol | Power pistol | Unique: fires both hands when dual-wielded |
| King's Shades | Face | +2% headshot, cosmetic Edgerunners frame |

**Dual-wield unlock:** You do **not** need a perk point. Dual-wielding is a base-game ability added in 2.5 — equip **any two pistols or revolvers** in your weapon slots, then hold the **weapon-swap button** (Triangle/Y on console, `X` on keyboard by default) to enter akimbo stance. The King's Pistol is the only weapon with a unique dual-fire animation; standard pistols share a generic akimbo pose.

## Glamdocs, Edgerunners Radio & the FOV Slider

**Glamdocs** are the new cyberware cosmetic system. Visit any **ripperdoc**, open the cyberware menu, and you'll see a new **"Glam" tab**. Here's the catch most players miss: Glamdoc skins only apply to cyberware you've **already installed** — you can't preview a skin for uninstalled chrome. Skins are free and reversible, so experiment freely.

- **Where to buy Glamdoc skins:** Ripperdocs in Japantown and the Afterlife sell the Edgerunners-themed sets. Prices range from €$500 to €$2,000.
- **Edgerunners radio:** Tune to **"89.7 Growl FM"** — the new station sits between existing frequencies. It plays the anime's licensed tracks plus two new original mixes. If it's silent, verify your **audio language pack** is installed (see FAQ).
- **FOV slider:** Now available on **all platforms**, including consoles. Find it under **Settings → Video → Field of View**. PC allows 70–120; consoles cap at 70–100. Bumping FOV above 100 on base PS5 can drop frames in Dogtown — test before committing.

## Console Mods via mod.io: Setup & Troubleshooting

Patch 2.5 introduces **official mod.io support on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** — a first for the game. Setup is quick but the rollout has rough edges.

1. **Link your account:** Main menu → **Mods → Link mod.io**. Scan the QR code with your phone and authorize.
2. **Browse & subscribe:** Mods install automatically on next game launch. Console mods are **curated** — no script extenders, no `.archive` injectors, cosmetic and QoL only.
3. **Enable in load order:** Settings → Mods → toggle each mod **on** manually. Subscribing alone does not activate it.

**Known issues and fixes:**

- **"Mod failed to download" loop:** Clear the mod cache via **Settings → Mods → Clear Cache**, then relaunch. This resolves 90% of failed downloads.
- **Mods disabled after update:** Console mods are wiped on major patches. Re-enable them post-2.5; your subscriptions persist on mod.io.
- **Crash on load with >10 mods:** The console mod budget is capped. Keep active mods under **10** to avoid memory crashes on Series S and base PS5.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Why isn't the Clampdown quest showing up?
Check three things: you've finished Act 1, your Street Cred is 20+, and you haven't declined the fixer call twice. If all three are true, load an earlier save from before the second decline — the quest flag corrupts after a double-decline.

### How do I dual-wield in Cyberpunk 2077 2.5?
Equip two pistols or revolvers, then hold the weapon-swap button. No perk required. The King's Pistol adds a unique dual-fire animation.

### Are console mods free on mod.io?
Yes — mod.io access is free, but you need a linked account. Some creators gate premium mods behind mod.io donations; those are optional.

### Why is the Edgerunners radio silent?
Your console likely lacks the **audio language pack** for the new station. Reinstall the language pack from your platform store, then reboot the game.
