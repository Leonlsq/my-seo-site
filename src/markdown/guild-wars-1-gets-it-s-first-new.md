## How to Prepare for Guild Wars Afterlife (January Release)

Nearly two decades after the *Eye of the North* expansion, ArenaNet has confirmed that **Guild Wars Afterlife** will launch in January. For veteran players and returning fans, this is more than just new content—it's a chance to revisit Tyria with purpose. The direct takeaway: don't wait until launch day to log in. Your account, characters, and gear likely need attention after years of dormancy. This guide walks you through exactly what to do now so you can jump straight into the new content when it drops.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Getting Your Account Ready

### 1. Recover and Secure Your Account
If you haven't logged in for years, your first hurdle is access.
- **Reset your password** via the official ArenaNet account portal.
- **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)** if you haven't already—account security is critical before any major release.
- **Verify your email** is current; password recovery and DLC codes will go there.
- If you've lost access entirely, contact ArenaNet support *now*, not in January. Ticket queues spike around launches.

### 2. Audit Your Characters and Storage
Log in and take inventory before the DLC arrives.
- **Check every character slot.** Note professions, levels, and campaign progress.
- **Empty your storage and Xunlai Chest** of junk; you'll need space for new loot.
- **Sell or salvage duplicate materials** at the trader to free up gold and slots.
- **Locate your best gear**—don't assume your main is still your best-equipped character.

### 3. Rebuild Your Build (Skills and Attributes)
The meta has shifted over the years, and *Afterlife* may introduce new skills or balance changes.
- **Review your skill bars.** Many old builds are still viable, but check community resources like the Guild Wars Wiki and PvXwiki for current meta.
- **Respec your attributes** at a profession trainer if needed.
- **Unlock missing elite skills** via signets of capture—stock up on Signet of Capture items now.

### 4. Stockpile Gold and Materials
Launch economies spike. Prepare financially.
- **Farm gold** via repeatable runs (e.g., Fissure of Woe, Underworld) or trading.
- **Buy common crafting materials** (iron, wood, dust) before prices rise.
- **Hold onto rare materials** (ectoplasm, obsidian shards) if the DLC introduces new crafting.

### 5. Complete Prerequisite Content
If *Afterlife* gates content behind story progress, you'll want to be caught up.
- **Finish all campaigns** (Prophecies, Factions, Nightfall) and *Eye of the North*.
- **Complete the Hall of Monuments** if you want legacy rewards—this has historically mattered for cross-game bonuses.
- **Clear your quest log** of old quests that might conflict with new content.

### 6. Prepare for Group Content
The DLC likely includes new group missions or dungeons.
- **Find a guild or Discord community** now; PUGs will be chaotic at launch.
- **Level a support or healer character**—these roles are always in demand.
- **Test your connection and hardware**; run the game and check for driver updates.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

**Account recovery delays:** ArenaNet support can take days to weeks. Start now.
**Missing DLC ownership:** Verify which campaigns you own in your account management page. *Afterlife* may require specific expansions.
**Hardware compatibility:** Guild Wars 1 runs on almost anything, but if you're on a modern Mac or Linux, check compatibility layers (Wine/Proton) ahead of time.
**Cost consideration:** The DLC price hasn't been fully detailed; budget for the expansion plus potential in-game purchases.
**Alternative prep:** If you're a brand-new player, start with *Prophecies* and follow a leveling guide—don't rush to endgame content before the DLC.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### When exactly does Guild Wars Afterlife release?
ArenaNet has confirmed a January release window, though an exact date hasn't been finalized. Watch official channels for the precise launch day.

### Do I need to own all previous expansions to play Afterlife?
It's likely the DLC requires at least the base game and possibly *Eye of the North*. Check the official store page for exact requirements before purchasing.

### Is Guild Wars 1 still active enough to find groups?
Yes—the community has seen a resurgence with the DLC announcement. Joining a guild or Discord server will make finding groups much easier.

### Will my old characters and progress carry over?
Your existing characters and account progress remain intact. The DLC adds new content on top of your current account.

## Final Verdict

Don't wait for January to start preparing. Spend a few hours now recovering your account, auditing characters, and restocking gold and materials. Veterans who prepare will hit the ground running on launch day, while unprepared players scramble through recovery queues and empty storage. The first new Guild Wars DLC in nearly 20 years deserves a proper homecoming—treat your account like it matters, because it does.
