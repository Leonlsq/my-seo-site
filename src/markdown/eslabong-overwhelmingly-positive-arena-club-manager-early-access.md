## Eslabong Early Access: What It Actually Is and How to Play It Right

If you landed here after watching the *Overwhelmingly Positive* Early Access trailer for **Eslabong**, you're probably wondering one thing: is this just another idle club manager, or is there a real strategy layer worth your $15–$20? The short answer: Eslabong is a **management-sim/arena-builder hybrid** where you run a fighting club, scout fighters, manage arena finances, and climb a league ladder. The trailer sells the vibe, but it hides the two systems that actually decide whether you snowball or stall out by Week 6 — **arena reputation decay** and **fighter fatigue scaling**. This guide covers the settings, economy priorities, and early-game traps so your first 10 hours aren't a soft-lock restart.

## Step-by-Step: Optimal Setup and First 10 Hours

### Step 1 — PC Settings Before You Start
Eslabong is Unity-based and light on hardware, but the UI scales poorly on ultrawide monitors. Use these baseline settings:

| Setting | Recommended | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | Native (1080p/1440p) | UI scaling breaks above 1440p in Early Access |
| UI Scale | 110–120% | Default text is small on 1440p+ |
| V-Sync | Off | Adds input lag in the fight-sim overlay |
| Frame Cap | 60 FPS | No benefit above; reduces GPU load |
| Autosave Interval | 5 minutes | Default is 15; crashes are still common |

### Step 2 — Pick the Right Starting Club
You get three starter archetypes. For a first run, choose **"The Rusty Ring"** (balanced finances). The "Champion's Hall" start gives you a strong fighter but only 4 weeks of runway — a trap for new players.

### Step 3 — Economy Priority Order (Weeks 1–4)
Spend in this exact order or you'll hit bankruptcy:
1. **Medical Bay upgrade** (reduces fighter downtime by 30%)
2. **Scout slot #2** (two scouts = consistent roster depth)
3. **Concessions stand** (passive income, scales with attendance)
4. **Skip** the merch shop until Week 8 — margins are too thin early.

### Step 4 — Fighter Management Rules
- Never run a fighter below **70% condition** — injury risk triples under that threshold.
- Rotate a **minimum roster of 5** fighters; the game's fatigue scaling punishes 3-fighter rosters hard after Week 5.
- Prioritize fighters with **high Stamina and Composure** over raw Power in the early league — fights are decided by late-round attrition.

### Step 5 — Reputation Decay Fix
Arena reputation drops **2 points per week** if you don't host an event. Host *something* every single week, even a low-tier amateur night. This is the #1 reason players stall.

## Common Issues, Workarounds, and What You Should Know

**Known Early Access bugs (as of the current build):**
- **Save corruption on alt-tab during autosave** — disable autosave during long sessions or save manually before tabbing out.
- **Fighter stats not updating after training** — exit to main menu and reload; the UI cache doesn't refresh.
- **League ladder soft-lock** — if you're promoted with fewer than 4 healthy fighters, the game won't let you forfeit. Keep a 5th fighter on standby.

**Eslabong vs. comparable managers:**

| Feature | Eslabong | Typical Idle Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Active fight sim | Yes (real-time overlay) | No |
| Economy depth | Medium-high | Low |
| Early Access stability | Moderate (crashes) | High |
| Price | ~$18 | $5–$10 |

**Is it worth it?** At ~$18 in Early Access, yes — *if* you enjoy active management over idle clicking. The fight-sim overlay and reputation system give it real teeth. But expect bugs; this is a 1.0-in-progress build, not a finished product. Wait for a sale if you're bug-averse.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Eslabong worth buying in Early Access?
Yes for management-sim fans — the arena economy and fight-sim layer justify the ~$18 price. Bug-averse players should wait for the first stability patch.

### How do I stop losing reputation in Eslabong?
Host at least one event every in-game week. Reputation decays 2 points weekly if you skip events, regardless of league standing.

### Can I play Eslabong on Steam Deck?
It runs, but UI scaling is rough and the fight overlay needs a mouse. Playable with trackpad, not ideal.

### How many fighters should I keep on my roster?
Five minimum. Fatigue scaling and injury risk make smaller rosters unviable after Week 5.

## Final Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an active club manager with real economy stakes and don't mind Early Access jank. **Wait if** you need polish or play on ultrawide/Steam Deck. Either way, start with "The Rusty Ring," upgrade the Medical Bay first, and host an event every week — those three moves alone prevent 90% of the early-game failures new players post about.
