## RAM Prices Aren't Coming Down — Here's How to Build Smart in 2026

Team Group CEO Chen Ching-wen just told investors something every PC builder needs to hear: "How can prices fall when there's nothing left?" With no downward trend in memory pricing and shortages expected to worsen next year, the era of cheap DDR5 is over. This guide cuts through the panic and gives you a concrete, step-by-step plan to future-proof your build, stretch every gigabyte, and avoid overpaying when "even money can't buy goods."

The short version: **buy the capacity you need now, not later, and buy it in the right configuration.** Below is exactly how to do that.

## Step-by-Step: Future-Proofing Your Build During a Memory Crisis

### Step 1: Lock In Your RAM Before Q1 2026
Chen's warning points to worsening conditions "next year." Historically, memory contract prices move in 3–6 month cycles. If you're planning a build in the next 12 months, **buying RAM now is the single highest-ROI move** — you're effectively hedging against a price ceiling with no floor.

### Step 2: Right-Size Your Capacity (Don't Overbuy, Don't Underbuy)
Overspending on 128GB you'll never use is as bad as buying 16GB you'll outgrow. Use this table:

| Use Case | Recommended Capacity | Sweet Spot Config | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Office / browsing | 16GB | 2×8GB DDR5-5600 | Cheapest reliable tier |
| 1080p/1440p gaming | 32GB | 2×16GB DDR5-6000 CL30 | Future-proof for 3–5 years |
| 4K gaming / streaming | 32–64GB | 2×32GB DDR5-6000 | Headroom for background apps |
| Content creation / VMs | 64GB | 2×32GB DDR5-6000 | Avoids 4-DIMM instability |
| Local AI / LLM work | 64–128GB | 2×48GB or 2×64GB | Model size scales with RAM |

### Step 3: Buy Two Sticks, Not Four
Four-DIMM kits are the first to disappear in shortages and carry a price premium — plus they often force lower speeds (EXPO/XMP instability). **Two high-density sticks beat four low-density ones** on both price and stability in 2026.

### Step 4: Prioritize Speed Over RGB
In a shortage, RGB and "gaming" branding inflate prices 10–20% for zero performance gain. A plain DDR5-6000 CL30 kit outperforms a flashy DDR5-5600 CL40 kit every time. Spend the delta on capacity.

### Step 5: Verify QVL and Enable EXPO/XMP
1. Check your motherboard's **QVL (Qualified Vendor List)** before buying.
2. Install sticks in slots **A2 and B2** (2nd and 4th from CPU).
3. Enter BIOS → enable **EXPO (AMD)** or **XMP (Intel)**.
4. Run MemTest86 for one full pass to confirm stability.

## Common Issues, Alternatives & What You Should Know

- **Don't hoard.** Buying 4 kits to resell is risky — warranty and counterfeit concerns are real, and prices can plateau.
- **Refurb/used DDR5 is a trap** in a shortage: no warranty, and you can't verify binning.
- **Consider DDR4 platforms** (AM4, older Intel) if budget is tight — DDR4 is also rising but remains cheaper per GB for entry builds.
- **Laptop buyers:** soldered RAM means you *must* buy the right config upfront — there's no upgrade path later.
- **Watch for price-gouging** on marketplace listings; buy from authorized retailers only.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Will RAM prices drop in 2026?
According to Team Group's CEO, no — there is "no downward trend in price whatsoever," and conditions are expected to worsen next year due to supply shortages.

### Should I buy RAM now or wait?
If you need a build within 12 months, buy now. Waiting risks higher prices and limited stock as shortages deepen.

### Is 32GB of RAM enough for gaming in 2026?
Yes. 32GB (2×16GB DDR5-6000) is the current sweet spot and will remain sufficient for most games for 3–5 years.

## Final Verdict
**Buy your RAM now, in a 2-stick DDR5-6000 CL30 kit sized to your real workload — 32GB for most gamers, 64GB for creators.** Skip RGB, skip four-DIMM kits, and lock in capacity before the shortage bites harder next year. In a market where "money can't buy goods," the person who bought smart in 2025 wins.
