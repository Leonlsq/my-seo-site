- **Minutes and leadership.** Expect 30-plus minutes per game and a formal spot in the leadership council, a signal Wade is grooming him for an NBA-ready season.
- **No transfer talk tolerated.** Wade explicitly shut down any notion of a mid-season exit or re-entry into the portal, calling Luis a foundational piece for the 2026-27 campaign.

The subtext here is fascinating. Wade is betting big on continuity in an era where rosters turnover like fast fashion. By publicly anointing Luis, he is also sending a message to recruits and the transfer market: LSU develops and rewards loyalty.
