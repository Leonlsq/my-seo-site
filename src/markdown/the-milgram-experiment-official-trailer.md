## The Milgram Experiment vs. Papers, Please vs. Severance: What to Expect

The comparison is the most-searched angle, so here's the honest breakdown:

- **vs. Papers, Please:** Both are bureaucratic sims about complicity. *Papers, Please* is document-checking under time pressure; *The Milgram Experiment* appears focused on the **psychological escalation of obedience** rather than inspection mechanics. Expect fewer stamps, more dialogue choices.
- **vs. Severance:** *Severance* is corporate satire about partitioned consciousness. The game seems to borrow the **institutional dread and sterile authority aesthetic**, not the sci-fi premise.
- **vs. the real Milgram experiment:** The 1961–63 Yale study had subjects administer what they believed were shocks to a "learner" under authority pressure. The game's hook — "why do good people follow evil orders?" — is a direct adaptation of that question into interactive form.
