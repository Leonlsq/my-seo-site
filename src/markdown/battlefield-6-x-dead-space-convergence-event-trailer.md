## Battlefield 6 Convergence Event: How to Beat Marker Incursion Without Dying

The Convergence Event is live in Battlefield 6 and REDSEC, and it is not a standard playlist. Marker Incursion drops 16 players into a PvE survival scenario in Area 51 where Marked Soldiers spawn in escalating waves, and the only win condition is destroying the Marker before the Convergence meter fills. Most squads wipe in the first five minutes because they treat it like a horde shooter and ignore the objective timer. This guide breaks down the exact loadout, spawn priorities, and Marker damage windows you need to clear it consistently on Hard difficulty.

## Step-by-Step Guide: Marker Incursion Loadout and Strategy

### 1. Pick the right class before the match starts
The Pulse Rifle Battle Pick Up is the single most important item on the map, but it is not a substitute for a coordinated team. Build your squad around these four roles:

- **Two Assault players** with the M4A1 or AEK-971 for clearing Marked Soldiers between Pulse Rifle spawns.
- **One Support** running the M249 with extended mags and a repair tool for the generator objectives.
- **One Recon** with a spawn beacon placed near the Marker arena for fast re-entry after death.

Do not stack four Assault players. The Convergence meter accelerates when a teammate is downed and not revived within 20 seconds, so revive speed from Support matters more than raw DPS.

### 2. Locate the Pulse Rifle Battle Pick Up immediately
The Pulse Rifle spawns at three fixed locations in Area 51: the hangar control room, the underground tram platform, and the rooftop antenna array. It deals bonus damage to Marked Soldier weak points (the glowing blue nodes on the chest) and is the only weapon that staggers a charging Brute variant. Grab it within the first 90 seconds. If a teammate already has it, do not contest the spawn — rotate to the next location and cover them.

### 3. Destroy the Marker in three damage windows
The Marker cannot be damaged continuously. It only takes damage during the **Convergence Surge**, a 30-second window that triggers after you destroy a Necromorph nest. Here is the cycle:

| Phase | Duration | Objective |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Nest Hunt | 3–4 min | Destroy 3 Necromorph nests to trigger Surge |
| Convergence Surge | 30 sec | Focus all fire on the Marker's four glowing conduits |
| Recovery | 60 sec | Revive, resupply, reposition before next wave |

You need roughly **four Surge windows** to destroy the Marker on Hard. Miss a window and the Convergence meter jumps 15%.

### 4. Manage the Convergence meter
The meter fills from three sources: time elapsed, downed teammates, and un-destroyed nests. Prioritize nests over kills — killing Marked Soldiers does not slow the meter, but destroying a nest buys you 45 seconds and triggers the Surge. If the meter hits 100%, the run ends in a scripted wipe regardless of how many players are alive.

## Common Issues and What You Should Know

- **Pulse Rifle not spawning?** It spawns on a 4-minute respawn timer. If another squad member grabbed it and died, the pickup returns to its original location — check the hangar first.
- **Brute enemies one-shotting you?** Brutes telegraph a charge with a red glow on their shoulders. Dodge laterally, not backward — their charge hitbox extends behind them.
- **Convergence meter filling too fast?** You are likely ignoring nests. Each un-destroyed nest adds a passive 0.5% per second to the meter.
- **Rewards:** Completing Marker Incursion on Hard unlocks the Dead Space-themed "Convergence Survivor" weapon charm and a Pulse Rifle blueprint. Normal difficulty only grants XP, so the cosmetic grind is Hard-only.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How many players are in Battlefield 6 Marker Incursion?
Marker Incursion is a 16-player PvE mode. You can queue solo, but matchmaking fills your squad to four, and the difficulty scales with squad size.

### How long does the Convergence Event last?
The limited-time event runs through the current REDSEC season. Check the in-game event tab for the exact end date, as Battlefield 6 events typically run 3–4 weeks.

### Can you destroy the Marker without the Pulse Rifle?
Yes, but it is significantly slower. Standard weapons deal 40% reduced damage to Marker conduits. The Pulse Rifle is effectively mandatory for Hard clears.

## Final Verdict

The Convergence Event is worth playing, but only if you treat it as an objective mode rather than a kill farm. Grab the Pulse Rifle early, destroy nests on cooldown, and save your Surge windows for coordinated Marker damage. If you are chasing the cosmetic rewards, go straight to Hard — Normal difficulty does not drop the Convergence Survivor charm, and the extra 10 minutes per run is not worth the wasted time.
