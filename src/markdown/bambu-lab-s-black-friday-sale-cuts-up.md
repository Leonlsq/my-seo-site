## Setup Tips, Hidden Settings, and Common Issues

Buying is the easy part. Here's how to get the H2C dialed in and avoid the most common first-week frustrations.

1. **Run full calibration before your first real print.** Auto bed leveling, input shaping, and flow calibration take ~15–20 minutes but prevent hours of failed prints. Don't skip the vibration compensation step — CoreXY machines are sensitive to it.
2. **Preheat the chamber for engineering materials.** For ABS/ASA, set chamber temp to 45–50°C and let it stabilize for 10 minutes before printing. Cold-chamber ABS is the #1 cause of warping and layer splitting.
3. **Tune the AMS for brittle filament.** TPU and old PLA snap in the AMS feed path. Keep filament dry (a dryer box is non-negotiable), and avoid TPU in the AMS unless you use the recommended hardness (95A+).
4. **Enable LAN-only mode if cloud sync is flaky.** Many users report cloud connection drops; switching to LAN mode with the local IP stabilizes remote monitoring.
5. **Update firmware before the sale ends.** New units sometimes ship with older firmware missing the latest calibration routines — update first, calibrate second.

### Common first-week issues and fixes
- **First-layer adhesion failures:** Clean the textured PEI plate with warm water and dish soap (not IPA alone — IPA leaves residue that hurts adhesion). Re-run bed leveling.
- **Stringing on PETG:** Drop nozzle temp 5–10°C, increase retraction, and dry the filament — PETG absorbs moisture fast.
- **AMS "filament runout" false alarms:** Check the PTFE tube seating at both ends; a partially inserted tube triggers phantom runout errors.
- **Noisy prints:** Tighten the belt tensioners per the manual; CoreXY belts loosen slightly during shipping.

### Alternatives worth comparing before you buy
- **Bambu Lab P1S/X1C:** Cheaper, no heated chamber — better for PLA/PETG-only users.
- **Creality K2 Plus:** Larger volume, competing price, but a less polished ecosystem and weaker multi-color integration.
- **Prusa Core One:** Excellent reliability and support, but slower and pricier at list — the H2C discount widens the gap.
- **Voron 2.4 (DIY):** Cheapest per-spec if you build it, but you trade warranty and plug-and-play for tinkering time.
