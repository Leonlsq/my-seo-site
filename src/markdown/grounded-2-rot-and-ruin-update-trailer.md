## Grounded 2 Rot and Ruin: Everything You Need to Unlock Bismuth Tier Fast

The **Rot and Ruin update** for Grounded 2 just dropped, and it fundamentally changes how you progress through the late game. If you watched the trailer and saw the Centipede boss, the Potato Beetle Buggy, and the mysterious Yoking Press but had no idea where to start, this guide is for you. The headline takeaway: **Bismuth Tier is the new endgame ceiling, and the Yoking Press is the only way to reach it** — so prioritize unlocking Brookhollow Park and the Woodpile before you waste resources upgrading anything else.

This is a practical, step-by-step walkthrough of the new progression loop, the exact upgrade path, and how to beat the community-requested Centipede boss without losing your mind (or your legs).

## Step-by-Step Guide to the Rot and Ruin Progression Loop

The update introduces a clear chain: explore → gather new resources → build the Yoking Press → upgrade to Bismuth Tier → tackle the Centipede. Here's how to execute it efficiently.

### Step 1: Unlock Brookhollow Park and the Woodpile
The Woodpile is the new adventure area inside **Brookhollow Park**. It's crawling with returning Termites and the new **O.G.R.R. variants**. Before you go in, craft Tier 2 armor and bring a stack of healing items — the O.G.R.R. variants hit harder than the base game's robots and punish players who rush in.

### Step 2: Farm Bismuth
Bismuth is the new upgrade material and the backbone of this entire update. You'll find it while exploring the Woodpile and surrounding Brookhollow Park zones. Treat it like the old Quartzite grind: **don't spend a single unit on cosmetic stuff until your Yoking Press is built.** Bismuth is the bottleneck for every Tier upgrade.

### Step 3: Build the Yoking Press
The **Yoking Press** is a new crafting station that converts raw Bismuth into higher-tier gear upgrades. This is the single most important structure in the update. Build it at your main base, not a forward camp — you'll be returning to it constantly.

### Step 4: Upgrade Gear to Higher Tiers
Once the Yoking Press is running, feed it Bismuth to push your weapons and armor into the new tier bracket. Prioritize your **weapon first**, then chest armor. A higher-tier weapon dramatically shortens Centipede fight times, which saves resources in the long run.

### Step 5: Craft the Potato Beetle Buggy
The **Potato Beetle Buggy** is your new mount for traversing Brookhollow Park quickly. It's not just a convenience item — faster travel means faster Bismuth farming runs, which compounds into faster upgrades. Craft it as soon as your weapon is upgraded.

### Step 6: Challenge the Centipede
The **Centipede** is the marquee boss: a community-requested fight with plenty of legs and no intention of giving you a leg up. Bring your Bismuth-tier weapon, the Buggy for the approach, and expect a multi-phase fight. Don't attempt it in Tier 2 gear.

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

### The Bismuth Bottleneck
The most common complaint new players will hit: **Bismuth is scarce early.** Don't spread upgrades across every weapon — commit to one loadout. Spreading Bismuth thin is the #1 progression mistake in Rot and Ruin.

### Don't Sleep on the Splotcher
The **Splotcher** gives your base a fresh coat and is easy to overlook as "cosmetic." Build it early for the base customization benefits, but don't let it distract from the Yoking Press grind.

### Collectibles and SCA.B Themes
The update adds **new SCA.B Themes** and **collectible miniatures** scattered across Brookhollow Park. These are optional but worth grabbing on your Bismuth runs — they don't cost resources and add long-term completion value.
