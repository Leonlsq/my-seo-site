## How to Report AI CSAM on Discord: A Safe, Step-by-Step Guide

The arrest of a New Jersey deputy attorney general—known online as "thegentlemanpervert"—after a Discord user flagged AI-generated child sexual abuse material (CSAM) proves one thing: a single, correctly filed report can trigger a real criminal investigation. This guide shows you exactly how to report AI CSAM on Discord, preserve evidence legally, and escalate to the right authorities—without exposing yourself to legal risk or re-victimization. The direct takeaway: **do not download, re-share, or "test" the material. Screenshot only what's necessary, report in-app, then escalate to NCMEC and local law enforcement.**

## Step-by-Step: Reporting AI CSAM on Discord Safely

Follow these steps in order. Timing matters—platform reports and law enforcement reports reinforce each other.

1. **Do not save or forward the file.** Possession of CSAM—even AI-generated—can be illegal in many jurisdictions. In the U.S., 18 U.S.C. § 2252 and § 1466A cover obscene visual depictions of minors, including computer-generated images.
2. **Capture minimal evidence.** Take a screenshot showing the username, server name, channel, timestamp, and message context. Do not zoom into or crop the abusive image itself—capture enough to identify the account and message ID.
3. **Copy the Message ID.** Enable Discord Developer Mode (User Settings → Advanced → Developer Mode), then right-click the message → **Copy Message ID**. This is the single most useful identifier for Discord's Trust & Safety team.
4. **Report in-app.** Right-click the message → **Report Message** → select the category (sexual content involving minors). Discord's in-app reporting routes directly to its Trust & Safety queue.
5. **File a report with NCMEC's CyberTipline** at report.cybertip.org. This is the U.S. clearinghouse that forwards reports to law enforcement, including the FBI and ICAC task forces. Include the Discord Message ID, server name, username, and timestamps.
6. **Contact local law enforcement or the FBI IC3** (ic3.gov) if you have identifying details about the user. In the New Jersey case, the flag from a Discord user was reportedly the trigger for the investigation.
7. **Do not confront the user.** Confrontation tips off the suspect and can lead to evidence deletion.

## Common Issues, Legal Risks, and What You Should Know

**"Is AI-generated CSAM actually illegal?"** In the U.S., yes in many cases. The **PROTECT Act of 2003** criminalized computer-generated child pornography, and courts have upheld convictions under 18 U.S.C. § 1466A. Several states have also passed explicit AI-CSAM statutes. The New Jersey case shows prosecutors are willing to charge it.

**"What if I accidentally viewed it?"** Accidental viewing is generally not a crime, but downloading or saving it can be. Delete nothing from your device if law enforcement may need it—but do not save new copies.

**"Discord didn't act—what now?"** Escalate to NCMEC (report.cybertip.org) and the FBI IC3. NCMEC reports carry legal weight and are routed to ICAC task forces.

**"Can I report anonymously?"** Yes. NCMEC's CyberTipline accepts anonymous reports, though providing contact info helps investigators follow up.

**"What about other platforms?"** The same workflow applies to Reddit, X, and Telegram—use in-app reporting first, then NCMEC. Telegram is notoriously slow to act, so escalate to law enforcement faster.

**"How fast does Discord respond?"** Discord's Trust & Safety team typically reviews priority CSAM reports within hours to days. In high-severity cases, law enforcement subpoenas can accelerate account preservation.

### Red Flags That Warrant Escalation

- The user is soliciting or trading material (indicates a network).
- The account appears to hold a position of trust (teacher, official, law enforcement)—as in the New Jersey deputy AG case.
- The material depicts identifiable real children (not just AI-generated).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is AI-generated CSAM illegal in the US?
Yes, in many circumstances. The PROTECT Act of 2003 and 18 U.S.C. § 1466A criminalize obscene computer-generated depictions of minors, and multiple states have added explicit AI-CSAM laws. Prosecutors have charged cases involving AI-generated material.

### How do I report a Discord user for AI CSAM anonymously?
Use Discord's in-app **Report Message** feature, then file an anonymous report at NCMEC's CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org). You can omit personal details, though including contact info helps investigators follow up.

### What is the Discord Message ID and why does it matter?
The Message ID is a unique numeric identifier for each message. Enabling Developer Mode lets you copy it via right-click. It lets Discord's Trust & Safety team locate the exact message without you re-sharing the abusive content.

### Can I get in trouble for reporting CSAM?
No—good-faith reports are legally protected in the U.S. under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and related safe-harbor provisions. The risk comes from downloading, saving, or re-sharing the material, not from reporting it.

### What happened in the "thegentlemanpervert" New Jersey case?
A Discord user flagged AI-generated CSAM, which reportedly triggered an investigation leading to the arrest of a New Jersey deputy attorney general who used the handle "thegentlemanpervert." The case illustrates how a single platform report can escalate to criminal charges.
