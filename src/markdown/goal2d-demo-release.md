## GOAL2D Demo: Best Settings & Setup Guide

The demo doesn't hold your hand, so here's how to configure it for a competitive edge from your first kickoff.

### Step 1: Verify Your PC Can Run It
GOAL2D is a lightweight 2D title, so almost any modern machine handles it. Still, check these baseline expectations before downloading:

| Component | Minimum (Expected) | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------|
| OS | Windows 10 64-bit | Windows 11 64-bit |
| GPU | Integrated graphics | Any dedicated GPU |
| Storage | Under 1 GB | SSD for faster loads |
| Input | Keyboard or controller | Controller (recommended) |

*Note: Exact specs aren't finalized in the demo build—confirm on the Steam store page before full release.*

### Step 2: Pick Your Input Method
- **Controller:** Best for precise directional dribbling and quick shot angles. The analog stick gives you finer control over ball placement than eight-direction keys.
- **Keyboard:** Viable, but you'll lose diagonal finesse. If you're keyboard-only, rebind shoot and pass to keys your fingers can reach simultaneously.

### Step 3: Learn the Three-Minute Match Rhythm
Every match is three minutes. That changes strategy completely:
1. **First 30 seconds:** Feel out your opponent's pressure style.
2. **Middle two minutes:** Exploit gaps—spam passing to drag defenders out of position.
3. **Final 30 seconds:** If you're ahead, hold possession. If behind, push high and shoot on sight.

### Step 4: Master Ball Handling First, Shooting Second
The game's tagline isn't marketing fluff—ball control wins matches. Prioritize:
- **Close dribbling** over long passes in the demo's casual lobbies.
- **Quick one-touch passes** to break pressing.
- **Angled shots** rather than power shots; the top-down view rewards placement.

If you're expecting progression or ranked grind in the demo, you'll be disappointed—it's a mechanics sandbox, not a content showcase.

### Potential Launch Issues to Watch
- **Matchmaking in casual lobbies:** With demo populations fluctuating, expect occasional waits or mismatched skill levels.
- **Controller detection:** If your pad isn't recognized, unplug and reconnect before launching, and verify Steam Input is enabled.
- **Performance stutter:** Rare for 2D titles, but if you see frame drops, cap your framerate to your monitor's refresh rate.

### How It Compares to Alternatives
- **vs. Rocket League (2D mods):** GOAL2D is simpler and faster to pick up, but far less mechanically deep.
- **vs. classic Sensible Soccer-style games:** GOAL2D leans arcade-fast rather than simulation.
- **vs. full football sims (FIFA/FC, eFootball):** Not a competitor—this is a pick-up-and-play skill game, not a management or simulation experience.
