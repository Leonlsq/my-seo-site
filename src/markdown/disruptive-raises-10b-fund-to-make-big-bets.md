## What Disruptive's $10B Fund Actually Means for Founders and AI Users

Disruptive has raised a $10 billion fund with a mandate to make big bets on at least 10 companies — and if you're a founder, investor, or AI power user, this changes the calculus more than the headlines suggest. The short version: this is a concentrated, high-conviction fund, not a spray-and-pray index. Based on its track record with chip startup Groq and AI voice-generation startup ElevenLabs, expect Disruptive to target **infrastructure and applied-AI companies with defensible technical moats**, not consumer apps. Below is a practical breakdown of what that means, how to position yourself, and what to watch.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Position Yourself for a Disruptive-Style Mega-Fund

If you're a founder hoping to land on a shortlist like this — or an operator deciding which AI vendors to bet on — here's the actionable playbook.

### For Founders: The 6-Step Positioning Checklist

1. **Lead with infrastructure, not wrappers.** Disruptive's known bets (Groq = inference chips, ElevenLabs = voice models) are deep-tech. If your product is a thin GPT wrapper, reframe around proprietary data, latency, or hardware.
2. **Quantify your compute story.** Mega-funds want to know your cost-per-inference and scaling ceiling. Prepare a one-page unit-economics sheet.
3. **Show enterprise traction, not just users.** Concentrated funds need proof of revenue durability. Aim for 3+ referenceable enterprise logos.
4. **Build a 10x technical moat narrative.** Explain what breaks if a competitor copies you — patents, model weights, supply-chain deals.
5. **Prepare for a concentrated check.** A $10B fund writing big bets means fewer, larger rounds. Be ready to justify a $100M+ raise with a clear deployment plan.
6. **Map the 10-company thesis.** Show how you fit a specific slot (chips, voice, agents, data) rather than "AI broadly."

## Common Issues, Alternatives, and What You Should Know

**Concentration risk is real.** A fund that bets on "at least 10 companies" with $10B means average checks could be enormous — potentially $500M–$1B per company. That's great for the winners but means portfolio companies face intense pressure to justify valuation. If you're a customer, don't assume a vendor is safe just because it raised big — ask about burn and runway.

**Alternatives to watch.** If you're evaluating AI infrastructure, compare Disruptive-backed players against diversified incumbents:

- **Inference chips:** Groq vs. Nvidia vs. Cerebras — benchmark latency *and* availability, not just TOPS.
- **Voice AI:** ElevenLabs vs. OpenAI voice vs. open-source (Coqui, Piper) — test on your actual accent/language mix.
- **Capital sources:** Mega-funds (concentrated) vs. corporate VCs (strategic but slower) vs. revenue-based financing (no dilution).

**Hidden consideration:** Mega-funds often push portfolio companies toward aggressive growth, which can mean pricing changes or pivot risk. Build vendor-switching clauses into contracts.
